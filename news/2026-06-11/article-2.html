DAP Finalises GE16 Candidate List, Announcement Expected Within Days

DAP secretary-general Anthony Loke Siew Fook has confirmed that the party's candidate list for the 16th General Election (GE16) is in its final stages of preparation, with a formal announcement expected within days. The revelation comes as Malaysia's political temperature rises amid widespread speculation that GE16 could be called sooner than its constitutionally mandated deadline of February 2028.

Speaking to reporters on the sidelines of a party event, Loke said the list has been carefully deliberated to ensure a strong lineup of candidates across all constituencies that DAP is expected to contest. He acknowledged that there will be some changes from the previous election cycle, reflecting both leadership development within the party and adjustments to the political landscape following recent realignments.

The disclosure signals that DAP — one of the anchor parties within the Pakatan Harapan (PH) component of the unity government — is operationally ready for an early election. Party insiders say the internal vetting process has been ongoing for months, and the finalisation of the list marks the last major preparatory step before a formal submission to the Elections Commission when a writ is issued.

DAP's announcement of readiness follows similar signals from coalition partners MCA and Gerakan, both of which have been quietly conducting voter outreach in key constituencies. The synchronised preparation across parties suggests that party leaders expect Prime Minister Anwar Ibrahim to announce an early dissolution of parliament in the coming weeks, though no official announcement has been made.

Political analysts caution that the publication of candidate lists before an election is called carries risks as well as benefits — it allows rivals to prepare targeted campaigns, but also reinforces a party's image of transparency and organizational strength with voters who are increasingly sceptical of backroom dealings.
