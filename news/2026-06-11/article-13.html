Malaysia Showcases Visit Malaysia 2026 at Thailand Travel Mart Plus in Pattaya

Malaysia is actively participating in Thailand Travel Mart Plus 2026 (TTM+ 2026), running from June 10 to 12 in Pattaya, Thailand, alongside nearly 500 exhibitors from more than 50 countries. The event is one of Southeast Asia's premier travel trade gatherings, bringing together tourism boards, hospitality operators, airlines, and international buyers in a structured B2B environment that facilitates tour-packaging and booking agreements.

Tourism Malaysia's delegation at TTM+ 2026 is focused on promoting the country's Visit Malaysia Year 2026 campaign to buyers from key target markets including Europe, the Middle East, South Asia, Northeast Asia, and Oceania. Representatives are highlighting Malaysia's diverse offerings — from Kuala Lumpur's urban experiences to Borneo's eco-tourism and the cultural richness of Penang and Melaka — to encourage multi-day itineraries and higher per-capita spending.

The timing of TTM+ 2026 is strategically important for Malaysia, as it coincides with peak holiday-planning activity for the Northern Hemisphere's summer travel season and falls midway through Visit Malaysia Year, allowing the delegation to share first-half arrivals progress and build momentum heading into the year's peak travel quarter. Officials have reported strong buyer interest, particularly from markets where Malaysia has invested heavily in targeted digital advertising campaigns.

ASEAN cooperation on tourism promotion has been a key theme at TTM+ 2026, with participating Southeast Asian tourism boards coordinating on multi-destination itinerary packages that combine Malaysia with Thailand, Singapore, and Vietnam in a single trip. This approach is seen as particularly appealing to long-haul travellers from Europe and the Americas who seek maximum regional coverage during limited vacation time.

Malaysia's participation in TTM+ 2026 also serves a bilateral trade purpose, strengthening links with Thailand's established travel distribution network. Thailand remains one of Malaysia's major tourism trade partners, with cross-border visitor flows running in both directions. Enhanced cooperation on travel facilitation — including streamlined border processes at major land crossings — is expected to boost arrivals on both sides ahead of the year-end holiday season.
