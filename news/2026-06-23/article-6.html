Amanah Unveils Fresh Faces for Johor State Election

The Star · 23 June 2026 · Politics

Parti Amanah Negara (Amanah) has released its list of candidates for the Johor state election on July 11, with the party opting for a strategy that heavily favours fresh and younger faces over incumbents and established politicians. The majority of candidates on the list are first-time state election contestants, a deliberate choice by the party's leadership to inject new energy and broaden the party's appeal among younger voters and communities that may not have previously engaged with Amanah's brand of progressive, moderate Islamic politics. The announcement was met with a mixture of anticipation and cautious optimism within Pakatan Harapan circles.

Amanah's decision to field primarily new candidates reflects the competitive reality of contesting in Johor, where the party has historically struggled to gain traction against the dominant UMNO and PAS machines. Party leaders argued that recycling familiar names would do little to shift voter perceptions, and that new candidates with strong community ties and professional backgrounds would be better positioned to win over undecided voters in mixed and swing seats. Several of the new candidates are understood to be professionals, academics, and community organisers with proven track records in their respective constituencies.

Johor remains one of Malaysia's most hotly contested states at the state level, given its size, economic importance, and diverse demographic mix. As the home state of UMNO's founding, it holds enormous symbolic value for Barisan Nasional, which swept back to power in the 2022 Johor state election with a commanding majority. Pakatan Harapan, led by Amanah and DAP within the PH component, is aiming to significantly improve on that performance by capitalising on any dissatisfaction with the state government's management of development, cost of living, and governance issues.

Competition in the Johor election will be particularly intense given the presence of multiple contesting forces. In addition to the PH-BN unity government ticket and the Perikatan Nasional opposition coalition, Parti Bersama Malaysia has also announced its entry into 15 seats. This multi-cornered contest scenario in some constituencies could either dilute the opposition vote to BN's advantage or produce surprise upsets where a well-organised new party candidate draws from a previously untapped voter base. Amanah's strategists are understood to be carefully tracking seat dynamics to determine where their candidates are best placed to maximise returns.

Amanah's national leadership, including party president Mohamad Sabu, has pledged active campaigning support for the Johor candidates in the final weeks before polling day. The party's campaign messaging is expected to focus on economic delivery, affordable housing, and the unity government's track record since GE15, while differentiating Amanah from both the hardline religious politics of PAS and the old-guard conservatism of UMNO. With nomination day approaching, all eyes in Malaysian politics are turning to Johor as the first major electoral test of 2026.
