Theta Edge Chairman Tan Sri Abd Rahman Mamat Resigns

The Edge Malaysia · 23 June 2026 · Business

Tan Sri Abd Rahman Mamat has stepped down as non-executive chairman of Theta Edge Berhad, the Bursa Malaysia-listed ICT services provider trading under the stock code 9075, with his resignation taking effect on 22 June 2026. The development was disclosed through an official filing to Bursa Malaysia, in accordance with the exchange's continuous disclosure requirements. No reasons were cited in the filing for his departure, a common practice in Malaysian boardroom announcements of this nature.

Tan Sri Abd Rahman had served as chairman of Theta Edge during a significant period in the company's evolution. The company, which provides a broad portfolio of services spanning IT solutions, telecommunications engineering, civil infrastructure works, and green energy consulting, has positioned itself as a technology-driven enterprise serving both government and corporate clients. His tenure at the helm of the board coincided with growing national emphasis on digital transformation and homegrown ICT capabilities, areas in which Theta Edge has sought to carve out a niche presence.

The resignation comes at a particularly active juncture for Malaysia's ICT sector. The country has attracted tens of billions of ringgit in data centre and artificial intelligence infrastructure investments over the past several years, with global hyperscalers and regional technology firms competing to establish a foothold in what has become one of Southeast Asia's premier digital investment destinations. For companies like Theta Edge, which operate in the services layer of the digital economy, the challenge lies in capitalising on this investment surge while adapting to rapid shifts in technology demand — including the growing integration of AI into enterprise IT systems.

Investors and market watchers will be paying close attention to Theta Edge's next steps in identifying a successor to lead its board. The choice of a new chairman will be seen as a signal of the company's strategic direction at a time when ICT firms in Malaysia face both significant opportunity and heightened competition. The board is expected to announce a successor in due course, and analysts note that strong governance and a clear articulation of the company's positioning in the AI-enabled services market will be critical to maintaining investor confidence during the transition period.
