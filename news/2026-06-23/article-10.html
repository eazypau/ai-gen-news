Bursa Malaysia Closes Lower as KLCI Sheds 11.19 Points

Malay Mail · 23 June 2026 · Business

Bursa Malaysia ended Monday's trading session in negative territory, with the benchmark FBM KLCI shedding 11.19 points, or 0.65%, to close at 1,700.84. The broad sell-off was driven by cautious investor sentiment amid ongoing global uncertainties, particularly surrounding the state of US-Iran peace negotiations and their potential impact on energy prices and international trade flows.

Blue-chip counters bore the brunt of the day's losses, with banking, plantation, and telecommunications stocks among the key drag. Key index heavyweights such as Maybank, CIMB, and Tenaga Nasional all closed lower, reflecting the risk-off mood that pervaded regional markets. The broader market mirrored the KLCI's decline, with more losers than gainers on the exchange across all sectors.

Analysts noted that the market remains in a consolidation phase following a period of strong gains in the first half of 2026, fuelled by the ringgit's appreciation and improved corporate earnings expectations. The FBM KLCI had earlier in the year traded above the 1,800 level, buoyed by optimism over Malaysia's data centre boom and positive GDP revisions from the IMF. The pullback to the 1,700 range reflects profit-taking and a recalibration of valuations.

Trading volume on the exchange was moderate at approximately 3.2 billion shares worth RM2.1 billion. The healthcare and consumer sectors showed relative resilience compared to industrials and financials. Market watchers are keeping a close eye on Wall Street cues and any developments on the US tariff front, which continues to pose a downside risk to export-oriented Malaysian manufacturers.

Looking ahead, analysts from several investment banks remain cautiously optimistic about the second half of 2026, citing continued domestic demand and infrastructure spending as key supports for corporate earnings growth. The LRT3 opening later this week, along with continued data centre construction activity, is expected to provide a modest boost to sentiment in the coming sessions.
