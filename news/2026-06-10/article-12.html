Unifi TV and RTM to Stream All 104 FIFA World Cup 2026 Matches Live

Sports Free Malaysia Today 10 June 2026

With the FIFA World Cup 2026 set to kick off on June 12 across host cities in the United States, Mexico, and Canada, Malaysian football fans have been given comprehensive coverage options through Telekom Malaysia's Unifi TV platform and the national broadcaster RTM. Communications Minister Fahmi Fadzil confirmed that all 104 matches of the expanded 48-team tournament will be broadcast live.

Unifi TV subscribers can access the full tournament via a special season pass priced at RM60, while existing subscribers are eligible for a discounted rate of RM50 covering matches from June 11 through to the final on July 19. The platform will offer multiple simultaneous streams for days when matches are played concurrently, ensuring no fan misses their key fixture.

RTMKlik, the national broadcaster's streaming arm, is expected to livestream all 104 World Cup matches at no additional cost to viewers. Crucially, the RTMKlik service is accessible regardless of the user's internet service provider, meaning fans on Maxis, Celcom, Digi, TIME, or any other network can tune in without a Unifi subscription.

This year's World Cup marks a historic departure from previous editions, featuring 48 national teams competing across 16 groups in the round-robin phase. The expanded format means more football drama across more cities, with matches spread across time zones that are generally accessible to Malaysian viewers watching in the evening and early morning.

Malaysian football fans have been particularly vocal about the change in broadcaster from Astro, which held the rights for previous tournaments. While some Astro-exclusive subscribers are adjusting to new platforms, the wider availability of RTMKlik has been broadly welcomed as a move toward democratising sports broadcasting in Malaysia.
