US Closes AI Chip Loophole That Benefited Malaysian Tech Distributors

Technology The Star 10 June 2026

The United States government has enacted new export control regulations that effectively close a loophole which had allowed Chinese AI and technology firms to procure advanced semiconductors through third-party markets, including Malaysia. The move significantly tightens the global semiconductor supply chain and ends a grey-market pathway that had generated considerable business for Southeast Asian distributors.

Malaysia had emerged as one of several transit points through which advanced chips—particularly high-end graphics processing units used in AI training systems—were routed to Chinese end-users, circumventing earlier US restrictions that applied only to direct exports to China. Local distributors and trading companies that facilitated these transactions face an abrupt change in the regulatory environment.

The US Department of Commerce's Bureau of Industry and Security stated that the updated Foreign Direct Product Rules now encompass a broader category of chip types and imposes stricter end-user verification requirements on all parties in the supply chain, regardless of their country of incorporation. Malaysian distributors are required to perform enhanced due diligence on buyers effective immediately.

Malaysia's Ministry of Investment, Trade, and Industry acknowledged the regulatory change and said it would work with industry players to ensure compliance with international trade law. Officials stressed that Malaysia does not condone the circumvention of legitimate export control regimes and will cooperate with allied trading partners to uphold global supply chain integrity.

Technology sector analysts note that the immediate impact is expected to be a contraction in the re-export segment of Malaysia's semiconductor distribution business. Longer term, the development underscores the need for Malaysia to focus on genuine value-added semiconductor activities—design, testing, and packaging—rather than depending on chip transit trade as a revenue source.
