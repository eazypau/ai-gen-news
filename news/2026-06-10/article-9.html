New MyKad with 53 Security Features Set to Launch This Month

Technology Malay Mail 10 June 2026

The National Registration Department (JPN) is set to roll out a significantly upgraded MyKad national identity card in June 2026, featuring 53 advanced security elements designed to combat counterfeiting and identity fraud. The new card represents the most comprehensive redesign of the MyKad since its introduction in 2001.

Security enhancements in the new card include laser engraving, ultraviolet (UV) reactive elements visible only under specialised lighting, intricate guilloche patterns, microtext that is invisible to the naked eye, and QR codes specifically designed for law enforcement use. The embedded chip has also been upgraded with next-generation encryption technology to safeguard the cardholder's personal data.

One notable change from earlier announcements is the removal of the Touch 'n Go contactless payment function from the new MyKad. Officials explained that this decision was taken following a review of the functionality's utility and security implications in the modern payments landscape, where standalone e-wallet apps have largely superseded card-based transit payments.

Alongside the MyKad, updated versions of MyPoCA (for police personnel) and MyTentera (for military personnel) will also be issued. The JPN has assured existing cardholders that their current MyKad will remain valid until its expiry date, and that the new card will be issued during routine renewal cycles.

The launch is part of a broader government push to digitise and modernise Malaysia's civil identity infrastructure. Citizens are reminded to verify their personal details with JPN prior to renewal and to be vigilant against scammers who may attempt to exploit the high-profile launch to collect personal information through phishing schemes.
