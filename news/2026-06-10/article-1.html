PAS Stays in Perikatan Nasional Despite Bersatu Split

Politics Free Malaysia Today 10 June 2026

PAS leadership has remained tight-lipped over the party's position within Perikatan Nasional (PN) following the coalition's announcement to end political cooperation with Bersatu. PAS information chief Ahmad Fadhli Shaari indicated that the party is, for now, still part of the coalition, declining to elaborate further on internal discussions.

The announcement by PN to distance itself from Bersatu—whose president Tan Sri Muhyiddin Yassin is embroiled in multiple corruption cases—has sent shockwaves through the opposition bloc. Political analysts note that PAS's continued membership in PN without Bersatu fundamentally changes the coalition's dynamics and bargaining power in Parliament.

UMNO President Datuk Seri Ahmad Zahid Hamidi separately ruled out any possibility of reviving the Muafakat Nasional pact, which had previously brought UMNO and PAS together. His statement closes what some had hoped would be an alternative alignment route for PAS should PN fracture further.

Opposition leaders have called on PAS to clarify its stance more definitively, warning that ambiguity could hurt the party's credibility ahead of the next general election. PAS grassroots members are reportedly divided, with some pushing for greater independence from PN while others advocate staying the course.

Political observers are watching closely to see whether PAS will leverage this moment to renegotiate its role and seat allocations within PN, or whether the party will maintain the status quo in a display of coalition solidarity. The coming weeks are expected to be pivotal in shaping Malaysia's opposition landscape.
