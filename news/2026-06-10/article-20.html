Malaysia Rolls Out Hospital Information System to 16 Public Hospitals

Health Healthcare IT News 10 June 2026

Malaysia's Ministry of Health has begun the phased expansion of the Total Hospital Information System (THIS)—an integrated digital platform for managing clinical and administrative hospital operations—to an additional 16 public hospitals across the country. The rollout is a critical component of the government's health digitalisation agenda and represents a significant investment in modernising the public healthcare infrastructure.

THIS consolidates patient records, appointment scheduling, billing, pharmacy dispensing, laboratory results, and clinical decision support tools into a single interconnected system, reducing paperwork and the risk of medical errors associated with fragmented record-keeping. The system has already been piloted in several flagship hospitals, with positive feedback from both healthcare workers and patients.

Simultaneously, a cloud-based Clinic Management System (CMS) is being deployed to approximately 2,489 primary care clinics operated under the Ministry of Health's network. The CMS will enable seamless referrals between clinics and hospitals, improve continuity of care for patients with chronic conditions, and allow the ministry to collect real-time health data for policy planning purposes.

The Dental Information System will also be extended to 157 dental clinics nationwide, standardising records management for oral health services and supporting a planned national oral health screening initiative targeting schoolchildren. The dental system will be interoperable with the broader national health information ecosystem.

Technology vendors working on the deployments include a mix of international IT firms and Malaysian companies, consistent with the government's policy of growing local technology capability. Training programmes for clinical and administrative staff are being conducted concurrently with system installations to ensure smooth transitions. The ministry has set a target of completing the full rollout phase by the end of 2026.
