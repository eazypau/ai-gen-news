EXO Returns to Kuala Lumpur for First Concert in Six Years

Entertainment Hype Malaysia 10 June 2026

Legendary K-pop group EXO is returning to Kuala Lumpur this June for their first Malaysian performance in six years, sending their devoted EXO-L fanbase into a frenzy of anticipation. The announcement of the concert was met with an immediate surge in ticket demand, with many categories selling out within hours of going on sale.

EXO's return follows years of individual member activities, solo projects, and mandatory military service obligations that had kept the group from performing as a unit on international stages. The reunion concert is one of the most awaited K-pop events in Malaysia in recent memory, given the group's enormous influence on the Malaysian Hallyu wave throughout the 2010s and beyond.

The Kuala Lumpur show, to be held at the Axiata Arena, is part of EXO's broader Asian comeback tour. The group is expected to perform fan-favourite hits spanning their entire discography, from early classics such as "Wolf" and "Growl" to more recent solo-turned-group compositions. A special production with elaborate stage design has been teased by the concert promoter.

Local K-pop merchandise vendors and fan cafes around Kuala Lumpur have reported unprecedented pre-event business, with fans flocking to purchase EXO-themed items and organising fan gatherings leading up to the concert. Tourism officials noted that the event is expected to draw fans from neighbouring countries including Singapore, Indonesia, and Thailand.

Concert promoter Live Nation Malaysia confirmed that security and crowd management arrangements have been significantly upgraded following lessons from previous large-scale events. Fans are advised to arrive early, use public transportation, and follow all safety and entry protocols to ensure a smooth and memorable evening.
