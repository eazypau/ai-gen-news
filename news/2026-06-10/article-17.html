June Performing Arts: STEP Theatre Festival 2026 Takes Centre Stage

Entertainment Free Malaysia Today 10 June 2026

The STEP Theatre Festival 2026, hosted at Universiti Sains Malaysia's Dewan Sri Peria in Penang, is bringing three compelling productions to audiences over three consecutive weekends from June 5 to 21. The festival—a collaboration between student, emerging, and professional theatre practitioners—is one of Malaysia's most highly anticipated annual performing arts events.

This year's lineup includes Jean Genet's provocative classic "The Maids," adapted and directed by award-winning Malaysian theatre director Faridah Merican. The production examines themes of power, servitude, and role reversal through a distinctly Malaysian cultural lens. "Inside Voices," the second production, is an original Malaysian work exploring mental health and intergenerational family dynamics.

The third and final production, "Peristiwa di Zoo" (Incident at the Zoo), is a Malay-language adaptation that reimagines the boundaries between the wild and the civilised, drawing on both local folklore and contemporary urban anxieties. Critics who previewed the production have praised its vivid staging and emotionally resonant performances.

June is rapidly becoming Malaysia's most vibrant month for the performing arts, with venues across Kuala Lumpur, Penang, and Johor Bahru hosting a diverse mix of theatre, dance, and music. The Kuala Lumpur International Mobility Show and other events running concurrently provide additional entertainment options for culture seekers.

Organisers of the STEP Theatre Festival expressed gratitude to the National Arts, Culture, and Heritage Academy (ASWARA) and their corporate sponsors for making the 2026 edition possible. Tickets are available at the venue box office and selected online platforms, with student discounts offered for all three productions to encourage the next generation of theatre audiences.
