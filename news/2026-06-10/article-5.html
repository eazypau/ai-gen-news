Malaysia Attracts RM92.8 Billion in Approved Investments in Q1 2026

Business MIDA 10 June 2026

Malaysia secured RM92.8 billion in approved investments during the first quarter of 2026, demonstrating robust economic momentum despite ongoing global geopolitical uncertainties. The Malaysian Investment Development Authority (MIDA) reported that this figure reflects strong confidence from both foreign and domestic investors in Malaysia's long-term growth story.

Foreign direct investment (FDI) accounted for the majority, at 60.5% or RM56.2 billion of total approvals. Meanwhile, domestic investments grew at an impressive 13% year-on-year, reaching RM36.6 billion—a signal that Malaysian businesses are also expanding their footprint and capabilities.

The investments span a wide range of sectors, with digital transformation, advanced manufacturing, and services leading the pack. MIDA highlighted that the approved investments are expected to create more than 50,000 new employment opportunities across the country, providing a significant boost to the labour market.

Japan led all foreign investors with RM21.5 billion in approved investments, followed by China (RM10.1 billion), the United States (RM10.1 billion), Singapore (RM6.7 billion), and Thailand (RM2.5 billion). The concentration of Japanese investments in digital transformation activities underscores Malaysia's growing role as a high-technology hub in Southeast Asia.

Investment, Trade, and Industry Minister Tengku Datuk Seri Zafrul Abdul Aziz welcomed the numbers as evidence that Malaysia's pro-business policies and strategic positioning are bearing fruit. He added that the government remains committed to creating an enabling environment that attracts quality investments aligned with the New Industrial Master Plan 2030.
