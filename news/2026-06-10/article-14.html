OCBC Cycle Kuala Lumpur 2026 Set to Roll on June 28

Sports EverythingBoleh 10 June 2026

Kuala Lumpur's premier cycling event, the OCBC Cycle Kuala Lumpur 2026, is confirmed for June 28, with thousands of cyclists from Malaysia and across Southeast Asia expected to converge on the capital for a day of competitive and recreational riding through the city's most iconic routes. Registrations have been brisk, with organisers reporting strong participation numbers from both elite cyclists and enthusiastic amateurs.

The event will feature multiple categories catering to different skill levels, including a competitive race for seasoned cyclists and leisure ride options for families and beginners. Routes will wind through major KL landmarks including the KLCC park area, the Putrajaya linkway, and stretches of newly completed cycling infrastructure developed under the city's urban mobility masterplan.

OCBC Bank, the event's title sponsor, has been a long-standing supporter of cycling culture in the region, running similar events in Singapore and other Southeast Asian cities. The Kuala Lumpur edition has grown considerably since its inaugural year and is now considered a flagship fixture on Malaysia's sporting calendar.

Kuala Lumpur City Hall (DBKL) has confirmed that a number of major roads will be temporarily closed to vehicular traffic during the morning of June 28 to facilitate safe passage for cyclists. Motorists are advised to plan alternative routes and check traffic advisory notices closer to the event date.

Beyond the racing action, the event serves as a platform to promote sustainable urban mobility and cycling as an everyday mode of transport. Environmental awareness and wellness booths will be set up at the start and finish points, underscoring the event's broader message of healthy living and green transportation choices.
