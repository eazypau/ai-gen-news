Lee Zii Jia Called Up for Malaysia's BWF Thomas Cup Finals Squad

Sports Olympics.com 10 June 2026

Malaysian badminton star Lee Zii Jia has been selected for the national squad heading to the 2026 BWF Thomas Cup Finals, despite his current world ranking of 83—a reflection of a difficult period of form following his switch to independent status and subsequent return to the national fold. The Badminton Association of Malaysia has expressed confidence that Zii Jia's talent and big-match temperament remain undiminished.

The Thomas Cup Finals, the pinnacle of men's team badminton, will see Malaysia competing alongside traditional powerhouses Indonesia, China, and Denmark. Malaysia last won the Thomas Cup in 1992 and has been seeking to end that drought in recent editions, consistently reaching the knockout stages but falling short of the title.

Zii Jia's inclusion is seen as a calculated gamble by selectors, who believe the 26-year-old still possesses the ability to upset top-10 opponents on his best day. His powerful smash and unpredictable style of play can be a match-winner against any opponent in the world on a given day, according to the national coaching staff.

The squad also includes reigning world number one Ng Tze Yong in the singles, while the doubles lineup features proven pairings in both men's doubles and mixed events. Team captain and head coach have expressed measured optimism, noting that the preparation camp has gone smoothly and that team chemistry is strong heading into the competition.

Malaysian badminton fans have taken to social media to express both excitement and cautious hope. Many remember Zii Jia's electrifying performances at his peak and are hoping that the Thomas Cup team environment will help him recapture his best form at a tournament where team pride can be an enormous motivational factor.
