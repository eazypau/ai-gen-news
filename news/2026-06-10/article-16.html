BTS Concert Malaysia 2026: ARMY Presale Tickets Now Available

Entertainment ZALORA Malaysia 10 June 2026

Global K-pop phenomenon BTS has officially announced a Malaysia concert date in 2026, with the ARMY member presale for tickets kicking off on June 3. The announcement was met with an outpouring of excitement from Malaysian fans who have waited years for the group to perform in the country following the members' completion of mandatory military service.

Tickets for the Kuala Lumpur show are available in multiple tiers, ranging from general admission floor pits to premium seated sections with enhanced stage views. ARMY fan club members were given a 48-hour exclusive presale window before tickets opened to the general public, a now-standard practice for the group's massive worldwide tours.

The concert, to be staged at the newly expanded National Stadium Bukit Jalil, is expected to be one of the largest K-pop events ever hosted on Malaysian soil. Capacity for the outdoor configuration is estimated at approximately 80,000 spectators, and concert organisers say they are preparing logistics to manage the scale of the event safely and efficiently.

BTS's return to Malaysia comes as all seven members—RM, Jin, SUGA, j-hope, Jimin, V, and Jungkook—have completed their mandatory military service obligations, reuniting the group for their first full worldwide tour in several years. The Malaysia date is among over 30 cities on the global itinerary.

The Malaysian economy is expected to benefit from the influx of international fans travelling to Kuala Lumpur specifically for the event, boosting hotel, retail, and food and beverage sectors. Tourism Malaysia has listed the BTS concert as a signature event under the Malaysia Mega Sale and Malaysia Festival Kuala Lumpur 2026 promotional calendar.
