Bursa Malaysia Sees Wave of New IPOs as Startup Scene Matures

Business The Edge Malaysia 10 June 2026

Bursa Malaysia is experiencing a notable surge in initial public offerings in 2026, reflecting the maturation of the country's startup and technology ecosystem. Companies including SkyeChip, Manforce Group, 5E Resources, BIM, and Sunway Healthcare Group have recently listed, drawing substantial investor interest from domestic and foreign institutional funds.

Kuala Lumpur's reputation as a startup hub is gaining international traction, with sectors such as fintech, retail tech, health technology, edtech, and smart mobility producing companies of global scale. The city's relatively low operating costs, multilingual talent pool, and improving regulatory environment are cited as key advantages by founders and venture capitalists alike.

Sunway Healthcare Group's listing has been among the most closely watched, given the group's extensive hospital network and its push into digital health services. Analysts expect the healthcare IPO wave to continue as Malaysia's ageing population drives sustained demand for quality medical care.

SkyeChip's debut was particularly significant for the semiconductor narrative, as it represents one of the first locally-founded chip design companies to go public. The listing signals that Malaysia is not only attracting foreign chip investments but also cultivating domestic semiconductor talent and enterprise.

Bursa Malaysia's CEO noted that the quality and diversity of new listings in 2026 reflect strong investor confidence in Malaysia's economic fundamentals and the government's reform commitments. Plans are underway to streamline the IPO listing process further and to attract more foreign companies to dual-list on the exchange.
