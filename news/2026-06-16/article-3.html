PH Chairman Warns of Plot to Destabilise Negeri Sembilan Government

The Pakatan Harapan (PH) chairman has issued a strong warning about an alleged attempt to topple the Negeri Sembilan state government, despite the state's continued political and economic stability. The warning was made amid a backdrop of intensifying political manoeuvring nationally as Malaysia approaches the 16th General Election (GE16).

Negeri Sembilan, governed by PH under Menteri Besar Datuk Seri Aminuddin Harun, has maintained a stable administration since the last state election. The state has recorded consistent economic indicators and has been spared much of the turbulence experienced in other states during recent political realignments. The PH chairman said the alleged plot is an attempt to undermine a legitimately elected government.

The warning echoes similar concerns that have been raised in other PH-held states, where political rivals have allegedly attempted to engineer party crossings or defections to destabilise sitting governments. The practice, locally known as "katak" (frog) politics, has been a persistent feature of Malaysian political life and drew national outrage during the Sheraton Move of 2020.

PH officials have called on state assemblymen to remain vigilant and united in the face of alleged inducements. The party has also indicated that it possesses intelligence about the source of the alleged destabilisation attempts, though specific details were not publicly disclosed at the time of this report.

The development comes as political parties across Malaysia are jockeying for position ahead of GE16, expected to be called within the coming year. Negeri Sembilan's strategic location, diverse electorate, and relatively stable economy make it a high-value prize for opposition forces seeking to expand their state-level foothold.
