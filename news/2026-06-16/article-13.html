Pos Malaysia Launches Special VM2026 Stamps Showcasing Iconic Malaysian Landmarks

Pos Malaysia Berhad has unveiled a special commemorative series of postage stamps in conjunction with the Visit Malaysia 2026 (VM2026) campaign, carrying the theme "Malaysia, Truly Asia." The stamps celebrate the country's rich tapestry of natural beauty, cultural heritage, and architectural landmarks, and are now available at Pos Malaysia counters nationwide and via the PosShop online platform.

The collection features iconic Malaysian destinations including Langkawi in Kedah — the jewel of the Andaman Sea — Penang Hill, a beloved colonial-era retreat offering sweeping views of George Town; Cameron Highlands in Pahang, known for its tea plantations and cool highland climate; the historic A'Famosa Fort in Melaka, a UNESCO World Heritage Site; and the Petronas Twin Towers in Kuala Lumpur, arguably Malaysia's most recognisable landmark globally.

Tourism Malaysia Director-General Datuk Manoharan Periasamy, speaking at the launch, said the stamps represent a "piece of Malaysia" that collectors and tourists can take home as cherished mementos. The stamps are designed to complement VM2026's broader promotional campaign, which targets 35.6 million tourist arrivals and RM147.1 billion in tourism receipts for the year.

The VM2026 stamp series has already attracted interest from philatelists in Malaysia and internationally, as well as from the tourism and hospitality industry who see them as unique keepsakes for hotel guests and convention attendees. Limited-edition first-day covers and stamp sets are available for collectors at selected post offices.

VM2026 is Malaysia's flagship national tourism campaign, the first such initiative since the COVID-19 pandemic disrupted the previous Visit Malaysia Year in 2020. The campaign is supported by Tourism Malaysia's aggressive promotional activities in key source markets across Asia, the Middle East, Australia, and Europe.
