Sofia Sewing Claims Triple Crown at Leapmotor APP Kuala Lumpur Open 2026

Padel star Sofia Sewing delivered a dominant performance across the board to clinch the prestigious Triple Crown title at the conclusion of the Leapmotor APP Kuala Lumpur Open 2026. The tournament, which attracted 1,760 players from across Asia and beyond, concluded with Sewing cementing her status as one of the premier competitors on the Asia-Pacific Padel (APP) Tour circuit.

The Kuala Lumpur Open is one of the flagship events on the APP Tour calendar and was staged at a state-of-the-art padel facility in the capital. The 2026 edition was the largest in the tournament's history by participant numbers, reflecting the explosive growth of padel as a sport in Malaysia and across the Asia-Pacific region over the past few years.

Sewing's Triple Crown win — encompassing titles in three different event categories within the same tournament — is a remarkable feat that underscores her all-round technical proficiency and physical conditioning. The achievement attracted widespread coverage in Malaysia's sporting media and generated significant social media buzz among the growing padel community.

Padel, a racquet sport combining elements of tennis and squash, has seen rapid adoption in Malaysia, with dozens of new padel courts opening across the Klang Valley, Penang, and Johor Bahru over the past two years. The sport's accessibility — it can be played competitively by people of all ages and fitness levels — has made it particularly popular among corporate players and recreational athletes.

Tournament organisers expressed satisfaction with the KL Open's success and confirmed that Kuala Lumpur would continue to host a round of the APP Tour in 2027, with plans to further expand the event. The title sponsorship by Leapmotor, a Chinese electric vehicle brand with growing presence in Southeast Asia, reflects the increasing commercial attractiveness of padel events in the region.
