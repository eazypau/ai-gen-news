Hamzah Zainudin Launches Parti Wawasan Negara, Confirmed as Opposition Leader

Former Bersatu strongman Tan Sri Hamzah Zainudin officially unveiled his new political vehicle, Parti Wawasan Negara (PWN), on June 13, 2026, just days before the Dewan Rakyat reconvenes for its June 22 sitting. The launch, held at a "Reset Convention," marks the formal conclusion of months of political manoeuvring that began when Hamzah was sacked from Parti Pribumi Bersatu Malaysia (Bersatu) in February 2026.

PAS confirmed it has always preferred Hamzah's return as Opposition Leader, citing discomfort with his continued role following his Bersatu expulsion. With PWN now registered, PAS and the newly formed Parti Wawasan Negara are expected to form the core of a reconstituted opposition bloc ahead of the 16th General Election (GE16). This signals a significant realignment in Malaysian opposition politics.

Hamzah's expulsion from Bersatu stemmed from a bitter leadership tussle with party president Tan Sri Muhyiddin Yassin. After being removed, Hamzah launched the "Reset" movement and declared he would form a new political entity. The launch of PWN fulfils that pledge, giving him a formal platform from which to contest GE16 and reclaim his standing in the national opposition hierarchy.

Political analysts note that the creation of PWN introduces fresh uncertainty into the existing opposition coalition structure. The Perikatan Nasional (PN) bloc, which previously comprised Bersatu, PAS, and Gerakan, now faces the challenge of incorporating PWN or risking a fragmented opposition front against the ruling Pakatan Harapan-led government.

With Dewan Rakyat set to convene on June 22, all eyes will be on whether the speaker formally recognises Hamzah as Opposition Leader, a recognition that carries both ceremonial and strategic importance. The seating arrangement and official designations will signal how Malaysia's parliament intends to reflect the post-Bersatu split political landscape.
