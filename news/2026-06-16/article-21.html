AS Watson and L'Oréal Paris Launch Exclusive Co-Created Beauty Product in Malaysia

AS Watson Group, operator of the Watsons pharmacy and health-beauty retail chain, and L'Oréal Paris, one of the world's largest cosmetics companies, have jointly unveiled the first-ever exclusive co-created beauty product in Malaysia. The collaboration sets a new benchmark for retailer–brand partnership innovation in Southeast Asia and signals a shift toward more personalised, market-specific product development strategies in the beauty industry.

The exclusive product — a skincare formulation tailored specifically for Southeast Asian consumers — was developed using consumer insights gathered through Watsons' loyalty data and L'Oréal's advanced research and development capabilities. AS Watson operates over 200 Watsons stores across Malaysia, giving the partnership unparalleled reach into the Malaysian beauty consumer market.

The co-creation model represents a departure from the traditional retailer–brand relationship, where a brand simply supplies products for a retailer to sell. By involving Watsons directly in the product development process, L'Oréal Paris gains access to proprietary consumer preference data and shelf-level insights, while Watsons benefits from exclusive product offerings that drive store traffic and customer loyalty.

Beauty industry analysts have hailed the partnership as a forward-looking model that others are likely to emulate. In an increasingly competitive retail environment, exclusive co-created products offer both parties differentiation against pure online competition from regional e-commerce platforms. The Malaysian launch is also expected to serve as a template for rollouts in other AS Watson markets across Asia.

The product launch was accompanied by a 360-degree marketing campaign across in-store displays, digital advertising, and influencer partnerships. The campaign targets Malaysia's growing segment of beauty-conscious millennials and Gen Z consumers who actively seek products formulated with local skin type and environmental concerns in mind.
