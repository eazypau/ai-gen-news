LRT3 Shah Alam Line Ready to Launch by End of June, Official Date Coming Soon

Transport Minister Anthony Loke announced that the long-delayed Light Rail Transit 3 (LRT3) Shah Alam Line is now operationally ready, with the official public launch date to be announced within the next two weeks. The news ends years of anticipation for commuters living and working along the line's 37-kilometre corridor stretching from Bandar Utama in Petaling Jaya to Johan Setia in Klang.

The LRT3 project, first announced as part of the Klang Valley integrated transport masterplan, suffered multiple delays due to technical challenges, funding restructuring, and the Covid-19 pandemic. The line has 25 stations and is expected to serve hundreds of thousands of daily commuters, significantly reducing congestion on major arterial roads including the Federal Highway and the KESAS expressway.

Speaking in the Dewan Negara, Loke confirmed that train trials have been conducted to ensure smooth operations. Of the 22 train sets, 14 have already completed trials, while the remaining eight were still undergoing final checks. All technical issues are reported to have been resolved ahead of the imminent launch announcement.

The LRT3 connects key townships and industrial areas including Shah Alam, Klang town, and Port Klang, and will provide crucial last-mile connectivity for workers in Selangor's manufacturing belt. Integration with existing MRT, LRT, and KTM Komuter services at interchange stations will make it a pivotal piece of the Klang Valley rail network.

Industry stakeholders and commuter advocacy groups have welcomed the announcement, noting that the LRT3's opening will be a significant boost to the Klang Valley's public transport ecosystem. Prasarana, which operates the line under its RapidKL brand, has confirmed station facilities, park-and-ride arrangements, and fare structures are all in place for the launch.
