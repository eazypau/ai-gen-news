Red Bull Malaysia Launches 'Next Generation' Programme to Develop Young Athletes

Red Bull Malaysia officially announced the launch of Red Bull Next Generation on June 10, 2026, a regional athlete development platform designed to discover and support the next generation of sporting talent across Southeast Asia. The programme is open to athletes aged 18 to 25 across all sports and disciplines, with applications now officially open to Malaysian athletes.

The initiative aims to provide selected athletes with a comprehensive support package that goes beyond financial assistance. Participants will gain access to world-class coaching expertise, sports science support, performance analytics, nutrition guidance, and mental performance coaching. Red Bull's global network of elite athletes and coaches will be made available to assist programme participants in their development.

Red Bull Malaysia's Managing Director expressed confidence that Malaysia's sporting ecosystem has matured significantly, and that a structured development platform like Next Generation can help bridge the gap between talented grassroots athletes and international competitive excellence. The company cited Malaysia's achievements in badminton, squash, and cycling as evidence of the country's sporting potential.

Applications are evaluated based on athletic potential, competitive results, and personal character attributes. Red Bull Malaysia has emphasised that the programme is not limited to athletes in high-profile sports — niche disciplines such as climbing, mountain biking, and esports are equally welcome. Regional athletes from ASEAN countries are also eligible to apply.

The launch aligns with Malaysia's National Sports Policy and the government's aspiration to win multiple medals at the 2028 Los Angeles Olympics and the 2030 Commonwealth Games, which Malaysia is co-hosting. Red Bull's investment in grassroots athlete development is seen as a positive example of private sector support for Malaysian sport.
