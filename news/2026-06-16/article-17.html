Malaysia Extends 5G Coverage to Most Populated Areas, Boosting Connectivity

Malaysia's government-backed 5G rollout, delivered through Digital Nasional Berhad (DNB), has now extended coverage to most of the country's populated areas, significantly improving mobile broadband connection speeds and reducing latency for millions of Malaysian users. The milestone represents a major achievement for Malaysia's national digital infrastructure agenda.

According to the latest coverage reports, 5G connectivity is now available across most major urban and suburban zones in Peninsular Malaysia, with ongoing deployments extending coverage to secondary towns and selected rural areas. Users on 5G-compatible devices are reporting median download speeds several times faster than 4G LTE, enabling high-definition video streaming, low-latency gaming, and improved productivity applications.

The expanded 5G infrastructure is expected to serve as a critical enabler for Malaysia's digital economy, supporting smart manufacturing applications in industrial parks across Selangor, Penang, and Johor; precision agriculture in rural states; and connected healthcare services in public hospitals. The Malaysian Digital Economy Corporation (MDEC) has highlighted 5G as foundational to achieving the targets set under the Malaysia Digital Economy Blueprint (MyDIGITAL).

Mobile operators including Celcom Axiata, Digi (CelcomDigi), Maxis, and U Mobile have been progressively migrating their subscribers to 5G networks under access agreements with DNB. The single wholesale network model adopted by Malaysia — a first in the region — has been cited by policymakers as key to achieving faster, more equitable 5G deployment compared to a fragmented multi-operator approach.

With the national 5G Phase 1 rollout nearing completion, the government has begun planning for Phase 2, which will focus on deepening indoor coverage in office buildings, shopping malls, and transit hubs, as well as extending 5G to rural and remote communities as part of Malaysia's digital inclusion agenda.
