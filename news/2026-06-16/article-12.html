Mazda CX-5 Gen 3, GWM ORA 5 HEV and Perodua C2 Concept Shine at KLIMS 2026

Several high-profile vehicle premieres have made KLIMS 2026 a must-visit event for Malaysian car enthusiasts. Among the most anticipated debuts are the third-generation Mazda CX-5, GWM Malaysia's electrified ORA 5 HEV, and Perodua's tantalising C2 concept car, each representing a different facet of Malaysia's rapidly evolving automotive market.

The all-new Mazda CX-5 Gen 3 has been the subject of intense speculation for months, and its regional debut at KLIMS 2026 has lived up to expectations. Boasting a redesigned exterior influenced by Mazda's Kodo design philosophy, a premium interior, and a more advanced Skyactiv powertrain, the third-gen CX-5 is expected to retain its status as one of Malaysia's best-selling SUVs when it goes on sale later this year.

GWM Malaysia's ORA 5 HEV — a hybrid-electric iteration of the popular ORA 5 electric hatchback — signals Great Wall Motor's intent to capture the growing hybrid segment in Malaysia. The HEV variant offers extended range and reduced running costs compared to a pure EV, addressing consumer concerns about charging infrastructure. GWM's pricing strategy for the ORA 5 HEV is expected to be aggressively competitive to gain market share.

Perodua's C2 concept drew crowds and significant media attention as a preview of the national carmaker's ambitions in the compact SUV segment. While officially labelled a concept, industry observers believe the C2 closely previews an upcoming production model set to challenge rivals such as the Honda WR-V and Toyota Raize. Perodua has indicated the production version could arrive by 2027.

The strong line-up of debuts at KLIMS 2026 reflects the health of Malaysia's automotive market, which has been one of Southeast Asia's most resilient, driven by robust new model launches, competitive financing, and the government's supportive EV incentive framework. Total industry volume (TIV) is on track to remain above 700,000 units for 2026, according to the Malaysian Automotive Association (MAA).
