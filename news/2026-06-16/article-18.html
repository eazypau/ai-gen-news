Malaysia's Social Media Ban for Under-16s Takes Effect June 1, Platforms Must Verify Ages

Malaysia's landmark legislation restricting social media access for users under 16 officially took effect on June 1, 2026, making Malaysia one of only a handful of countries globally to implement such a measure. Under the new rules, all social media platforms operating in Malaysia must implement robust age verification mechanisms to prevent minors from accessing their services.

Platforms that fail to implement compliant age verification systems or that knowingly allow users under 16 to access their platforms face substantial fines of up to RM1 million per violation. The Communications Ministry has indicated it will monitor compliance through a combination of regulatory audits and public reporting mechanisms. Major platforms including TikTok, Instagram, YouTube, and X (formerly Twitter) have confirmed they are implementing the required changes.

The legislation, passed after extensive public consultation and debate in Parliament, was driven by growing evidence linking excessive social media use among children with mental health challenges including anxiety, depression, body image issues, and cyberbullying. The government cited international research and the experiences of countries like Australia, which introduced similar restrictions in late 2024.

Consumer and digital rights advocates have raised concerns about the practicality of age verification and the potential for privacy risks if platforms collect overly sensitive identity data from users. The Communications Ministry has been working with the Personal Data Protection Department to ensure that age verification processes are privacy-preserving and proportionate.

Educators and child welfare groups have broadly welcomed the measure, while acknowledging it must be accompanied by digital literacy education to help young Malaysians navigate the online world safely as they approach the legal age for social media access. The government has committed to reviewing the policy's effectiveness after 12 months of implementation.
