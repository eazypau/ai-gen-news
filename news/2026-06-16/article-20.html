Malaysia to Expand Hospital Information Systems to 16 Hospitals in 2026 Digital Push

The Malaysian Ministry of Health (MOH) is pressing ahead with a landmark healthcare digitalisation programme, expanding the Total Hospital Information System (THIS) to 16 additional public hospitals in 2026. The initiative is central to Health Minister Dr Dzulkefly Ahmad's vision of transforming Malaysia's public healthcare sector through technology, improving both clinical outcomes and administrative efficiency.

The THIS system integrates all clinical and administrative functions within a hospital — from patient registration and appointment scheduling to laboratory results, pharmacy dispensing, and billing — into a single unified platform. Hospitals operating THIS experience significant reductions in paperwork, faster patient processing, and improved accuracy in medical records management.

Beyond hospitals, the MOH is simultaneously deploying a cloud-based Clinic Management System (CMS) to approximately 2,489 primary care settings across the country, including government health clinics (klinik kesihatan) and 1Malaysia Clinics. This will digitise patient records at the primary care level and enable seamless referrals to specialist hospitals, reducing duplication of investigations and improving care continuity.

A Dental Information System (DIS) is also being rolled out to 157 dental clinics as part of the same digital health initiative. The DIS will digitalise dental records, streamline appointment management, and enable better tracking of oral health trends across the population. Malaysia's public dental service handles millions of patient contacts annually.

The healthcare IT expansion is funded through the national budget and is aligned with Malaysia's health system reform agenda. Health Minister Dr Dzulkefly has described 2026 as a "watershed year" for the MOH's digital transformation, noting that comprehensive health information systems will ultimately enable data-driven policymaking, predictive health analytics, and better resource allocation across Malaysia's public health network.
