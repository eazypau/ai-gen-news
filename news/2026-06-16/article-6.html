Kerjaya Prospek Wins RM529.3M Residential Contract in Taman Duta, KL

Kerjaya Prospek Group Berhad (KPGB) has secured a significant building contract worth RM529.3 million from BRDB Developments Sdn Bhd for a prestigious residential development in Taman Duta, Bukit Tunku, Kuala Lumpur. The project cements KPGB's position as one of Malaysia's premier construction companies and adds substantially to its order book.

Taman Duta is one of Kuala Lumpur's most sought-after residential addresses, nestled in the leafy hills of Bukit Tunku near the city centre. BRDB Developments, a subsidiary of BRDB (formerly known as Bandar Raya Developments Berhad), is known for delivering high-end residential and mixed-use developments in prime urban locations across Malaysia.

The contract involves the construction of a high-rise residential project comprising luxury condominiums and associated infrastructure. The development is expected to appeal to high-net-worth individuals and expatriates drawn to Bukit Tunku's established community, proximity to key embassies, and easy access to Kuala Lumpur's central business district.

KPGB has been on a strong growth trajectory in 2026, capitalising on Malaysia's buoyant property development pipeline. The company's order book now stands at a multi-year high, reflecting robust demand from both public and private sector clients. Analysts expect the Taman Duta contract to underpin KPGB's revenue visibility for the next two to three years.

The award further demonstrates continued investor and developer confidence in Kuala Lumpur's premium residential market, even as some segments of the property market remain subdued. Upmarket projects in established and well-connected locations continue to find buyers, driven by limited land supply and sustained demand from the capital's affluent population.
