Johor Bahru–Singapore RTS Link on Track for End-2026 Passenger Service

The long-awaited Johor Bahru–Singapore Rapid Transit System (RTS) Link is on schedule to commence passenger services by end-2026, with full commercial operations targeted for January 2027. The cross-border rail link, jointly developed by Malaysia's Prasarana and Singapore's SMRT, will fundamentally transform daily cross-border commuting between the two nations.

Prasarana and SMRT have signed a Joint Venture Agreement to govern the operations of the RTS Link, signalling the advanced stage of the project's development. The line will connect Bukit Chagar in Johor Bahru to Woodlands North in Singapore, with the journey expected to take under five minutes — a dramatic improvement over the current road crossings that can take hours during peak periods.

The RTS Link will have a capacity to carry up to 10,000 passengers per hour in each direction at peak times, with trains running at high frequency to cater to the estimated 300,000 to 400,000 daily crossers between Johor Bahru and Singapore. The project is designed to reduce vehicle traffic across the Causeway and the Second Link, easing congestion on both sides of the border.

Custom, immigration, and quarantine (CIQ) facilities are being constructed at both ends to ensure smooth passenger processing. Both governments have committed to a seamless, cashless fare payment system integrated with each country's existing transit network, enabling commuters to travel from a Johor Bahru KTM station all the way to central Singapore on a single card.

The project's completion is a key deliverable of ongoing Malaysia-Singapore bilateral cooperation and is expected to catalyse further economic integration between the Iskandar Malaysia development corridor in Johor and Singapore's expanding business ecosystem. Property developers on both sides of the strait have already been factoring RTS connectivity into new projects.
