KLIMS 2026 at MITEC: 300,000 Visitors Expected as Malaysia's Auto Show Goes Big

The Kuala Lumpur International Mobility Show (KLIMS) 2026 is in full swing at the Malaysia International Trade and Exhibition Centre (MITEC), running from June 12 to 21, 2026. Now in its 11th edition under the theme "Beyond Mobility," KLIMS 2026 has evolved beyond a traditional car show into a comprehensive celebration of smart mobility, sustainability, and lifestyle, with an expected attendance of 300,000 visitors over 10 days.

The show spans eight exhibition halls and features confirmed exhibitors including global automotive giants AION, Audi, BMW, BYD, Honda, Mercedes-Benz, Perodua, Proton, Toyota, and Volkswagen, alongside a host of tech and mobility startups. Visitors can experience live demonstrations, electric vehicle test drives, advanced safety technology showcases, and an esports racing zone.

KLIMS 2026 forms part of Tourism Malaysia's Visit Malaysia Year 2026 (VMY 2026) calendar of major events, positioning the show as both an industry platform and a national tourism draw. Free admission is offered on weekdays (excluding public holidays), making it accessible to school groups, families, and car enthusiasts from across the country.

Beyond cars, the event features a lifestyle marketplace in collaboration with Riuh — a popular Malaysian creative bazaar — as well as live musical performances by Kyoto Protocol and other local artists, a dedicated Pokémon gaming zone, flight simulators, and an extensive food court. The inclusion of lifestyle elements reflects organiser MAA's strategy to broaden KLIMS's appeal beyond traditional auto enthusiasts.

Parking and transport options have been carefully planned, with free shuttle buses connecting KLIMS to nearby KTM Komuter and MRT stations. Visitors can purchase advance tickets online at a discounted rate or pay at the door on weekends. Detailed visitor guides and brand-by-brand previews have been widely published by automotive media to help attendees plan their visit.
