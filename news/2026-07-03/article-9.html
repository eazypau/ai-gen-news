DNB Activates Additional 100MHz of 5G Spectrum Across Malaysia's Network

Lowyat.NET 3 July 2026 Technology

Digital Nasional Berhad (DNB), the government-linked company tasked with rolling out Malaysia's 5G network, has begun activating an additional 100MHz of spectrum across its nationwide infrastructure. The move significantly boosts available 5G capacity and is expected to deliver meaningfully improved speeds and lower latency for both consumer and enterprise users across urban and suburban coverage zones.

The spectrum activation is part of DNB's ongoing commitment to expand coverage and quality as take-up of 5G services continues to grow among Malaysian mobile operators and their subscribers. The additional bandwidth complements the existing 700MHz and 3.5GHz spectrum bands already in use and will enable higher simultaneous user densities in congested areas such as urban centres, transport hubs, and sports venues.

DNB chief executive officer Azlan Badai said the activation is a milestone in the company's network enhancement roadmap, which also includes the deployment of additional 5G base stations in currently underserved areas. "Our focus remains on delivering a 5G network that is reliable, fast and accessible to all Malaysians, not just those in major cities," he stated.

The 5G rollout has been a contentious topic in Malaysia's telecommunications sector, with several major mobile operators seeking greater clarity on the government's long-term network sharing framework. The Communications Minister has previously indicated that a dual-network model — allowing a second 5G wholesaler to compete with DNB — may be explored if coverage and service quality targets are not met on schedule.

Industry observers say the spectrum expansion will be especially beneficial for enterprise customers deploying private 5G networks in manufacturing plants, logistics hubs, and smart city applications. With Malaysia targeting a 5G population coverage rate of over 80% by end-2026, DNB's latest activation is seen as an important step toward meeting that commitment and reinforcing the country's digital infrastructure competitiveness in the ASEAN region.
