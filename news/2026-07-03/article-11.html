Lee Zii Jia Called Up for Malaysia's BWF Thomas Cup Finals Squad

Olympics.com 3 July 2026 Sports

Former world No. 1 badminton star Lee Zii Jia has been recalled to Malaysia's national squad for the BWF Thomas Cup Finals, despite his current world ranking having slipped to No. 83 following a difficult year marked by injury and inconsistent form. The Badminton Association of Malaysia (BAM) selectors chose to back the 27-year-old's match experience and big-tournament temperament over raw ranking points.

BAM chief selector Rexy Mainaky said the decision reflects confidence that Lee Zii Jia's proven ability to perform under pressure in team events makes him a valuable asset for the Malaysian squad. "In team championships, experience and the ability to handle pressure at the deciding rubber matters enormously. Zii Jia has shown time and again he can deliver in those moments," he stated.

Lee Zii Jia's inclusion will likely see him deployed in singles competition, where Malaysia has faced depth challenges in the top tier since the retirement of several veteran players. His pairing with emerging young stars such as Leong Jun Hao and Ng Tze Yong gives Malaysia a formidable blend of youth and experience across its men's singles lineup.

Malaysia last won the Thomas Cup in 1992 and has been striving to reclaim the title ever since. The team reached the final at the 2024 edition but fell short against China. Coach Hendrawan has emphasised team cohesion and tactical flexibility as the key pillars of the 2026 campaign, and Lee Zii Jia's return is seen as a significant confidence boost for the squad's morale.

Lee Zii Jia himself expressed gratitude for the recall and acknowledged the responsibility of representing Malaysia at such a prestigious team event. "I am fully focused on contributing whatever I can to help Malaysia bring the Thomas Cup home. The team comes first, and I will give everything I have," he said. Training camp is currently underway at the Akademi Badminton Malaysia in Bukit Kiara ahead of the tournament.
