DAP Bets on New-Generation Candidates to Shake Up Johor Election

Malay Mail 3 July 2026 Politics

The Democratic Action Party (DAP) is taking a bold gamble ahead of the July 11 Johor state election by fielding a slate of first-time, younger candidates under the Pakatan Harapan (PH) banner. Party leaders say the decision reflects their commitment to nurturing the next generation of political leaders and injecting fresh energy into state governance.

A total of 172 candidates have been cleared to contest all 56 seats in the Johor State Legislative Assembly, producing a hotly contested field that includes 14 straight fights, 27 three-cornered contests, 12 four-cornered battles and three five-way races. The election commission has set July 7 for early voting and July 11 as polling day.

DAP's youth-centric strategy comes as Pakatan Harapan and Barisan Nasional — two coalition partners in Prime Minister Anwar Ibrahim's unity government at the federal level — find themselves contesting every state seat against each other in Johor. Political analysts describe the contest as a "dress rehearsal" that could foreshadow the dynamics of any future snap federal election.

Party veteran and DAP secretary-general Gobind Singh Deo noted that placing first-time candidates in winnable seats is a long-term investment in the party's leadership pipeline. "We believe in giving deserving young Malaysians the chance to serve — and Johor is where we are proving that commitment," he said at a campaign launch event in Johor Bahru.

The Johor seat count from the previous state election in 2022 saw BN win 40 seats, PH secure 12 and Perikatan Nasional take four. With PAS riding a wave of rising religious conservatism and the Johor voters known for pragmatic swing patterns, both PH and BN are competing fiercely for every marginal constituency.
