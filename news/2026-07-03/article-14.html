Limp Bizkit to Play Malaysia's Only Asia Show on 9 December 2026

Buro 24/7 Malaysia 3 July 2026 Entertainment

American nu-metal legends Limp Bizkit have confirmed they will perform their first-ever concert in Malaysia on 9 December 2026, with Kuala Lumpur's National Hockey Stadium serving as the exclusive Asia venue for the band's international tour. The announcement has generated enormous excitement among Malaysian rock and metal fans, many of whom grew up listening to the band's iconic late-1990s and early-2000s discography.

The concert is being promoted as a one-of-a-kind event for fans across the Asian continent, as Kuala Lumpur is the sole Asia stop on the band's current tour schedule. This means fans from neighbouring countries including Singapore, Indonesia, Thailand, and the Philippines are expected to travel to Malaysia specifically for the show, boosting hotel bookings and tourism spending in the city.

Led by frontman Fred Durst, Limp Bizkit rose to global fame with albums including "Significant Other" (1999) and "Chocolate Starfish and the Hot Dog Flavored Water" (2000), which together produced some of the most recognisable rock anthems of the era. The band has experienced a significant commercial and cultural revival in recent years, driven partly by nostalgia among millennial audiences and renewed interest from younger fans discovering the nu-metal genre.

The event promoter, Live Nation Malaysia, said tickets will go on general sale in mid-July 2026, with early access offered to Maybank and selected credit card holders. Ticket pricing will be announced closer to the general on-sale date, with seated, standing and VIP floor categories expected to be available at the 45,000-capacity National Hockey Stadium.

Music fans have celebrated the announcement on social media, with the hashtag #LimpBizkitKL trending on X (formerly Twitter) within hours of the news breaking. The concert adds to a strong lineup of international acts confirmed for Malaysia in the second half of 2026, cementing Kuala Lumpur's growing reputation as a top destination for major live music events in Southeast Asia.
