Singer 2026 Malaysia Reveals Six Debut Performers Including Jaclyn Victor and Priscilla Abby

World of Buzz 3 July 2026 Entertainment

Astro has officially unveiled the six debut singers for Singer 2026 – Malaysia, its adaptation of the hit Chinese singing competition format. The confirmed line-up features veteran Malaysian performers Jaclyn Victor, Priscilla Abby, and Vanessa Reynauld alongside newer talents Geraldine Gan, Uriah See, and Nicole Lai — a group that together represents the breadth and diversity of Malaysian musical talent.

Jaclyn Victor, best known for winning the inaugural Malaysian Idol competition in 2004, brings over two decades of live performance experience to the competition and is widely regarded as one of Malaysia's most powerful vocalists. Priscilla Abby, a veteran R&B and soul singer with a devoted fan base built over years of consistent album releases and live shows, is expected to be a standout in the competition's later rounds.

The inclusion of Uriah See and Nicole Lai signals Astro's intent to showcase emerging Malaysian Chinese talent alongside established names, giving younger audiences a new generation of artists to root for. Geraldine Gan and Vanessa Reynauld round out the group with their distinct styles in pop and indie music respectively, promising a wide variety of musical performances throughout the series.

Singer 2026 – Malaysia follows the format of the internationally successful Chinese programme, in which established artists compete through a series of escalating performance challenges judged by industry panels and audience votes. Previous Malaysian editions have consistently ranked among Astro's highest-rated entertainment programmes, drawing viewership from across Southeast Asia.

The production team has confirmed that the 2026 edition will feature a reimagined stage design and an expanded live audience experience compared with previous seasons. Premier dates and broadcast scheduling on Astro's entertainment channels have yet to be officially announced, but industry sources suggest filming has already begun and the first episode is expected to air before August 2026.
