Fake WhatsApp Account Impersonates Johor PH Candidate, Communications Minister Condemns Act

Free Malaysia Today 3 July 2026 Politics

Communications Minister Datuk Fahmi Fadzil has strongly condemned the creation of a fraudulent WhatsApp account impersonating Pakatan Harapan candidate Saiful Nizam Samat, who is contesting the Endau state seat in the upcoming Johor election. The fake account was used to spread disinformation to voters in the constituency ahead of the July 11 polling day.

Minister Fahmi described the act as "irresponsible and dangerous," warning that spreading false information during an election campaign constitutes a criminal offence under Malaysian communications law. He urged voters to verify all political communications through official party channels and to report suspected fake accounts to the Malaysian Communications and Multimedia Commission (MCMC).

The incident is part of a broader pattern of digital disinformation that has intensified during the Johor election campaign. Authorities have noted a rise in fabricated screenshots, fake endorsements, and manipulated audio clips circulating on social media platforms since the campaign period officially began. The Election Commission has reiterated its zero-tolerance stance on election-related misinformation.

Police confirmed they are investigating the fake account under Section 233 of the Communications and Multimedia Act 1998, which carries a penalty of up to one year imprisonment and a fine of up to RM50,000. Saiful Nizam Samat filed an official report at a local police station on the evening of July 2.

Digital rights advocates have called for stronger platform accountability, urging WhatsApp's parent company Meta to respond faster to election-related impersonation complaints in Malaysia. With early voting scheduled for July 7, observers say swift action is critical to prevent the disinformation from influencing voter turnout in the Endau constituency.
