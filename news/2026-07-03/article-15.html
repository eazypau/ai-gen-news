AsiaTop Music Festival to Feature Seventeen Sub-Unit, Win Metawin and Local Stars

Free Malaysia Today 3 July 2026 Entertainment

The AsiaTop Music Festival is set to bring a star-studded cross-cultural celebration to Malaysia, featuring an extensive lineup that draws from South Korea, China, Thailand, and homegrown Malaysian talent. The festival promises to be one of the biggest pan-Asian music events held in Malaysia to date, blending K-pop, C-pop, and Thai pop alongside beloved local artists.

Confirmed K-pop acts include CxM — a sub-unit of the globally acclaimed group Seventeen — along with Daesung of BIGBANG fame, girl groups NMixx and StayC, boy bands TWS, Cravity, and 82Major. The South Korean contingent alone represents a significant draw for Malaysia's large and dedicated K-pop fanbase, which has grown steadily over the past decade.

From China, the lineup includes Firdhaus, Kun, and Next1de, bringing Mandopop and C-pop styles that resonate strongly with Malaysia's Chinese-speaking community. Thai actor and singer Win Metawin, who has cultivated a massive pan-Asian following through his roles in popular Thai dramas and solo music releases, rounds out the international roster alongside Thai and regional pop acts.

Malaysian artists anchoring the local segment include Aina Abdul — one of the country's most celebrated contemporary pop voices — Belle Sisoski, and Uriah See, who also features in the Singer 2026 Malaysia competition announced this week. The combination of international headliners and beloved local names is designed to appeal to as broad an audience as possible and promote Malaysian artists to international attendees.

Full festival details including dates, venue, and ticketing information are expected to be officially announced within the next two weeks. Early interest has been enormous, with a pre-registration mailing list reported to have surpassed 50,000 sign-ups within 24 hours of the lineup reveal. Organisers say the festival is targeting an audience of over 30,000 attendees per day and is in final negotiations with a major Kuala Lumpur outdoor venue.
