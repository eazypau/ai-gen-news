Visit Malaysia 2026 Campaign Success Prompts Extension Plans Into 2027

Buoyed by strong performance in tourism arrivals and receipts, the Malaysian government is planning to extend its Visit Malaysia 2026 (VM2026) campaign into 2027, Tourism Minister Tiong King Sing announced. The proposal, which must be formally endorsed by the Cabinet, reflects the government's confidence in Malaysia's growing appeal as a global tourism destination and its desire to sustain the economic momentum generated by the campaign.

Tourism Minister Tiong said that extending VM2026 into 2027 would allow Malaysia to continue capitalising on the extensive promotional infrastructure, partnerships, and brand recognition built up during the current campaign year. Rather than starting from scratch with a new campaign identity, the extension would allow Marketing Malaysia to maintain continuity and build on international media coverage, travel agent relationships, and event programming already in place.

The extension proposal comes as Malaysia's tourism sector records its strongest performance in years. International visitor arrivals in Q1 2026 grew 22% year-on-year, with the number of high-value, longer-stay visitors — a key quality metric for the campaign — showing particular strength. Tourism receipts are on track to surpass the RM147.1 billion full-year target, with the peak travel season from June to August expected to provide a significant boost.

Industry stakeholders — including hotel chains, airlines, tour operators, and retail businesses — have strongly supported the proposed extension, noting that tourism campaigns typically take 18 to 24 months to reach their full promotional impact in key source markets. An extension into 2027 would ensure that investments made in marketing Malaysia to first-time visitors from emerging source markets such as India, the Middle East, and Central Asia bear fruit across two full travel seasons.

If approved by Cabinet, the extended VM2026/27 campaign would represent the longest continuous national tourism initiative in Malaysia's history. Tourism authorities are expected to finalise and announce the extension formally within weeks, with a refreshed event calendar and new tourism product launches planned to sustain visitor interest through 2027.
