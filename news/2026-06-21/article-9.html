Petronas and Japan's JERA Sign Long-Term LNG Supply Deal Extending Partnership into the 2040s

Malaysia's Petronas and Japan's largest power generation company JERA Co., Inc. have reaffirmed and extended one of Asia's longest-standing Liquefied Natural Gas (LNG) partnerships through the signing of a new long-term supply agreement. The deal, signed at a formal ceremony in Kuala Lumpur, extends their energy collaboration well into the 2040s, reflecting mutual confidence in the long-term role of LNG in Asia's energy mix.

Under the terms of the new agreement, Petronas will supply JERA with up to approximately 2 million tonnes per annum (MTPA) of LNG over a 20-year period commencing in 2028. The contract replaces and upgrades a previous arrangement and represents one of the most significant long-term LNG commitments in Southeast Asia's energy trade history. JERA operates some of Japan's largest and most modern gas-fired power plants, making it a cornerstone customer for Malaysian LNG exports.

Both parties emphasised that the deal underpins energy security for Japanese consumers and industrial users, who rely heavily on imported LNG to meet electricity and heating demands. Japan's energy policy continues to prioritise stable, long-term supply contracts with trusted partners as the country navigates its own energy transition — balancing the phase-out of coal with the expansion of renewables and the continued role of natural gas in ensuring grid stability.

For Petronas, the deal cements its position as one of the world's most reliable LNG suppliers and provides long-term revenue visibility for its upstream gas projects, particularly those in Sarawak and the deepwater fields off Sabah. The Malaysian national oil company has been actively renewing and expanding its portfolio of long-term LNG contracts with Northeast Asian buyers as existing agreements approach maturity.

Petronas President and Group CEO Tan Sri Tengku Muhammad Taufik described the agreement as a testament to the enduring strength of Malaysia-Japan energy ties. He added that Petronas remains committed to being a trusted, responsible, and sustainable energy partner for JERA and all its customers as the region navigates the global energy transition together.
