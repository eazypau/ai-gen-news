Visit Malaysia 2026 Campaign Officially Launches, Targeting 35.6 Million International Visitors

Malaysia's flagship Visit Malaysia 2026 (VM2026) tourism campaign officially launched with nationwide celebrations at major international entry points including KLIA, Penang International Airport, and Senai International Airport in Johor. The campaign, branded under the iconic "Malaysia Truly Asia" tagline, sets bold targets: 35.6 million international visitor arrivals and RM147.1 billion (approximately US$33 billion) in tourism receipts for the year.

Tourism Malaysia described the VM2026 campaign as the most ambitious and well-resourced tourism initiative in the country's history, backed by a dedicated promotional budget and a calendar packed with more than 500 major events spanning culture, sports, food, nature, and arts throughout 2026. Key events include the Formula E Street Circuit races in Kuala Lumpur, the Malaysian Philharmonic Orchestra's international gala season, the National Heritage Festival in Penang, and a series of eco-tourism showcases in Sabah and Sarawak.

The campaign places significant emphasis on promoting Malaysia's lesser-known states and destinations — particularly Kelantan, Terengganu, Perlis, Sabah, and Sarawak — to diversify visitor footfall beyond the traditional hotspots of Kuala Lumpur and Penang. New tourism packages highlighting indigenous cultural experiences, wildlife conservation tourism, and wellness retreats have been developed in collaboration with local communities and tour operators.

Malaysia has also leveraged its growing status as a medical tourism destination, the second-largest in ASEAN after Thailand, to attract health-conscious travellers. Combined health and leisure packages have been marketed to visitors from the Middle East, South Asia, and East Asia, where Malaysia's combination of world-class private hospitals, tropical destinations, and halal-friendly hospitality offers a compelling proposition.

Tourism Minister Tiong King Sing expressed confidence that the VM2026 targets are achievable given Malaysia's strong recovery trajectory since 2023 and the country's growing profile as a destination of choice for experience-driven travellers. Early results from the first quarter of 2026 were encouraging, with international arrivals up 22% year-on-year, putting the annual target within reach.
