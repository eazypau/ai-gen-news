PETRONAS Confirms Fuel Supply at Its Stations Secured Through End of June 2026

Malaysia's national oil company PETRONAS issued a formal public statement confirming that fuel supply at all PETRONAS petrol stations across Peninsular Malaysia, Sabah, and Sarawak is fully secured through the end of June 2026. The announcement was made to address growing public concern about potential fuel shortages following media reports of disruptions to maritime oil transport routes through the Strait of Hormuz due to ongoing geopolitical tensions in the Middle East.

PETRONAS said the company had taken proactive steps to secure adequate fuel inventories well in advance, working with its upstream production subsidiaries, refining operations at the PETRONAS Refinery and Petrochemical Integrated Development (RAPID) complex in Pengerang, Johor, and alternative supply routes to ensure continuity of supply. The company confirmed that its strategic fuel reserves are at healthy levels and that retail stations across the country would experience no disruption to supply.

Prime Minister Anwar Ibrahim had earlier personally thanked Iranian President for ensuring the safe transit of Malaysian oil tankers through the Strait of Hormuz, acknowledging the diplomatic sensitivity of the situation and the importance of maintaining open maritime lanes for energy trade. Malaysia, as a net energy exporter, is less exposed than many Asian economies to import disruptions, but the country still relies on the secure passage of its own crude oil and LNG exports through key maritime chokepoints.

Energy analysts noted that PETRONAS's rapid and transparent communication helped prevent unnecessary public panic and fuel hoarding, which could have created artificial shortages. The statement was praised by consumer associations and industry observers as an example of responsible corporate communication during a period of external uncertainty.

Looking beyond June, PETRONAS said it was in advanced negotiations with additional supply sources to further diversify its procurement and reduce exposure to any single transit route. The company also reiterated its commitment to investing in domestic refining capacity to enhance Malaysia's energy self-sufficiency over the medium and long term, as part of its broader strategic alignment with the national energy transition agenda.
