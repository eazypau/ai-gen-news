Banks Scrap RM1 Interbank ATM Fee from July, Giving Malaysians Unlimited Free Withdrawals

Malaysians will enjoy a significant financial relief starting July 1, 2026, as all major banks in the country have agreed to eliminate the RM1 fee previously charged for interbank cash withdrawals at Automated Teller Machines (ATMs) and Smart Recycler Machines (SRMs) nationwide. The move, announced earlier this month, ends a long-standing charge that affected millions of Malaysians who frequently use ATMs belonging to banks other than their own.

The decision was made following consultations between Bank Negara Malaysia and the country's major banking institutions, including Maybank, CIMB, Public Bank, RHB, and Hong Leong Bank, among others. The central bank said the abolition of the fee aligns with its broader agenda to enhance financial inclusion and reduce the cost of banking for ordinary Malaysians, particularly those in lower-income brackets who may not always have convenient access to their own bank's ATMs.

Under the previous system, customers were charged RM1 each time they withdrew cash from an ATM not owned by their primary bank — a fee that added up quickly for individuals who relied on interbank withdrawals regularly. Consumer advocacy groups had long called for the fee's removal, arguing it placed a disproportionate burden on those with fewer financial resources.

The announcement has been broadly welcomed by the public and consumer organisations. The Malaysian Consumer Movement (MCM) issued a statement praising the decision as a meaningful step toward a fairer banking ecosystem. Fintech observers noted that the move also reflects increasing pressure from digital wallets and e-payment platforms that offer fee-free fund transfers, prompting traditional banks to improve their value proposition to customers.

Bank Negara Malaysia confirmed that the change will apply across all participating banks' ATM networks nationwide, covering both urban centres and rural communities. Customers do not need to take any action — the fee waiver will take effect automatically from the first day of July 2026.
