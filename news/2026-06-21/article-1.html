Anwar: Malaysia Entering New High-Tech Economic Phase, Development Must Benefit All

Prime Minister Datuk Seri Anwar Ibrahim declared on Saturday that Malaysia is entering a transformative new economic phase defined by high technology, semiconductors, artificial intelligence, and digital innovation. Speaking at a national economic forum in Kuala Lumpur, he said this shift represents the most significant structural change in Malaysia's economy since the era of industrialisation in the 1990s.

Anwar emphasised that while the push toward high-tech industries is essential for Malaysia's long-term competitiveness, the government remains firmly committed to ensuring that the benefits of this transition reach all Malaysians — including those in rural areas, lower-income communities, and marginalised groups. "Prosperity must be shared. We cannot build a high-tech Malaysia that leaves half the population behind," he said.

The Prime Minister highlighted several key pillars of Malaysia's high-tech strategy, including the expansion of the semiconductor supply chain in Penang and Kulim, investment in AI research centres and data infrastructure, and the development of quantum computing capabilities through university-industry partnerships. He noted that Malaysia has already attracted over RM200 billion in data-centre and manufacturing commitments in recent years.

Anwar also pointed to the government's efforts to reskill the Malaysian workforce to meet the demands of a digital economy. New technical and vocational education programmes are being rolled out in collaboration with leading global technology companies, and a national AI literacy campaign targeting three million Malaysians was announced as part of the initiative.

The Prime Minister concluded by urging government agencies, the private sector, and civil society to work in tandem to accelerate the transition, warning that countries that fail to adapt to the new technological order risk being left behind in an increasingly competitive global landscape. Malaysia, he said, has the talent, the infrastructure, and the political will to succeed.
