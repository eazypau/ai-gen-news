Malaysian Semiconductor Firms Launch Advanced Packaging Consortium Targeting 7% Global Market Share

Five leading Malaysian semiconductor companies — SkyeChip, Inari Amertron, FusionAP, Pentamaster, and NSW Automation — formally launched the Malaysia Advanced Packaging Consortium (MAPC) at SEMICON Southeast Asia 2026 in Penang. The consortium aims to carve out a 7% share of the global advanced packaging market by 2035, a segment currently dominated by Taiwanese, South Korean, and US firms.

Advanced packaging — the technology used to stack, connect, and integrate multiple chips into a single module — has become one of the most strategically important areas in semiconductor manufacturing. It enables the dense, high-performance chip configurations required by AI accelerators and high-performance computing systems. Malaysia currently holds no meaningful position in this segment, making the MAPC's formation a bold and necessary strategic move.

The consortium's inaugural project is an HBM4 (High Bandwidth Memory 4) pilot line, designed to develop and commercialise high-bandwidth memory stacking technology critical to next-generation AI chips. Each of the five founding members contributes defined and complementary capabilities — from chip design expertise to precision automation and test systems — creating a vertically integrated domestic capability for the first time.

The Malaysian government's support was cited as a key enabler, with the Ministry of Investment, Trade and Industry (MITI) and the Malaysian Investment Development Authority (MIDA) confirming a dedicated RM500 million co-investment fund for advanced packaging research and pilot infrastructure. Industry observers noted that the initiative positions Malaysia to move up the semiconductor value chain, from back-end commodity packaging to high-value advanced integration.

MAPC chairman and SkyeChip co-founder Lim Wei Kang said the consortium represents a once-in-a-generation opportunity for Malaysia's semiconductor industry. "We are not just assembling chips — we are building the intellectual and industrial foundation to compete at the highest level of global semiconductor technology," he said at the launch ceremony.
