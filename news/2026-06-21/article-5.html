SEMICON Southeast Asia 2026 Convenes Over 20,000 Leaders in Malaysia to Drive Semiconductor Growth

SEMICON Southeast Asia 2026 drew more than 20,000 industry innovators, senior executives, policymakers, and technology experts to Malaysia this month, making it one of the largest and most influential semiconductor industry gatherings ever held in the region. The event, organised by SEMI, took place at the Penang International Convention Centre and spanned three days of conferences, exhibitions, and networking sessions.

A landmark milestone was highlighted throughout the conference: the global semiconductor industry is on track to surpass USD 1 trillion in annual sales for the first time in history in 2026, driven by an unprecedented boom in AI infrastructure investment. Demand for chips powering data centres, AI accelerators, and edge computing devices has outpaced all previous industry forecasts, with Southeast Asia — and Malaysia in particular — emerging as a critical node in the global supply chain.

Malaysia's role in the semiconductor ecosystem was a central theme of the event. The country currently accounts for approximately 13% of global semiconductor back-end packaging and testing capacity, and government officials used the conference to announce further incentives to attract front-end wafer fabrication investment. Several major US, Taiwanese, and European chipmakers confirmed expansion plans at Malaysian facilities during the event.

The conference also addressed the urgent need for talent development to sustain Malaysia's semiconductor ambitions. Delegates discussed strategies to produce 100,000 additional skilled engineers and technicians over the next five years, with universities and polytechnics announcing new specialised programmes in semiconductor engineering, chip design, and advanced materials science.

Industry leaders at SEMICON SEA 2026 described Malaysia as uniquely positioned to benefit from supply chain diversification trends, citing the country's political stability, existing manufacturing expertise, and proactive government investment policies. The next edition is already being planned for 2027, with organisers expecting even greater participation from global players.
