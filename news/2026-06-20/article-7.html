GreatAsic Raises US$6.9 Million to Build Southeast Asia's Leading IC Design Firm

Source: Digital News Asia Date: 20 June 2026 Technology

Malaysian integrated circuit design startup GreatAsic has closed a US$6.9 million pre-series A funding round, with ambitions to establish itself as Southeast Asia's leading IC design house. The raise was led by regional venture capital firms with participation from a strategic semiconductor industry investor, and will fund talent acquisition, design tool infrastructure and new customer engagements.

Founded by a team of veteran chip designers, GreatAsic specialises in custom application-specific integrated circuits (ASICs) targeting automotive, industrial IoT and consumer electronics applications. The company's growth trajectory is closely aligned with Malaysia's National Semiconductor Strategy, which explicitly identifies IC design as a priority capability gap to be filled over the next decade.

CEO of GreatAsic said the funding would allow the company to double its engineering headcount and open a second design centre in Penang, complementing its existing Kuala Lumpur operations. The company is also investing in EDA (Electronic Design Automation) software licenses and cloud-based chip simulation infrastructure to accelerate design cycles.

Malaysia currently punches below its weight in front-end semiconductor activities relative to its strong back-end assembly, test and packaging (ATP) sector. GreatAsic and a handful of other emerging local design houses are seen as early catalysts in bridging that gap, supported by government incentives under the Chips Act-equivalent framework introduced in the 2025 budget.

Investors expressed confidence that the global chiplet architecture trend and growing demand for customised silicon in edge AI applications create a favourable commercial environment for GreatAsic's design-house model, and that the company is well-timed to ride the semiconductor super-cycle fuelled by AI hardware demand.
