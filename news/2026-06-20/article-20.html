Inaugural Malaysia Open 2026 Pickleball Tournament Wraps Up at Grand Pickleball

Source: Sin Chew Daily Date: 20 June 2026 Sports

The first-ever Oriental Daily News Malaysia Open 2026 pickleball competition has concluded successfully at Grand Pickleball, marking a historic milestone for the sport in Malaysia. The tournament drew competitors from across Malaysia and several ASEAN nations, reflecting pickleball's rapid ascent from niche recreational activity to a competitive sport with a fast-growing grassroots and professional following in the region.

The event featured categories for both professional and amateur players, with mixed doubles, men's singles and women's singles divisions drawing strong participation numbers. Organisers noted that the quality of play on display exceeded expectations for a debut tournament, with several competitors demonstrating a high standard of skill that bodes well for Malaysia's growing pickleball competitiveness at the international level.

Pickleball, which combines elements of tennis, badminton and table tennis, has exploded in popularity globally over the past four years, driven partly by its accessibility to older players and beginners, its low equipment cost and the relatively compact court size compared to tennis. In Malaysia, dedicated pickleball courts have sprouted up in recreational clubs, condominiums and sports complexes across the Klang Valley, Penang and Johor Bahru.

The Malaysia Pickleball Association (MPA) welcomed the tournament as a catalyst for the sport's further development and announced plans to grow the national competitive calendar to include at least six sanctioned events annually by 2027. The association is also in discussion with the Olympic Council of Malaysia about a pathway for pickleball to be included as a demonstration sport at future SEA Games editions.

Organisers have confirmed that the Malaysia Open will return in 2027 as a larger event with higher prize money and the ambition to attract international Professional Pickleball Association (PPA) tier players. The next staging will also incorporate a youth development tournament running concurrently, aiming to identify and nurture the next generation of Malaysian pickleball champions.
