Digital Realty Establishes Malaysia Operations as Part of Asia Pacific Data Centre Expansion

Source: Digital News Asia Date: 20 June 2026 Technology

Global data centre real estate investment trust Digital Realty has officially established its Malaysia operations as part of a major Asia Pacific expansion push, adding Malaysia to its international PlatformDIGITAL network. The company operates data centre campuses across four continents and its Malaysia entry marks a significant vote of confidence in the country's digital infrastructure maturity.

Digital Realty's initial Malaysia deployment focuses on carrier-neutral colocation and interconnection services, targeting enterprise customers in financial services, media, telecommunications and cloud computing verticals. The company has already secured anchor customers for its initial Malaysia capacity and is in active discussions with hyperscale cloud providers about larger dedicated deployments.

The choice of Malaysia for Digital Realty's APAC expansion follows assessments of several criteria including power reliability and cost, network connectivity, government incentives, climate risk, and geopolitical stability. Malaysia scored favourably across all dimensions, and the Digital Economy Corporation's facilitation support was cited as a differentiator.

The entry follows that of several other global data centre operators in Malaysia including Equinix, ST Telemedia Global Data Centres and NTT, which have collectively committed billions of ringgit in capital expenditure to Malaysian facilities over the past three years. Together they are helping Malaysia rapidly build out a digital infrastructure backbone that rivals Singapore at lower cost.

The government has set ambitious targets for the digital economy's contribution to GDP and views data centre attraction as a catalyst that creates high-value engineering jobs, stimulates electricity grid upgrades, and generates significant tax revenues. Digital Realty's arrival adds further momentum to that programme.
