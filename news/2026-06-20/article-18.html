Toh Ee Wei Refuses to Rule Out World Championship Title Defence in New Delhi

Source: Malay Mail Date: 20 June 2026 Sports

Malaysian mixed doubles badminton ace Toh Ee Wei has defiantly refused to close the door on a title defence at the BWF World Championships in New Delhi, even as she continues her recovery from an injury sustained earlier this year. The national number one and reigning world champion expressed cautious optimism about her fitness timeline when speaking to reporters on the sidelines of a training session in Bukit Jalil.

Toh, who alongside partner Chen Tang Jie claimed Malaysia's first mixed doubles world title in 2025, has been on a modified training regime that limits her court exposure to prevent re-aggravating the injury. Her medical team is said to have set a clearance deadline in early August, which would give her roughly six weeks of competitive preparation before the World Championships begin.

The reigning champion also confirmed she has not ruled out participation at the 2026 Asian Games in Aichi-Nagoya, Japan, which is scheduled for later in the year and represents another peak competition target for the Malaysian badminton programme. Badminton Association of Malaysia (BAM) officials indicated they are closely monitoring her recovery and will not rush her return if there is any risk of aggravating the injury.

Toh's injury has added pressure to Malaysia's mixed doubles depth, with Chen Tang Jie required to explore temporary pairing options for tune-up tournaments ahead of the World Championships. The pair's absence from recent Super Series events has allowed rival pairs from China, South Korea and Thailand to accumulate ranking points, potentially affecting Malaysia's seeding at the Worlds.

Badminton fans across the country have rallied behind Toh's recovery, with the national sports fraternity expressing confidence that the champion's determination and fitness levels make a timely return entirely possible. BAM's medical and physiotherapy team have been cited as among the best in the sport, giving grounds for optimism about her rehabilitation progress.
