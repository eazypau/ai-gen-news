KLCI Target Set at 1,821 Points as Tech and Semiconductor Stocks Drive Broad Market Rally

Source: The Edge Malaysia Date: 20 June 2026 Finance

Analysts have set an aggregate 12-month target of 1,821 points for the FBM KLCI based on compiled Bloomberg target prices for the 30 constituent companies, reflecting projected corporate earnings growth of approximately 7% for 2026 — more than double the pace recorded in 2025. The stock market closed the week on a positive note, with gainers clearly outpacing losers on the back of selective buying in technology-related counters.

Semiconductor and electronics stocks have been the standout performers, riding the global AI hardware investment wave and benefiting from Malaysia's growing profile as an alternative manufacturing destination for companies looking to diversify out of Taiwan and China. Companies involved in chip packaging, testing, and electronic manufacturing services have seen particularly strong foreign fund participation.

Banking stocks are also contributing to the positive market tone, with major lenders reporting improving net interest margins and benign asset quality trends. The financial sector's earnings are supported by loan growth recovery, particularly in mortgage, SME and trade finance segments, as the domestic economy maintains its expansion momentum.

Potential headwinds for the market include the impact of a stronger ringgit on exporters' earnings when translated back from US dollar revenues, geopolitical risk premium in global equities, and the possibility of portfolio outflows if US Federal Reserve policy shifts attract capital back to dollar assets. Some analysts have flagged stretched valuations in the technology segment as a near-term consolidation risk.

Retail investor participation on Bursa Malaysia has remained robust, with the Securities Commission's ongoing financial literacy initiatives contributing to a larger domestic investor base. The launch of new ETF products tracking AI and semiconductor indices has also channelled retail capital into relevant market segments, providing additional buying support for the technology sector.
