Perikatan Nasional Set to Settle Logo Dispute and Seat Allocations for Johor and Negri Sembilan Polls

Source: Free Malaysia Today Date: 20 June 2026 Politics

Perikatan Nasional (PN) is moving decisively to resolve its simmering logo dispute and finalise seat distribution arrangements ahead of anticipated state elections in Johor and Negri Sembilan. Senior PN leaders confirmed that negotiations between Bersatu, PAS and Gerakan are entering their final stages, with a resolution expected before the end of June.

The logo question has been a point of contention since PN's formation as a formal coalition, with component parties disagreeing over which party symbol should appear on ballot papers in mixed-ethnicity constituencies. Legal and electoral commission advisories have added complexity to the discussions, with the Election Commission requiring final logos to be submitted well in advance of any polling date.

On seat allocations, sources say PAS is pushing for a larger share of Malay-majority seats in Negri Sembilan, while Bersatu wants to contest its traditional strongholds in Johor. Gerakan, the coalition's non-Malay component, has been lobbying for at least six urban seats across both states where its candidate profile is strongest.

Political analysts note that PN's ability to present a unified front will be crucial to its performance in both states. A fractured coalition risks vote-splitting that could hand PH-BN victories even in seats where the combined opposition vote theoretically exceeds the ruling bloc's support.

PN leadership is expected to hold a formal press conference within days to announce the agreed framework, with party presidents from Bersatu, PAS and Gerakan all expected to appear jointly as a show of coalition solidarity ahead of the campaign period.
