Ringgit Hits 8-Year High at 3.97 Per Dollar, Crowned Asia's Top-Performing Currency

Source: Bloomberg / The Edge Malaysia Date: 20 June 2026 Finance

The Malaysian ringgit has strengthened to approximately RM3.97 against the US dollar, reaching its strongest level since June 2018 and cementing its status as Asia's best-performing currency over the past twelve months. The currency has appreciated more than 10% against the greenback in 2025 alone, and the gains have continued into mid-2026.

The ringgit's rally has been driven by a combination of factors: sustained foreign direct investment inflows into Malaysia's technology and semiconductor sectors, improving current account dynamics, steady Bank Negara Malaysia monetary policy, and growing investor confidence in Malaysia's role in the global AI infrastructure supply chain. The narrative of Malaysia as a preferred alternative to high-cost data centre locations like Singapore has attracted significant capital flows.

Bank Negara Malaysia has maintained its overnight policy rate at 3.00%, resisting pressure to cut rates despite regional easing trends, on the view that the Malaysian economy remains robust and that cutting prematurely could reignite inflationary pressures. The relative interest rate differential has also supported carry-trade demand for ringgit-denominated assets.

Exporters, particularly electronics and palm oil producers, have welcomed the stronger ringgit with caution, noting that it compresses margins on US dollar-denominated revenues. However, importers and consumers have benefited from lower input costs and more affordable imported goods. The central bank has reiterated that it does not target any specific exchange rate level.

Analysts at major investment banks have revised their year-end ringgit forecasts upward, with several projecting the currency could test the RM3.90 level if FDI inflows continue at current pace and global risk appetite remains elevated. The broader ASEAN currency complex has strengthened in tandem, with the Thai baht and Indonesian rupiah also posting solid gains.
