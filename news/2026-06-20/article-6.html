Collins Aerospace Quadruples MRO Footprint in Malaysia with US$63 Million Investment

Source: RTX / New Straits Times Date: 20 June 2026 Technology

RTX's Collins Aerospace has dramatically expanded its maintenance, repair and overhaul (MRO) operations in Malaysia, committing a US$63 million investment to quadruple its facility footprint at Subang Aerotech Park in Selangor. The expansion grows the facility from 46,000 square feet to 164,000 square feet, making it one of the largest Collins Aerospace MRO centres in the Asia Pacific region.

The expanded facility will service a wider range of aircraft systems, including avionics, aircraft interiors and mechanical components. Collins Aerospace cited Malaysia's strong aerospace workforce, strategic location between major Asian aviation markets, and supportive government policies as key drivers behind the investment decision.

The announcement was welcomed by Malaysia's Ministry of Investment, Trade and Industry (MITI) as further evidence of the country's growing attractiveness as a regional aerospace services hub. Malaysia is home to several major MRO operators and has been actively cultivating an aerospace cluster in the Klang Valley and Senai, Johor, under the National Aerospace Industry Blueprint.

Collins Aerospace employs hundreds of Malaysian engineers and technicians at the Subang facility, and the expansion is expected to create an additional 200–300 skilled jobs once at full operational capacity. Training partnerships with Universiti Kuala Lumpur's Malaysian Institute of Aviation Technology (UniKL MIAT) will be enhanced to support the talent pipeline.

The investment contributes to Malaysia's ambition of becoming a top-five global MRO destination by 2030. With Asian air traffic continuing to recover strongly post-pandemic, regional demand for quality MRO services is projected to grow significantly over the next decade, and Malaysia is well-positioned to capture that growth.
