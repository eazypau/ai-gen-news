Alibaba Cloud Launches Public Cloud Region in Johor, Strengthening Malaysia's Digital Infrastructure

Source: Business Today / Digital News Asia Date: 20 June 2026 Technology

Alibaba Cloud has activated its new public cloud region in Johor, Malaysia, giving businesses across the country and the broader ASEAN region faster, lower-latency access to enterprise cloud computing, storage and AI services. The Johor region is Alibaba Cloud's second availability zone in Malaysia, complementing its existing infrastructure and significantly increasing total capacity and redundancy.

The new region supports multi-availability zone deployments, enabling enterprise customers to architect highly available, disaster-resilient applications within Malaysian data sovereignty boundaries. Key services available at launch include Elastic Compute Service, Object Storage Service, ApsaraDB for RDS, and a suite of AI model inference endpoints powered by Alibaba's Qwen large language model family.

Alibaba Cloud's Malaysia country manager noted that local demand for cloud services has grown substantially as Malaysian enterprises accelerate digital transformation programmes, with particular growth from financial services, manufacturing and retail sectors. The Johor location also serves as a strategic node for customers in Singapore and Indonesia seeking cross-border data processing capabilities.

The investment is part of Alibaba Cloud's US$1 billion Southeast Asia infrastructure commitment announced in 2025, which targets Malaysia, Thailand and the Philippines as priority markets. Malaysia has attracted investments from multiple hyperscalers in recent years including Microsoft, Google, Amazon Web Services and ByteDance, reflecting the country's emergence as a regional data centre hub driven by competitive power costs, political stability and a growing tech talent pool.

Digital economy observers see the accumulation of hyperscaler investments as a structural tailwind for Malaysia's ambition to position itself as Southeast Asia's preferred AI infrastructure host. Johor in particular has become a focal point for data centre construction given its proximity to Singapore, its available industrial land, and improving power grid infrastructure.
