Pantai Hospital KL Launches AI-Powered Adaptive Radiotherapy for Personalised Cancer Care

Source: Digital News Asia Date: 20 June 2026 Health

Pantai Hospital Kuala Lumpur has officially launched an AI-powered adaptive radiotherapy system, marking a significant advancement in cancer treatment capabilities within Malaysia's private healthcare sector. The technology is designed to continuously adapt radiation delivery in real time, responding to daily changes in tumour size, shape and position — a precision that traditional radiotherapy systems cannot match.

The system uses machine learning algorithms integrated with on-board imaging to generate updated treatment plans within minutes before each session, rather than following a fixed plan set at the outset of treatment. Clinical studies have shown that adaptive radiotherapy can significantly reduce radiation dose to surrounding healthy tissue, thereby lowering the risk of long-term side effects in cancer survivors.

Pantai Hospital's oncology team said the technology is particularly beneficial for patients with prostate, cervical, bladder and head-and-neck cancers, where organ movement and weight changes during treatment can significantly alter the optimal radiation target. The hospital noted that the system is among only a handful deployed in Southeast Asia.

The launch is part of a broader strategic investment by IHH Healthcare, Pantai's parent company, in technology-enabled clinical services across its ASEAN hospital network. IHH has identified AI-assisted diagnostics and therapy as a core growth pillar, with similar systems being evaluated for deployment in Gleneagles KL and other group hospitals.

Healthcare analysts welcomed the development as a step toward positioning Malaysia as a regional centre of excellence for oncology, which in turn supports the government's ambition to grow medical tourism revenues and reduce the outflow of Malaysian patients to Singapore and Thailand for advanced cancer care.
