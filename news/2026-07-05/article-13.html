FIFA World Cup 2026 Round of 16: Malaysian Fans Pack Mamak Stalls for Late-Night Action

Sports Malay Mail / Says.com 5 July 2026

The FIFA World Cup 2026 is gripping Malaysian football fans like few tournaments before it, with the expanded 48-team format delivering wall-to-wall action throughout June and July. With the Round of 16 in full swing, 24-hour mamak stalls, kopitiams and community halls across the country are packed with fans braving late-night kickoff times to watch the knockout drama unfold live.

This year's tournament in the United States, Canada and Mexico marks a historic first for World Cup broadcasting in Malaysia. For the first time in over two decades, free-to-air broadcaster RTM has secured the rights to air all 104 matches on its television channels and the RTMKlik streaming platform, breaking Astro's long-standing monopoly on World Cup coverage. Unifi TV is also streaming the full tournament, with both subscription and pay-per-view options available.

Fans who missed out on subscribing to any paid service can stream selected matches for free through RTMKlik and MyTV Online using their MyDigital ID login credentials. The accessibility of this year's coverage has been broadly praised, with social media flooded with videos of diverse crowds gathering at public viewing areas and eateries to share the experience of watching the world's biggest sporting event together.

Among the storylines captivating Malaysian viewers are the unexpected runs of Asian and African nations, the performances of football superstars from Europe and South America, and the drama of penalty shootouts that have already produced several stunning upsets in the group stage. Fan favourite matches have seen entire neighbourhoods quiet down as viewers hunker around screens at home and in communal spaces.

The quarter-finals are scheduled for July 12, the semi-finals on July 15 and 16, and the final on July 20 at MetLife Stadium in New Jersey. With the decisive stages approaching, Malaysian fans are expected to keep the late-night viewing parties going right through to the final whistle of the championship match.
