MADANI Government Rolls Out RM15 Billion Financing Support for MSMEs

Business Bernama 5 July 2026

The MADANI Government has unveiled a sweeping RM15 billion financing package designed to empower micro, small and medium enterprises (MSMEs) across Malaysia. Announced in mid-May 2026, the initiative channels funds through a network of development financial institutions, commercial banks and government guarantee schemes to make credit more accessible for businesses that have historically struggled to secure conventional financing.

The package targets a broad range of underserved entrepreneurs, including women business owners, Orang Asli community enterprises, rural operators and young entrepreneurs launching their first ventures. SME Corp Malaysia has been tasked with coordinating outreach and ensuring that the funds reach those most in need rather than being concentrated among established businesses.

Finance Ministry officials said the RM15 billion commitment reflects the government's recognition that MSMEs — which account for over 98% of all business establishments in Malaysia and employ nearly half the national workforce — are the true backbone of the economy. Strengthening MSME financing capacity is seen as essential to sustaining domestic demand as global trade conditions remain uncertain.

The package includes soft loans at below-market interest rates, micro-financing facilities for businesses seeking loans of under RM50,000, and an expanded government guarantee programme that covers up to 80% of loan value for qualifying borrowers. Digital application platforms have been set up to reduce paperwork and speed up disbursement times.

Industry associations welcomed the announcement but called for streamlined approval processes and greater transparency in fund allocation. The Malaysian SME Association noted that access to financing remains the single biggest constraint for small businesses, particularly in the wake of rising operating costs driven by energy price adjustments and minimum wage increases in 2025.
