Singer 2026 Malaysia Unveils Lineup: Jaclyn Victor, Priscilla Abby and Vanessa Reynauld Among Six Debut Artists

Entertainment World of Buzz / Astro 5 July 2026

Astro has officially unveiled the six debut artists for Singer 2026 — Malaysia, the Malaysian edition of the globally popular singing competition franchise that originated in China and has since been adapted across Asia. The confirmed lineup comprises acclaimed Malaysian performers Jaclyn Victor, Priscilla Abby and Vanessa Reynauld, alongside Geraldine Gan, Uriah See and Nicole Lai, forming a diverse ensemble that spans multiple generations and musical styles.

Jaclyn Victor, who rose to national fame as the winner of the inaugural Malaysian Idol in 2004, brings more than two decades of concert performance and recording experience to the competition. Her powerful vocal range and commanding stage presence have made her one of Malaysia's most enduring pop and soul artists. Priscilla Abby is known for her contemporary R&B and crossover appeal, while Vanessa Reynauld brings a folk and singer-songwriter dimension to the mix.

Geraldine Gan, Uriah See and Nicole Lai represent a newer generation of Malaysian talent with strong independent music followings built through social media platforms and streaming services. Their inclusion signals Astro's intent to make Singer 2026 a showcase for both established icons and emerging voices in Malaysian music.

Singer 2026 — Malaysia will air on Astro's entertainment channels and streaming platform sooka, with the competition format involving weekly elimination rounds judged by a panel of music industry professionals and audience votes. The show is expected to generate significant social media buzz given the combination of nostalgia-driven stars and fresh talent that the lineup represents.

The announcement has generated considerable excitement on Malaysian social media, with fans expressing particular enthusiasm for Jaclyn Victor's participation. Her return to competitive performance for the first time in many years is expected to draw strong viewership from both long-time fans and younger audiences discovering her artistry for the first time through the competition format.
