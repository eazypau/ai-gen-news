Malaysia Unveils New Passport with State-of-the-Art Security Features

Technology New Straits Times 5 July 2026

The Malaysian government has officially unveiled a redesigned national passport featuring a comprehensive suite of next-generation security features aimed at reducing document fraud, streamlining immigration processing and enhancing Malaysia's standing in international travel agreements. The new passport was presented by the Home Ministry and the National Registration Department at a ceremony in Putrajaya.

The revamped document incorporates upgraded biometric enhancements including a more secure chip architecture that stores fingerprint and facial recognition data in line with the latest ICAO standards. The physical booklet features laser-perforated personalisation, polycarbonate data pages that are significantly harder to alter than laminate-based predecessors, and a range of optically variable security inks that are visible under ultraviolet and infrared light.

A new thematic design has been introduced on the visa pages, celebrating Malaysia's biodiversity and cultural heritage through artistic motifs representing iconic flora, fauna and traditional crafts from Peninsular Malaysia, Sabah and Sarawak. The cover retains the traditional dark blue with the national coat of arms embossed in gold.

Immigration Department Director-General said the new passport places Malaysia among the top tier of secure travel documents globally and will facilitate smoother processing at automated border gates in countries that have bilateral arrangements with Malaysia. Currently, Malaysian passport holders enjoy visa-free or visa-on-arrival access to over 180 countries and territories.

The new passport will be issued to Malaysians renewing or applying for the first time beginning August 1, 2026. Existing valid passports will remain accepted until their expiry date and holders are not required to replace them early. The NRD has set up an online appointment system to manage demand at its offices nationwide.
