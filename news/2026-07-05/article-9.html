Interbank ATM Fee Waiver Takes Effect July 1 — Relief for Millions of Malaysians

Technology Fintech News Malaysia 5 July 2026

A significant change for Malaysian bank customers has come into effect, with the banking industry's interbank ATM withdrawal fee waiver officially activated on July 1, 2026. Malaysians can now withdraw cash from any bank's ATM without being charged the RM1 interbank fee that has been a fixture of daily banking life for over a decade.

The fee waiver was brokered by Bank Negara Malaysia (BNM) as part of its broader financial inclusion and consumer protection agenda. BNM had been in negotiation with the Association of Banks in Malaysia (ABM) and the Association of Islamic Banking and Financial Institutions Malaysia (AIBIM) for several months before reaching agreement on a timeline for implementation.

Consumer advocacy groups praised the move, estimating that the waiver could save the average Malaysian up to RM12 to RM24 per year depending on their banking habits — a modest but meaningful amount especially for lower-income groups who tend to conduct more frequent small cash withdrawals. In rural areas where ATM density may be lower and people often use different banks' machines, the savings are expected to be proportionally higher.

Meanwhile, CTOS Digital, a leading credit reporting agency, welcomed the development as a positive signal for financial inclusion. The company also announced the appointment of Creador founder Brahmal Vasudevan as its new chairman, effective July 1, in a separate development that signals continued boardroom strengthening at one of Malaysia's key financial infrastructure companies.

Banks have noted that the waiver represents a revenue reduction but have committed to absorbing the cost rather than passing it on through other fees. Several banks are also using the occasion to accelerate their push for cashless transactions, with renewed promotions for digital wallet top-ups and QR payment incentives as they work to reduce ATM operating costs over the long term.
