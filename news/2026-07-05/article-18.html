July 2026 Streaming Guide: Enola Holmes 3 Headlines a Star-Studded Month for Malaysian Viewers

Entertainment Harper's Bazaar Malaysia 5 July 2026

July 2026 is shaping up to be a stellar month for Malaysian streaming audiences, with a packed calendar of new releases across Netflix, Disney+, Apple TV+ and local platforms like sooka and Viu. Leading the charge is Enola Holmes 3, the third instalment of Netflix's beloved mystery series starring Millie Bobby Brown, which whisks the young detective off to sun-drenched Malta for her most complex and treacherous case yet.

Brown's return as Arthur Conan Doyle's spirited younger sister has been eagerly anticipated by fans who fell in love with the franchise's blend of period mystery, feminist themes and sharp wit. The Malta setting promises visually stunning cinematography and a grander scale of production compared to the London-centric earlier films. Henry Cavill is confirmed to reprise his role as Sherlock Holmes.

K-drama enthusiasts have a full slate to enjoy through July, with several new series premiering on Netflix and Viu spanning romance, thriller and historical fantasy genres. Disney+ is dropping a highly anticipated Marvel series mid-month, while Apple TV+ adds a prestige drama miniseries featuring an ensemble of award-winning actors. Local Malaysian content also features prominently, with Astro Original series and Malay-language films available through sooka maintaining a strong domestic flavour in the streaming mix.

For those looking for something lighter, streaming platforms are supplementing the fictional slate with a range of food travel documentaries, comedy specials and reality competition shows that make good weekend viewing. The global football buzz around FIFA World Cup 2026 is also reflected in several sports documentaries and football film releases timed to capitalise on the tournament's momentum.

Harper's Bazaar Malaysia's monthly streaming guide recommends Malaysian viewers create watch lists early given the volume of releases and the likelihood that conversation around key titles will peak quickly. Enola Holmes 3 in particular is expected to trend strongly in Malaysia given the franchise's large following, and fans are encouraged to block out their July 4 weekend — the global Netflix premiere date — for a viewing session.
