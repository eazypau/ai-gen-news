Frasers Group Sells Sports Direct Malaysia Stake in $150 Million Deal

Sports Sharecast / The Star 5 July 2026

British retail conglomerate Frasers Group has announced the divestment of its entire stake in Sports Direct Malaysia in a transaction valued at approximately $150 million. The sale was announced on July 1, 2026, and marks the end of Frasers' direct operational presence in the Malaysian sports retail market after more than a decade of brand expansion across Southeast Asia.

Sports Direct Malaysia operates a network of stores across major shopping malls throughout Peninsular Malaysia, Sabah and Sarawak, selling a wide range of sporting goods, athletic apparel and footwear including brands such as Nike, Adidas, Puma, Under Armour and Frasers' own house brands including Slazenger and Donnay. The stores have been a prominent fixture in Malaysian retail, particularly in anchor positions at major malls.

The buyer has not been publicly named in regulatory disclosures filed by Frasers Group in London, with the announcement noting only that the transaction is subject to customary regulatory approvals and is expected to complete within the third quarter of 2026. Industry sources suggest the acquirer is a Malaysian or regional private equity firm with an existing retail portfolio in Southeast Asia.

Frasers Group, controlled by billionaire Mike Ashley, has been rationalising its international retail portfolio and concentrating resources on its premium House of Fraser department stores, Evans Cycles, and its growing e-commerce and luxury operations in Europe. The Malaysia divestment is consistent with this broader strategic refocus toward higher-margin markets and formats.

Malaysian retail analysts said the transaction reflects broader pressures on big-box sports retail in the country, where competition from online platforms such as Lazada, Shopee and Zalora has intensified, and consumer preferences have shifted toward specialised and experiential retail formats. The new owner will need to invest in the brand and the customer experience to maintain market share.
