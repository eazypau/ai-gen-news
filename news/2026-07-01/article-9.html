Ringgit Trades at RM4.08 Per USD Amid Persistent US Dollar Strength

Economy Source: The Edge Malaysia 1 July 2026

The Malaysian ringgit opened July 2026 trading at approximately RM4.08 against the US dollar, reflecting continued pressure from a strong greenback amid sustained US rate-hike expectations. The currency has weakened roughly 3% over the past month, touching levels close to its weakest point since November 2025, as investors gravitated toward higher-yielding US dollar assets.

Currency strategists point to the US Federal Reserve's hawkish stance as the primary driver of ringgit weakness. Despite robust domestic economic fundamentals — including strong GDP growth, low unemployment, and moderate inflation — the ringgit, like most emerging-market currencies, has been unable to fully resist the gravitational pull of USD strength.

Over a 12-month horizon, however, the ringgit has actually gained approximately 2.7% against the dollar, reflecting the overall improvement in Malaysia's economic fundamentals and the Bank Negara Malaysia's successful management of monetary policy. Bank Negara has maintained the overnight policy rate (OPR) at a level that balances growth support with currency stability.

Analysts expect the ringgit to remain range-bound between RM4.00 and RM4.15 per USD in the near term, barring a significant shift in US monetary policy signals or a major external shock. A moderation in US rate expectations — anticipated later in 2026 — could provide a catalyst for ringgit appreciation toward the end of the year.

Malaysia's export-oriented businesses, particularly those in the E&E and palm oil sectors, have benefited from a weaker ringgit as it makes their products more competitive globally. However, importers and businesses with USD-denominated costs face higher expenses, adding mild inflationary pressure to input costs in certain sectors. Bank Negara is expected to closely monitor exchange rate developments and intervene if volatility becomes excessive.
