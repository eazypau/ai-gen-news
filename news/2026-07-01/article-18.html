Malaysia T20 Cricket League to Debut in September–October 2026

Sports Source: Stock Titan / Flash Sports & Media 1 July 2026

Flash Sports & Media (NASDAQ: FLZH) has officially launched the Malaysia T20 League in partnership with the Malaysian Cricket Association (MCA), with the inaugural season set to run from September to October 2026. The franchise-based T20 cricket league represents a significant milestone for Malaysian cricket, which has long sought a professional domestic league to complement the national team's development programme.

The Malaysia T20 League will feature multiple city-based franchise teams competing across a round-robin group stage and knockout finals format, modelled on the success of leagues such as the Indian Premier League (IPL), Big Bash League in Australia, and the Caribbean Premier League. Franchise owners include a mix of local investors and international cricket personalities, reflecting growing commercial interest in the Malaysian cricket market.

MCA President Mahindran Jeffrey expressed enthusiasm for the league, calling it a "game-changing moment" for Malaysian cricket. He noted that the league would not only provide a high-quality competitive environment for Malaysian players but also attract overseas cricket stars to play in Malaysia, raising the overall standard of the game and drawing new fans to the sport.

Flash Sports & Media CEO indicated that broadcast rights for the Malaysia T20 League have been secured with local and international streaming partners, ensuring the matches are accessible to Malaysian cricket fans globally. Discussions are also underway with major Malaysian brands for title sponsorship, with several companies from the telecommunications, banking, and FMCG sectors reportedly in advanced talks.

Cricket, while not among the most popular sports in Malaysia, has a passionate following particularly within the Indian Malaysian community and among young Malaysians inspired by the global T20 boom. The launch of the Malaysia T20 League coincides with Malaysia's growing ambition to qualify for ICC global tournaments, with the national team having made significant strides in associate cricket rankings over the past three years.
