CTOS SME Biz Day 2026 Targets AI Adoption and Digitalisation for MSMEs

Technology Source: Says.com 1 July 2026

CTOS SME Biz Day 2026, Malaysia's premier event for micro, small, and medium enterprises (MSMEs), brought together thousands of business owners and entrepreneurs to accelerate their digital transformation journey. The event featured multiple thematic zones covering AI adoption, digitalisation strategy, leadership development, compliance, and business financing — giving MSMEs direct access to practical tools and ecosystem support under one roof.

Malaysia has approximately 1.2 million registered SMEs, which collectively account for 97.4% of all business establishments in the country and contribute 38.4% to GDP. Despite their economic significance, many Malaysian SMEs remain at early stages of digital adoption, with AI integration rates significantly lower than their counterparts in Singapore, South Korea, and Taiwan. CTOS SME Biz Day 2026 aims to bridge this gap by demystifying AI and helping business owners see the practical applications for their operations.

This year's event placed particular emphasis on generative AI tools for business productivity, including AI-powered customer service chatbots, predictive inventory management, and automated bookkeeping. Speakers from tech companies including Microsoft, Google, and local startups demonstrated how SMEs can adopt AI solutions at different budget levels without needing dedicated IT departments.

Credit risk management provider CTOS also showcased updates to its CTOS SME Score product, which uses machine learning to generate creditworthiness assessments for small businesses lacking traditional financial records. The product aims to improve access to financing for the estimated 40% of Malaysian SMEs that are currently underserved by conventional bank lending due to insufficient credit history.

The government's SME digitalisation grants, offered through agencies such as SME Corp, MDEC, and MARA, were highlighted as key enablers for MSMEs looking to fund their technology upgrades. Industry observers noted that uptake of these grants has accelerated significantly in 2026, with applications rising nearly 60% year-on-year as business owners grow more comfortable with digital tools following the post-pandemic transformation of Malaysian commerce.
