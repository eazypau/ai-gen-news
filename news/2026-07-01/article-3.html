Hamzah Zainudin Named Opposition Leader Without PN Supreme Council Approval

Politics Source: Sin Chew Daily 1 July 2026

PAS president Tan Sri Abdul Hadi Awang announced the return of Datuk Seri Hamzah Zainudin as Opposition Leader in Parliament without first consulting the Perikatan Nasional Supreme Council, triggering immediate backlash from within the coalition. The announcement, made unilaterally, has been criticised by PN component party leaders who insist that such a significant decision requires collective agreement at the highest level of the coalition.

Hamzah, who previously served as Opposition Leader before stepping down amid internal disagreements, is expected to reassume the role as the Johor state election campaign intensifies. His return is seen by some as an attempt to project unity within the PN coalition ahead of the July 11 polls, though the controversy surrounding how the announcement was made has overshadowed that message.

Sources within Bersatu and other PN component parties expressed frustration at Hadi's move, with some describing it as an overreach of PAS's authority within the coalition structure. The PN Supreme Council is the proper decision-making body for such appointments, and bypassing it risks undermining the coalition's internal governance mechanisms.

Political analysts suggest the episode highlights ongoing tension between PAS and Bersatu over leadership and direction within the Perikatan Nasional coalition. As the largest PN party in terms of parliamentary seats, Bersatu has at times chafed at what it perceives as PAS's dominance over coalition messaging and strategy.

The controversy comes at an inopportune time for PN as the coalition seeks to mount a credible challenge in the Johor state election. Opposition unity and a clear leadership structure are considered critical to maximising PN's vote share against BN and PH in the three-way contest for Johor's 56 state seats.
