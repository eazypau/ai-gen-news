May 2026 Supplementary Electoral Roll Open for Public Checking Until July 29

Politics Source: Bernama 1 July 2026

The Election Commission (SPR) has opened the May 2026 supplementary electoral roll for public inspection for a period of 30 days, running until July 29, 2026. All eligible Malaysian citizens are encouraged to verify their voter registration details online via the SPR portal at myundi.spr.gov.my or at any SPR office nationwide.

The supplementary roll includes newly registered voters, those who have updated their addresses, and citizens whose voter status has been revised since the last published electoral roll. Checking the roll is a critical step for voters wishing to participate in upcoming state elections, including the Johor state election on July 11 and the Negeri Sembilan election scheduled for August 1, 2026.

SPR Chairman Tan Sri Abdul Ghani Salleh urged Malaysians, especially young first-time voters registered under automatic registration, to confirm that their polling centres and addresses are correct. Errors in the electoral roll — such as incorrect constituency assignment — could prevent eligible voters from casting their ballots on polling day.

Under Malaysia's automatic voter registration system, all Malaysians who have turned 18 are automatically registered as voters. However, it remains the responsibility of each voter to ensure their records are up to date and that their assigned polling station is accessible and correct.

Any objections or discrepancies found in the supplementary roll must be submitted to the SPR within the 30-day inspection window. After the window closes, the roll will be finalised and used for subsequent elections and by-elections. The SPR has deployed additional helpdesk personnel at its state offices to assist voters with enquiries.
