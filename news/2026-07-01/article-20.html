Malaysia Passport Enhanced with 94 Security Features, Rises to 6th in World Ranking

Travel Source: Business Today 1 July 2026

Malaysia has significantly upgraded the security features of its international passport, incorporating 94 new anti-forgery measures that make it one of the most secure travel documents in the world. The enhanced passport, unveiled by the Immigration Department of Malaysia, features advanced holographic overlays, microprinting, UV-reactive inks, and biometric chip upgrades designed to prevent counterfeiting and enhance border security globally.

Alongside its upgraded security, the Malaysian passport has also climbed to sixth place in the 2026 Henley Passport Index, its highest ranking in the 20-year history of the index. This represents a significant improvement from 12th place in 2025 and reflects the expansion of Malaysia's visa-free and visa-on-arrival access to an unprecedented 183 countries and territories worldwide — up from 179 the previous year.

In Southeast Asia, Malaysia's passport ranks second only to Singapore's, which continues to hold the top position globally. Key recent additions to Malaysia's visa-free access list include several European Schengen zone nations and select Latin American countries, which were secured through bilateral diplomatic negotiations facilitated by the Ministry of Foreign Affairs under Datuk Seri Mohamad Hasan.

The Passport Index, a separate ranking system, places Malaysia's passport at third in the world with a mobility score of 174 — reflecting a slightly different methodology that weights visa-on-arrival and e-visa access more heavily than the Henley Index. Regardless of the specific ranking methodology used, all indices agree that the Malaysian passport is among the most powerful in the world, a fact that underscores the country's strong diplomatic relationships and global standing.

Home Minister Datuk Seri Saifuddin Nasution Ismail stated that the security enhancements were critical to maintaining Malaysia's trusted status among international immigration authorities. He noted that a high-quality, secure passport not only facilitates the freedom of movement of Malaysian citizens globally but also contributes to national security by ensuring the integrity of Malaysia's identity documents. The new enhanced passports are now being issued at all Immigration Department offices nationwide and at Malaysian diplomatic missions abroad.
