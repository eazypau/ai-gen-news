Johor State Election: 172 Candidates Contest 56 Seats on July 11

Politics Source: Malay Mail 1 July 2026

The 16th Johor state election is set for July 11, 2026, with 172 candidates cleared to contest across 56 state seats. The Election Commission confirmed that early voting will take place on July 7, with 24,677 postal ballot papers issued to eligible voters including overseas Malaysians. A total of 2.72 million voters are registered for the poll, making it one of the most significant state elections in recent years.

The contest includes 14 straight fights, 27 three-cornered races, 12 four-cornered battles, and three five-way contests, highlighting the complexity of the political landscape in Johor. More than a dozen parties and six independents are participating, reflecting a highly contested electoral environment where both the ruling state government and opposition blocs are fighting hard for every seat.

The incumbent Barisan Nasional state government under Menteri Besar Onn Hafiz Ghazi is defending its position against challenges from Pakatan Harapan and Perikatan Nasional. Notably, BN and PH — which sit together in the federal unity government under PM Anwar Ibrahim — are contesting every seat against each other in Johor, creating an unusual dynamic where federal allies become state rivals.

The Johor election is widely viewed as a key political barometer for public sentiment towards both the federal unity government and the opposition coalition. Election analysts note that Johor's results could influence political momentum ahead of the Negeri Sembilan state election scheduled for August 1, 2026.

Voters have been urged to turn out in force, with advocacy groups calling on the estimated 1.13 million Malaysians who work across the causeway in Singapore to return home and exercise their democratic right. The Election Commission has set up additional polling facilities to accommodate the expected high voter turnout across the southern state.
