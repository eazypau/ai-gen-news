Malaysian Athletes Gear Up for Commonwealth Games in Glasgow (July 23 – Aug 2)

Sports Source: Bernama 1 July 2026

Malaysia is finalising preparations to send a national contingent to the 2026 Commonwealth Games in Glasgow, Scotland, scheduled from July 23 to August 2. The Games represent one of the most important multi-sport events in the international calendar for Malaysian athletes, providing a high-profile competitive platform ahead of the Aichi-Nagoya Asian Games in September and October 2026.

The Malaysian contingent is expected to field athletes across a wide range of disciplines including badminton, shooting, athletics, cycling, diving, gymnastics, lawn bowls, squash, swimming, and weightlifting. Badminton and squash traditionally offer Malaysia the strongest medal prospects at Commonwealth level, while athletes in shooting and cycling have also produced podium results at past editions.

National Olympic Committee of Malaysia (OCM) Secretary-General Datuk Low Beng Choo confirmed that preparations are on track, with athlete selections for the Games finalised based on performance at national trials and international qualifying events held earlier this year. The OCM has set a medal target that reflects both the competitive strength of the Malaysian squad and the depth of talent from countries such as Australia, England, India, and Canada.

The Glasgow Commonwealth Games will be the first edition of the event held in the UK since the Birmingham 2022 Games, and organisers have promised a world-class experience with state-of-the-art venues across the city. For young Malaysian athletes, the Games serve as an important stepping stone — providing international competitive experience before the more demanding Asian Games and, looking further ahead, the 2028 Los Angeles Olympics.

National coaches across all disciplines have intensified training camps in the lead-up to July 23, with sports science support and mental conditioning playing an increasingly central role in Malaysia's elite athlete preparation programmes. Sports Minister Hannah Yeoh expressed pride in the national contingent and urged Malaysians to rally behind their athletes during the Games, noting that live coverage will be available through national broadcaster RTM and subscription sports channels.
