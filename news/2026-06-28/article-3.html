Anwar Unveils Pakatan Harapan's Full Slate of 56 Johor Candidates

Prime Minister Datuk Seri Anwar Ibrahim officially unveiled Pakatan Harapan's complete line-up of 56 candidates for the upcoming Johor state election during a well-attended press conference. The announcement, which came on June 22 ahead of nomination day, revealed a mix of incumbents, returning faces and fresh candidates selected to contest every constituency in the state.

Pakatan Harapan, which is contesting under the coalition banner comprising PKR, DAP and Amanah, is aiming to reclaim significant ground in Johor after its performance in the 2022 state election. The seat allocation reflects a concerted effort to balance ethnic representation, regional diversity and candidate quality across urban, semi-urban and rural seats.

Anwar expressed confidence that Pakatan's platform — centred on good governance, anti-corruption commitments, and inclusive economic development — resonates strongly with Johor voters, particularly younger and first-time electors. He pointed to the federal government's record on economic management and institutional reforms as key selling points for the campaign.

The line-up features several prominent local figures who are expected to be formidable competitors in their respective constituencies. A number of youth-oriented candidates have also been fielded as part of PH's broader strategy to energise the youth vote, which has grown significantly in Johor's electorate since the last state election.

Political analysts note that the strength of PH's candidate selection in marginal seats will be critical. With a three-cornered contest in many constituencies due to PN's presence, vote splitting remains a key variable that both BN and PH campaign strategists are carefully monitoring as the July 11 polling day approaches.
