Singer 2026 Malaysia Unveils Six Debut Acts Including Jaclyn Victor and Priscilla Abby

Astro has announced the six debut performers who will represent Malaysia in Singer 2026 – Malaysia, the local adaptation of the acclaimed regional singing competition. Leading the lineup are beloved veterans Jaclyn Victor — winner of the inaugural Malaysian Idol — and Priscilla Abby, alongside Vanessa Reynauld, Geraldine Gan, Uriah See and Nicole Lai, completing a diverse and talented cast of six Malaysian artistes.

Singer 2026 – Malaysia marks a new chapter in regional entertainment collaboration between Astro and partners in China, where the original Singer format originated. The competition places Malaysian talent on an internationally recognised stage, challenging them to deliver world-class vocal performances across a range of genres and styles over multiple broadcast episodes.

Jaclyn Victor, who is widely regarded as one of Malaysia's most powerful vocalists, is expected to be a crowd favourite throughout the competition. Her participation has already generated significant social media buzz, with fans expressing excitement at the prospect of seeing her compete against other regional talents on such a high-profile platform.

Priscilla Abby, known for her distinctive voice and versatile artistry, brings considerable competitive experience and a strong following to the lineup. The inclusion of younger contestants like Geraldine Gan and Nicole Lai signals Astro's commitment to developing and showcasing the next generation of Malaysian musical talent alongside established names.

Singer 2026 – Malaysia is expected to premiere on Astro later this year, with broadcast dates to be confirmed. The series is anticipated to draw strong viewership across the Malaysian-Chinese community and beyond, building on the success of previous regional editions that have introduced Malaysian audiences to the high production values and intense competitive format of the international Singer franchise.
