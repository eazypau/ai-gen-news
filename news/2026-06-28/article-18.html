EXO Returns to Malaysia for First Concert in Six Years

Legendary K-pop group EXO is set to return to Kuala Lumpur for their first Malaysian performance in six years, sending the local EXO-L fanbase into a frenzy of excitement. The announcement of the concert has been one of the most widely shared entertainment news items among Malaysian K-pop fans this month, with ticket demand expected to be exceptionally high.

EXO, which debuted under SM Entertainment in 2012, rose to become one of the defining acts of the third-generation K-pop wave. The group produced a string of mega-hits and held the record for multiple music chart records across Asia. Despite members fulfilling mandatory military service obligations and pursuing solo careers in recent years, EXO's collective identity as a group has remained powerful, with a devoted international fanbase that spans Southeast Asia.

The Malaysian EXO-L community — which has maintained an active presence across social media platforms and fan events — has been among the most vocal in calling for the group's return to the country. The six-year gap since their last Malaysian performance has only intensified the anticipation, with fans already mobilising to organise group ticket purchases and fan project activities ahead of the event.

Venue and ticketing details for the Kuala Lumpur concert are expected to be confirmed by the concert promoter in the coming days. Given the scale of anticipated demand, fans have been advised to prepare for a competitive ticketing process, similar to what was experienced during recent concerts by other major K-pop acts in Malaysia this year.

The EXO concert is part of a broader wave of high-profile international acts returning to Malaysia in 2026, reflecting the country's growing status as a must-stop destination for global touring artists. The Malaysian live entertainment industry has bounced back strongly from the post-pandemic period, with promoters reporting robust demand for premium concert experiences across all genres.
