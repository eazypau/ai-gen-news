FIFA World Cup 2026 Group Stage Concludes Today — Key Matches for Malaysian Fans

The FIFA World Cup 2026 group stage reaches its conclusion on June 28, with a full slate of simultaneous final-round fixtures determining which 32 of the 48 competing nations advance to the round of 32. For Malaysian fans, today's key matches include Panama vs England and Croatia vs Ghana, both scheduled in the early hours of Malaysian Morning Time, as well as Colombia vs Portugal and Congo DR vs Uzbekistan kicking off at 7:30am MYT.

This year's World Cup, hosted across Canada, Mexico and the United States, marks the first edition with an expanded 48-team format, resulting in a record 104 matches across the tournament. The expansion has been met with both enthusiasm — more nations competing means broader global representation — and some criticism from those who feel the group stage has featured too many mismatches in the opening rounds.

Malaysian viewers have had to adapt to unusual viewing hours, with most matches scheduled for the Americas timezone falling in the late-night to early-morning window in Malaysia. Unifi TV holds the broadcast rights for all 104 fixtures, with matches available on its premium sports channels and via the Unifi TV streaming app. Sports bars and mamak restaurants across the Klang Valley have reported strong crowds for the bigger games, with the England matches in particular drawing significant viewing numbers.

With the group stage wrapping up today, anticipation is building for the knockout rounds which begin on July 2. Football fans in Malaysia are eagerly discussing the potential bracket matchups as the tournament narrows to its 32 surviving nations. Several shock results in the group stage have upended pre-tournament predictions, with traditional powers facing unexpected early exits.

The FIFA World Cup 2026 final is scheduled for July 19 (July 20 at 3am in Malaysia), to be played at a yet-to-be-confirmed host city stadium. With 11 of the 48 competing teams from Asia, the AFC block has attracted considerable attention, though Malaysia itself did not qualify for the tournament despite a competitive qualifying campaign.
