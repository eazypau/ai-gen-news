BN Pledges "Mature Politics" — No Rival Attacks During Johor Campaign

Barisan Nasional chairman Ahmad Zahid Hamidi has declared that the coalition will adopt a "mature politics" approach throughout the Johor election campaign, specifically instructing party machinery to refrain from launching personal or inflammatory attacks on rival candidates and coalitions. The directive was announced at a campaign briefing and immediately attracted attention from political commentators.

Ahmad Zahid said BN's campaign in Johor will focus on its track record of governance, economic achievements and development delivery in the state under Menteri Besar Onn Hafiz Ghazi's administration. He emphasised that voters are looking for stability and proven leadership rather than political theatrics, particularly in the wake of recent national and global economic uncertainties.

The announcement comes as all three major coalitions — BN, Pakatan Harapan and Perikatan Nasional — officially launch their campaigns following nomination day. While BN holds incumbency advantage in Johor, its majority is expected to be tested more rigorously this time, with PH fielding a strengthened line-up of candidates under Prime Minister Anwar Ibrahim's direction.

Senior BN leaders have also been urged to coordinate messaging more tightly at the national level to prevent conflicting public statements from undermining the campaign in specific seats. Party insiders say Ahmad Zahid is particularly mindful of avoiding the kind of intra-party friction that has complicated past campaigns.

Whether the "mature politics" pledge translates into practice across all 56 BN campaign units remains to be seen, but the tone set by the chairman is being read as a deliberate signal to moderate voters who may be turned off by aggressive electioneering.
