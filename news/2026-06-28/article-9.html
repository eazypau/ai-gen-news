Johor Cementing Its Status as Southeast Asia's Fastest-Growing Data Centre Hub

Johor has solidified its position as Southeast Asia's fastest-growing data centre hub, according to industry analysts and government data released this week. The southern Malaysian state is projected to account for 60% of Malaysia's total data centre capacity by 2030, a staggering concentration that reflects the extraordinary scale of investment flowing into the region from global hyperscalers and cloud providers.

The milestone was further underscored by the successful launch of Malaysia's first Nvidia-powered AI data centre in Johor in October 2025, which has since become a showcase for the region's AI infrastructure capabilities. The facility has attracted significant interest from international technology firms looking to deploy AI workloads closer to Southeast Asian markets.

Industry data indicates that over two-thirds of all data centre capacity currently under construction across Southeast Asia's five main economies — Malaysia, Singapore, Indonesia, Thailand and Vietnam — is committed to facilities in Malaysia, with Johor leading the charge. This concentration is both a reflection of available land, power infrastructure and proximity to the Singapore tech ecosystem.

The growth of Johor as a data centre cluster is inextricably linked to its proximity to Singapore, which has faced land and power constraints that have limited its ability to accommodate the full scale of hyperscaler demand. Johor effectively functions as Singapore's data centre hinterland, benefiting from high-capacity subsea cable connectivity and improving cross-border infrastructure.

Analysts caution that sustaining this growth will require continued investment in electricity generation capacity — particularly renewable energy — and the development of local technical talent. The government's ongoing review of power tariff structures for large commercial users is expected to have significant implications for data centre operators planning future expansions in the state.
