Johor Election Nomination Day Complete — 56 Seats Up for Grabs on July 11

Nomination day for the 2026 Johor state election has concluded successfully, with candidates formally registered for all 56 state constituencies ahead of polling day on July 11. The Election Commission (EC) confirmed that the process proceeded smoothly despite weather forecasts warning of possible rain and thunderstorms in several parts of Johor on the day.

The EC had earlier advised all prospective candidates and their supporters to check their nomination forms carefully and pay deposits early to avoid last-minute complications. Returning officers across the 56 constituencies were stationed at designated centres from morning to receive the necessary documentation.

The Johor state election was triggered after Menteri Besar Onn Hafiz Ghazi announced the dissolution of the state assembly on June 1, 2026. The election comes four years after Barisan Nasional's landslide victory in the 2022 Johor polls, and observers note that the political landscape has shifted considerably since then.

Over 2.7 million registered voters in Johor will head to the polls on July 11 to determine whether the incumbent BN-led state government retains its mandate, or whether opposition coalitions — chiefly Pakatan Harapan and Perikatan Nasional — can secure enough seats to form a new government.

Political analysts predict a closely fought contest in several urban and semi-urban seats, particularly in Iskandar Puteri and Johor Bahru, where multi-cornered fights may influence outcomes. All eyes will be on how the votes split between the three main coalitions across the state's diverse constituencies.
