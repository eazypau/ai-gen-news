Lee Zii Jia Called Up to Malaysia's BWF Thomas Cup Finals Team

Source: Olympics.com / NST Sports 7 June 2026 Sports

Badminton Association of Malaysia (BAM) has included Lee Zii Jia in the national squad for the 2026 BWF Thomas Cup Finals, a decision that has generated considerable discussion in Malaysian badminton circles. Currently ranked world No. 83, the Sarawakian shuttler's inclusion signals that selectors still regard him as capable of delivering on the biggest stage despite a difficult ranking period.

Lee Zii Jia, 27, is one of Malaysia's most naturally gifted badminton players, known for his explosive attacking style and ability to produce remarkable performances against top-ranked opponents on his best days. His career has been marked by flashes of brilliance alongside periods of inconsistency, and his ranking decline in recent months raised questions about his place in the national setup.

BAM's selection committee has chosen to back Lee based on his track record in team competitions, where he has historically performed above his individual ranking. Thomas Cup formats, which involve team tie-based competition, often suit players who can raise their game under collective pressure and team motivation.

The Thomas Cup Finals will see Malaysia facing stiff competition from traditional powerhouses China, Indonesia and Denmark. Malaysia last claimed the Thomas Cup title in 1992, and there is significant national appetite for a revival of that success, with the team expected to field a strong all-around lineup across the five-rubber tie format.

Lee's inclusion will likely see him take on one of the top singles rubber slots, potentially matching up against world top-10 opponents. Coaches have reportedly been working intensively with him on consistency improvements, and BAM has expressed confidence that he can be a key contributor to Malaysia's campaign in the upcoming Finals.
