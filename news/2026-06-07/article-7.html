Unifi TV to Broadcast All 104 FIFA World Cup 2026 Matches in Malaysia

Source: Free Malaysia Today 7 June 2026 Sports

Telekom Malaysia Berhad (TM) has confirmed that its Unifi TV platform will carry live coverage of all 104 matches in the FIFA World Cup 2026, marking a significant shift in the Malaysian sports broadcasting landscape. The deal sees TM taking over broadcast rights from Astro, which had previously held exclusive rights to major international football tournaments in the country.

Subscribers can access the full World Cup package for RM60, while existing Unifi TV customers are eligible for a discounted rate of RM50 for the entire tournament season. The tournament runs from the opening match on June 11 through to the grand final on July 19, spanning over five weeks of football across the expanded 48-team format.

The FIFA World Cup 2026 is hosted jointly by the United States, Canada and Mexico, making it the first World Cup to be staged across three countries. The expanded format, which increased participating teams from 32 to 48, means the tournament features significantly more matches than previous editions, giving Malaysian football fans an unprecedented volume of live content.

TM has invested in studio production facilities and a commentary team to provide localised Bahasa Malaysia and English coverage throughout the tournament. Select high-profile matches will also be available through the Unifi TV mobile application, allowing fans to watch on smartphones and tablets while away from home.

The announcement has been welcomed by Malaysian football fans, with many expressing relief at having an affordable alternative to previous pay-per-view arrangements. Sports analysts predict a surge in Unifi TV subscriptions ahead of the opening kickoff, as the World Cup consistently ranks among the most-watched sporting events in Malaysia.
