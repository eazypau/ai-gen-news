Cosmetics Overtake Agricultural Goods as Malaysia's Top Consumer Export

Source: The Edge Malaysia 7 June 2026 Business

Malaysian cosmetics and personal care products have officially surpassed agricultural and fishery goods to claim the top spot among the country's five major consumer goods export categories, according to the latest trade data released by the Ministry of Investment, Trade and Industry (MITI). The milestone reflects a remarkable transformation in Malaysia's consumer goods manufacturing sector over the past decade.

Export values for cosmetics and personal care products reached record highs in the first quarter of 2026, driven by surging demand from key markets including China, the Middle East, ASEAN neighbours and the United Kingdom. Industry analysts attribute the growth to the increasing global recognition of Malaysian halal-certified cosmetics brands, which enjoy a significant competitive advantage in the world's rapidly expanding halal economy.

Several Malaysian cosmetics companies have successfully expanded from domestic manufacturing into global brand ownership in recent years. Brands such as SimplySiti, Bioessence and several newer halal-certified cosmetic startups have built substantial export pipelines, particularly into the GCC market where halal beauty products command premium pricing and consumer loyalty.

The Malaysia External Trade Development Corporation (MATRADE) has played a key role in facilitating market access for Malaysian cosmetics exporters through trade missions, international beauty exhibitions and product certification support. MATRADE's director-general noted that the cosmetics sector's rise reflects both the quality of Malaysian manufacturing and the effectiveness of targeted export promotion strategies.

Looking ahead, the industry is expected to further strengthen its export position as Malaysia continues to invest in cosmetics research and development, green formulation capabilities and digital marketing channels targeting international consumers. The government's Halal Industry Master Plan supports cosmetics as a priority growth area, with ambitious targets for doubling export revenues by 2030.
