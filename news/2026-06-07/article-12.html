Malaysia Mega Sale 2026 to Kick Off June 15 with Nationwide Promotions

Source: Buro 24/7 Malaysia 7 June 2026 Lifestyle

Malaysia's iconic Mega Sale Carnival is set to return with a bang on June 15, 2026, running through to July 31 in what Tourism, Arts and Culture Minister Datuk Seri Tiong King Sing has described as the biggest edition yet. The nationwide shopping extravaganza will see thousands of retail outlets, shopping malls and e-commerce platforms participating with discounts ranging from 20% to over 70% on a broad range of categories.

The Mega Sale coincides with the Malaysia Festival Kuala Lumpur 2026, a signature government-backed event designed to attract international tourists, particularly from the Gulf Cooperation Council (GCC) region, where Malaysia has been aggressively marketing itself as a premium shopping, dining and leisure destination. The combined campaign is expected to generate significant tourism receipts and boost retail GDP during the seven-week period.

Major shopping complexes including Pavilion Kuala Lumpur, Suria KLCC, Mid Valley Megamall, Sunway Pyramid and 1 Utama have announced dedicated Mega Sale programming including flash sales, lucky draws, celebrity appearances and exclusive pop-up events. E-commerce giants Shopee and Lazada Malaysia have also confirmed they will participate with dedicated Mega Sale sales pages and exclusive promotional codes.

The tourism ministry has projected an increase of up to 30% in footfall at major shopping destinations compared to the same period last year, buoyed by a combination of domestic consumer confidence and strong inbound tourist arrivals. Malaysia's retail sector, which has rebounded strongly from pandemic-era disruptions, is expected to record one of its best mid-year performance periods in this edition of the Mega Sale.

Additionally, new digital payment incentives will be offered during the Mega Sale period, with participating banks providing cashback and reward points for transactions made via the DuitNow QR system and selected credit card partnerships. Shoppers are encouraged to check their banks' Mega Sale promotional pages for personalised offers and spending challenges.
