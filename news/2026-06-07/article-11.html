EXO Returns to Kuala Lumpur for First Concert in Six Years

Source: Hype Malaysia 7 June 2026 Entertainment

Legendary K-pop group EXO is set to make a highly anticipated return to Kuala Lumpur this June, marking their first concert performance in Malaysia in six years. The announcement has triggered an outpouring of excitement from the group's devoted Malaysian fanbase, known as EXO-Ls, who have long awaited the group's return following an extended hiatus from international touring.

EXO, who debuted under SM Entertainment in 2012, rose to become one of the most commercially successful K-pop acts of the 2010s, with landmark albums and a string of sold-out world tours. Their last Malaysian appearance was a high-energy arena show in 2020 before global events disrupted touring schedules. The upcoming Kuala Lumpur show marks a significant milestone in the group's renewed activity on the international stage.

Ticketing details are expected to be announced imminently, with industry insiders predicting the venue — rumoured to be Axiata Arena — will sell out within hours of tickets going on sale. Fan clubs have already begun coordinating bulk purchasing strategies and organising pre-concert events for Malaysian EXO-Ls ahead of the confirmed date.

The return of EXO coincides with a broader resurgence of major K-pop tours in Malaysia, which has firmly established itself as one of Southeast Asia's most active live music markets. BTS has also announced a December 2026 concert at the same venue, making 2026 an exceptional year for K-pop fans in the country.

Beyond K-pop, Kuala Lumpur's performing arts calendar in June is rich with local talent as well, with the STEP Theatre Festival 2026 presenting three powerful productions — The Maids, Inside Voices and Peristiwa di Zoo — staged over three weekends from June 5 through June 21 at various city venues.
