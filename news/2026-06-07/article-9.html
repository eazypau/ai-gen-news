ENERtec Asia 2026 Opens at KLCC, Attracting 12,000 Energy Professionals

Source: The Edge Malaysia 7 June 2026 Technology

ENERtec Asia 2026, widely regarded as Asia's most important energy technology exhibition, has officially opened at the Kuala Lumpur Convention Centre (KLCC), drawing more than 12,000 industry professionals from 60 participating countries. The event features over 1,000 exhibiting brands spread across seven exhibition halls, covering the full spectrum of energy innovation from renewables and storage to smart grid technology and carbon capture solutions.

Malaysia's Minister of Energy Transition and Water Transformation, Datuk Seri Fadillah Yusof, officiated the opening ceremony, emphasising the country's ambition to become the region's leading hub for clean energy innovation and investment. He noted that Malaysia's strategic position as a transit economy with strong manufacturing infrastructure makes it ideally placed to catalyse the region's energy transition.

Highlights of this year's exhibition include live demonstrations of next-generation solar panel technology achieving record efficiency rates, hydrogen fuel cell systems for industrial applications, and AI-driven energy management platforms capable of optimising power distribution across entire city grids. Several major international energy firms have used ENERtec Asia as the platform to announce significant investments in Malaysian operations.

The exhibition coincides with growing global interest in Malaysia as a hub for energy technology, with the country having attracted substantial foreign direct investment in solar manufacturing, data centre power infrastructure and electric vehicle supply chain components in recent years. Malaysia's renewable energy capacity targets under the National Energy Policy have made it a magnet for regional energy sector players.

ENERtec Asia runs for four days and will include a high-level conference programme featuring panel discussions and keynote addresses from global energy leaders, policymakers and technology innovators. The event is expected to facilitate deal-making worth billions of ringgit in energy technology contracts and partnership agreements.
