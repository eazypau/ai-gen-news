Malaysia Doubles Employment Pass Salary Threshold to RM20,000

Source: The Star 7 June 2026 Business

Malaysia implemented a sweeping revision of its Employment Pass (EP) salary thresholds effective June 1, 2026, in a move designed to attract higher-skilled foreign professionals while protecting the domestic labour market. The most significant change sees the Category I minimum monthly salary doubled from RM10,000 to RM20,000, a substantial jump that signals a clear policy shift toward quality-over-quantity in foreign talent recruitment.

Under the revised framework, Category II Employment Pass holders must now earn between RM10,000 and RM19,999 per month, up from the previous RM5,000–RM9,999 range. Category III, which covers semi-skilled positions, now requires a minimum of RM5,000 per month, compared to the previous RM3,000–RM4,999 threshold. For the manufacturing sector, a specific floor of RM7,000 has been introduced to reflect the higher technical demands of the industry.

The Human Resources Ministry stated that the revised thresholds were developed through extensive consultation with industry associations, employers and economists. The ministry emphasised that the changes are intended to ensure that Employment Passes are reserved for roles that genuinely cannot be filled by local talent, rather than for positions where Malaysians could competitively fill the vacancy at lower wages.

Reactions from the business community have been mixed. Technology companies and multinational corporations have broadly welcomed the changes, arguing that the higher thresholds more accurately reflect the salary levels at which they recruit senior engineers, data scientists and finance professionals anyway. However, some mid-size businesses have expressed concern that the revisions will complicate their ability to hire certain specialist roles.

The new policy also introduces streamlined processing timelines for high-earning applicants and companies with significant FDI commitments, as part of efforts to make Malaysia an attractive destination for top global talent in key growth sectors including artificial intelligence, semiconductor engineering and financial services.
