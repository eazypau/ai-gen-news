Malaysia and Thailand to Deepen Sungai Golok Basin Cooperation

Source: Bernama 7 June 2026 Politics

Malaysia and Thailand have reaffirmed their commitment to deepening bilateral cooperation on the Sungai Golok Basin development project, with both nations agreeing to accelerate joint initiatives focused on flood control, water resource management and ecological restoration. The announcement was made following a high-level working meeting between officials from both countries' water and natural resources ministries.

The Sungai Golok River, which forms part of the natural border between Malaysia's Kelantan state and Thailand's Narathiwat Province, has historically been a source of both economic opportunity and environmental challenge for communities on both banks. Seasonal flooding has repeatedly caused significant damage to villages, agricultural land and infrastructure in the border region.

Under the renewed cooperation framework, both governments will jointly fund and implement a series of flood mitigation infrastructure projects, including the construction of retention ponds, river dredging programmes and the installation of early warning sensor networks. Ecosystem restoration efforts will focus on replanting riparian vegetation that has been lost to decades of agricultural encroachment.

Water security is a central pillar of the agreement, particularly given increasing concerns about climate-driven variability in rainfall patterns affecting the river's flow. Both nations will share hydrological data in real time through a bilateral monitoring system to improve coordinated responses to flood and drought events.

Separately, Bangkok has signalled its readiness to escalate a separate trade dispute involving Malaysia's temporary suspension of five categories of Thai shrimp imports to WTO and ASEAN dispute resolution forums if current bilateral technical talks do not yield a satisfactory resolution. Malaysian agriculture officials have indicated they remain open to continued dialogue before any formal escalation occurs.
