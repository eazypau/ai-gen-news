MetMalaysia Issues Thunderstorm and Heavy Rain Warning for Three States

Source: MalaysiaNow 7 June 2026 Environment

The Malaysian Meteorological Department (MetMalaysia) has issued an official weather alert warning of thunderstorms, heavy rainfall and strong gusty winds across three Malaysian states, with the warning in effect until 3pm on Sunday, June 7. The department urged residents in the affected areas to exercise caution and remain indoors where possible during the severe weather period.

The warning covers areas prone to flash flooding, and MetMalaysia has advised residents living near low-lying regions and riverbanks to monitor water levels closely. Local disaster management authorities have been placed on standby, with civil defence units ready to respond to any emergency situations arising from the adverse weather.

Motorists are advised to exercise extra care on roads during heavy downpours, as reduced visibility and slippery surfaces significantly increase the risk of accidents. The department also cautioned against seeking shelter under trees or in open fields during thunderstorm activity, given the elevated risk of lightning strikes.

MetMalaysia noted that the weather pattern is associated with the Southwest Monsoon transition season, which typically brings intermittent heavy rainfall events to Peninsular Malaysia during this period. Forecasters are monitoring the situation and may extend warnings or issue new alerts if conditions deteriorate further.

Members of the public are encouraged to check MetMalaysia's official website and mobile app for real-time weather updates and to register with the MyDisaster mobile application to receive localised alerts. Emergency contacts for the Civil Defence Department and State Disaster Management Committees have been widely shared through official social media channels.
