Form Three Student Stabbed at Banting School; Victim Stable After Emergency Treatment

A Form Three student is recovering after suffering 16 stab wounds, including a serious lung injury, following an attack at a secondary school in Banting, Kuala Langat, Selangor. The victim received emergency surgery at Hospital Banting and is now in stable condition.

A serious incident of school violence unfolded at a secondary school in Banting, Kuala Langat, when a Form Three student was attacked and stabbed multiple times by another individual on the school premises. The Selangor Health Department confirmed on July 6 that the victim sustained 16 stab wounds and suffered a lung injury as a result of the assault. Emergency services were alerted and the student was rapidly transported to Hospital Banting, where medical staff performed emergency treatment to address the lung injury.

As of the latest update from the Selangor Health Department, the victim's condition is described as stable, though they remain under medical observation. Hospital Banting's medical team is monitoring the patient's recovery and managing post-surgical care for the lung injury, which requires careful monitoring to prevent complications.

The incident has prompted calls from parents, teachers, and community groups in the Banting area for a review of school security protocols. Questions have been raised about how a bladed weapon was able to be brought onto school grounds, and the Education Ministry has been urged to conduct an urgent audit of security arrangements at secondary schools across the state.

Police investigations are ongoing, and the Royal Malaysia Police (PDRM) Kuala Langat District have confirmed that a suspect has been identified and is assisting with the investigation. The motive behind the attack has not yet been made public, and authorities have cautioned against speculation while the inquiry is active.

The incident is the second school violence case to attract national attention in recent weeks, raising broader concerns about student safety and the social environment in Malaysian secondary schools. Education Minister officials have stated that the ministry takes all incidents of school violence seriously and will work with the relevant state education departments and police to prevent recurrence.
