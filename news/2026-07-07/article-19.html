CTOS Digital Appoints Creador Founder Brahmal Vasudevan as New Chairman

CTOS Digital Berhad has appointed Brahmal Vasudevan, the founder and CEO of pan-Asian private equity firm Creador, as its new executive chairman effective July 1, 2026 — a move signalling strategic ambitions for the credit reporting giant across Southeast Asia.

CTOS Digital Berhad, Malaysia's largest and most widely used credit reporting and risk management services company, has announced a significant leadership change with the appointment of Brahmal Vasudevan as its new executive chairman, taking effect from July 1, 2026. Vasudevan is the founder and chief executive officer of Creador, a pan-Asian private equity firm with a track record of investments across Malaysia, India, Sri Lanka, Bangladesh, and Vietnam.

Brahmal Vasudevan is one of Southeast Asia's most recognisable private equity figures, known for backing consumer, financial services, and technology businesses across emerging markets. His appointment to CTOS Digital's board as chairman comes at a strategically significant time: the company is actively pursuing regional expansion beyond Malaysia, with a particular focus on becoming a leading credit data and risk analytics provider across Southeast Asia.

CTOS Digital's core business — operating Malaysia's largest credit reporting ecosystem, used by banks, financial institutions, telcos, and individuals to assess creditworthiness — provides a strong domestic base. However, the company has set its sights on replicating its Malaysian model in other regional markets where credit infrastructure remains underdeveloped. Vasudevan's private equity network and his experience in scaling financial services businesses across multiple markets makes him a well-suited strategic leader for this expansion phase.

The appointment follows a period of strong growth for CTOS Digital, driven in part by the rapid growth of Malaysia's digital lending and BNPL (buy now, pay later) segments, which have significantly increased demand for real-time credit data services. The company's platform now serves tens of millions of individual and commercial credit records.

Industry observers say the appointment reflects CTOS Digital's ambition to position itself as a regional fintech infrastructure player rather than simply a Malaysian credit bureau. Financial services analysts have generally responded positively to the leadership change, viewing Vasudevan's track record as additive to the company's growth strategy.
