Sushiro Malaysia Expands with Second Branch at 1 Utama Shopping Centre

Japanese conveyor-belt sushi chain Sushiro is opening its second Malaysia outlet at 1 Utama Shopping Centre in Petaling Jaya, responding to overwhelming demand that has kept queues long at its first branch since launch.

Sushiro Malaysia is expanding its local footprint with the opening of a second restaurant at 1 Utama Shopping Centre in Petaling Jaya, one of Malaysia's largest and most-visited shopping malls. The new branch arrives approximately one year after Sushiro's Malaysian debut, which generated enormous public interest and near-constant queues that at times stretched beyond two hours on weekends.

Sushiro is Japan's largest conveyor-belt sushi chain by revenue and store count, known for its competitive pricing, consistent quality, and rotating menu of seasonal specials. Its Malaysian debut was one of the most anticipated restaurant openings in recent memory, drawing comparisons to the demand frenzy that accompanied other major Japanese F&B brands entering the local market.

The 1 Utama location has been selected to serve the dense residential and commercial catchment area of Petaling Jaya, Damansara, and surrounding townships — providing an alternative for fans in the Klang Valley's northern corridor who previously had to travel further for their Sushiro fix. The outlet will feature Sushiro's standard menu including classic nigiri, rolls, hot dishes, and desserts, as well as Malaysia-specific seasonal offerings.

Food and beverage observers note that the Japanese casual dining segment remains one of the most resilient categories in Malaysia's restaurant market despite broader consumer spending caution. The success of Sushiro's first Malaysian outlet has reportedly attracted attention from several other Japanese restaurant chains that are now evaluating market entry, potentially signalling a new wave of Japanese F&B expansion in Malaysia.

Sushiro Malaysia has not yet confirmed the official opening date for the 1 Utama branch, but social media posts from the brand suggest a launch is imminent, with staff training and fit-out work nearing completion. Fans are already sharing excited posts and planning their first visits.
