realme 16 5G Mid-Ranger Launches in Malaysia at RM1,599, Promo at RM1,499

Technology Technave / realme Malaysia 19 June 2026

realme Malaysia has officially launched the realme 16 5G, positioning it as a compelling mid-range offering in an increasingly competitive smartphone segment. Priced at RM1,599, the device was available from June 13, 2026, with an attractive first-sale promotional price of RM1,499 for early buyers — a RM100 discount that generated strong opening-day traffic both online and at retail outlets.

The realme 16 5G is built around a capable mid-range chipset designed to deliver smooth performance for everyday tasks, mobile gaming, and multimedia consumption. Key highlights include a large display with high refresh rate support, an expansive battery with fast charging capability, and a multi-lens rear camera system equipped with a primary sensor optimised for low-light performance. The device also supports the 5G connectivity that is becoming increasingly expected at this price tier in the Malaysian market.

realme has been one of the more aggressive smartphone brands in the Malaysian market, consistently delivering devices that punch above their price class in terms of specifications. The 16 5G continues this tradition, targeting consumers who want a capable 5G-ready device without stretching into premium pricing territory occupied by flagship offerings from Samsung, Apple, and Xiaomi's upper-tier lineup.

The launch comes amid a broader expansion in 5G device availability in Malaysia, as Telekom Malaysia (Unifi), Maxis, Celcom-Digi, and U Mobile continue expanding their 5G coverage footprints across the Klang Valley, Penang, Johor Bahru, and other major cities. The growing network coverage is increasing the practical value proposition of 5G-capable devices for everyday consumers.

realme Malaysia confirmed the device will be available through its official online store, major e-commerce platforms including Lazada and Shopee, and authorised retail partners nationwide. The company indicated that the promotional RM1,499 price was available for a limited early-adoption window, reverting to the standard RM1,599 retail price thereafter.
