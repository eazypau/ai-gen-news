RTM and Unifi TV to Broadcast All 104 FIFA World Cup 2026 Matches Free for Malaysians

Sports Malay Mail 19 June 2026

Communications Minister Fahmi Fadzil confirmed on May 13 that all 104 matches of the FIFA World Cup 2026 will be available for Malaysian viewers at no cost, through platforms operated by Radio Television Malaysia (RTM) and Unifi TV. This announcement marked a historic shift in how the World Cup is broadcast in Malaysia, ending a two-decade run where pay-TV broadcaster Astro held exclusive rights to the tournament.

Astro's failure to secure the primary broadcasting rights for this edition meant that millions of Malaysians who previously relied on the satellite broadcaster were left without a familiar viewing platform. The government-linked RTM and Telekom Malaysia's Unifi TV stepped in to fill the gap, ensuring that free-to-air and broadband subscribers alike can enjoy the full breadth of the tournament's 104 matches in the expanded 48-team format.

Matches are accessible via RTM's television channels — including TV1, TV2, and TV Okey — as well as through RTMKlik, the broadcaster's free streaming application available on smartphones, tablets, and smart TVs. Unifi TV subscribers additionally have access through their existing broadband packages, with the matches available on dedicated sports channels within the Unifi TV platform.

Malaysian football fans have had to adjust to an unusual schedule given the time difference with North America, where the tournament is co-hosted by the United States, Canada, and Mexico. Most group-stage matches kick off between midnight and noon Malaysian time, with some fixtures scheduled as early as 3am. Despite the challenging hours, bars, mamak stalls, and community viewing events have reported strong turnout for key matches.

The FIFA World Cup 2026 kicked off on June 12 with the opening ceremony and first match, and Malaysia — which has never qualified for the tournament — is cheering as neutral supporters for several teams with strong followings in the country, including Brazil, Argentina, England, and several Asian nations that made it through the AFC qualification rounds.
