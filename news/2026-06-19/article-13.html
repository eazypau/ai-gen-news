PETRONAS Syntium Launches Next-Gen Engine Oil Meeting New API SQ and ILSAC GF-7 Standards

Business Sin Chew / PETRONAS Lubricants International 19 June 2026

PETRONAS Lubricants International (PLI) officially introduced the next generation of its all-new PETRONAS Syntium engine oil on June 12, 2026. The upgraded product line features formulations specifically engineered to meet the latest API SQ and ILSAC GF-7 industry standards, offering enhanced engine protection and improved fuel efficiency for modern vehicles across a range of performance categories.

The launch marks a significant milestone for PLI, which has positioned PETRONAS Syntium as a flagship product in its global lubricants portfolio. The new API SQ and ILSAC GF-7 standards, the most demanding certifications in the industry to date, require formulations that deliver measurably superior engine cleanliness, reduced wear protection, and better fuel economy compared to previous-generation products — a standard that PLI says its new Syntium lineup meets and exceeds.

PLI's Chief Executive Officer described the launch as a reflection of the company's ongoing commitment to technological innovation and sustainability, noting that the new Syntium formulations also deliver improved CO2 emissions reductions through enhanced fuel efficiency. The product is designed to serve a wide range of modern passenger vehicles, including those equipped with turbocharged engines, hybrid drivetrains, and advanced direct injection systems.

PETRONAS Syntium is sold across more than 100 countries globally and serves as a technical partner to several Formula 1 and motorsport programmes, giving the brand significant engineering credibility in the performance automotive sector. The new product will be available at PETRONAS stations, authorised service centres, and auto parts retailers across Malaysia and globally.

The launch coincides with growing consumer awareness of the role engine oil quality plays in vehicle longevity and efficiency. Automotive experts noted that the transition to API SQ-compliant oils is expected to accelerate as new vehicle models requiring the higher specification lubricant enter the Malaysian market in greater volumes over the next two to three years.
