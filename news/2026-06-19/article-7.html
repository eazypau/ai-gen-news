Police Nab 58 in World Cup Gambling Crackdown Under Op Soga XI

Crime The Sun Daily 19 June 2026

The Royal Malaysia Police (PDRM) has arrested 58 individuals suspected of involvement in unlicensed sports betting and online gambling syndicates operating in connection with the ongoing FIFA World Cup 2026 tournament. The suspects were detained under Op Soga XI, a nationwide enforcement operation launched on June 11 that will run until July 19, 2026.

During the operation, law enforcement conducted 52 raids across the country, seizing RM25,684.65 in cash and uncovering betting credit values totalling RM488,582.47. The suspects range in age and background, with investigators reporting that many of the syndicates were leveraging social media platforms to advertise their betting services and recruit punters under the guise of legitimate sports analysis pages.

Op Soga XI is being conducted in collaboration with the Malaysian Communications and Multimedia Commission (MCMC), which has been tasked with monitoring and flagging gambling promotions and illicit betting advertisements across digital platforms, social media, and messaging applications. The MCMC's involvement reflects the increasingly online nature of illegal gambling operations in Malaysia, which have grown significantly during major sporting events.

PDRM reminded the public that participation in unlicensed betting activities is a criminal offence under the Common Gaming Houses Act 1953 and the Betting Act 1953, with offenders liable to fines and imprisonment upon conviction. The police also encouraged members of the public to report suspected gambling activities via the PDRM hotline or through official channels.

The FIFA World Cup 2026, being held across the United States, Canada, and Mexico with an expanded 48-team format, has generated extraordinary interest among Malaysian football fans, with matches broadcast live on RTM and Unifi TV. Police noted that major international sporting events historically trigger spikes in illegal gambling activity, and op Soga XI represents an intensified effort to curb this trend during the tournament period.
