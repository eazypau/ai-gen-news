Malaysia Data Centre Market to Double by 2031, Hitting USD 11.4 Billion

Technology Research & Markets / Bernama 19 June 2026

Malaysia's data centre market, valued at USD 6.14 billion in 2025, is projected to reach USD 11.4 billion by 2031, growing at a compound annual growth rate of 10.8%, according to a new market intelligence report. The remarkable expansion is being driven by surging artificial intelligence workloads, the rapid growth of cloud computing adoption across the region, and Malaysia's emergence as a strategic digital infrastructure hub in Southeast Asia.

Johor has cemented its position as a rising hyperscale data centre cluster, functioning as a strategic extension of Singapore's digital infrastructure and absorbing significant spillover demand that Singapore itself can no longer accommodate due to land and power constraints. Billions of dollars in cloud, AI, and hyperscale investments have flowed into Johor over the past three years, with major global players including Alibaba Cloud, Microsoft, Google, and Amazon Web Services all having established or committed to significant facilities in the state.

Cyberjaya remains Malaysia's leading domestic hub for data centre expansion, hosting more than 22 existing facilities with nine more under development. The Klang Valley corridor continues to attract both domestic and international operators, benefiting from established fibre connectivity, a growing pool of qualified engineers, and proximity to Malaysia's financial and business centre in Kuala Lumpur.

The Johor-Singapore Special Economic Zone (JS-SEZ), now operational, is expected to catalyse further investment by offering streamlined regulatory approvals and competitive incentives for data centre operators choosing to establish on the Malaysian side of the causeway. Industry experts noted that data centre construction-related activity is now a meaningful contributor to Malaysia's overall construction sector growth.

The government's commitment to digital economy development — targeting a 27% contribution to GDP by 2026 under the Malaysia Digital Economy Blueprint — has provided critical policy support for the sector's expansion. Analysts cautioned, however, that power supply constraints and the availability of skilled data centre operations talent will need to be addressed proactively to sustain the market's projected growth trajectory.
