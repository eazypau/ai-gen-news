EXO Returns to Kuala Lumpur After Six-Year Absence, EXO-Ls Go Wild

Entertainment Hype Malaysia 19 June 2026

Legendary K-pop boy group EXO is set to make their highly anticipated return to Kuala Lumpur this June, marking their first concert appearance in Malaysia in six years. The announcement has sent EXO-Ls — the fandom's official name — into a frenzy of excitement on social media, with Malaysian fans expressing that the wait has been well worth it as the group returns with renewed energy following a period of military service completions among its members.

EXO debuted under SM Entertainment in 2012 and quickly rose to become one of the defining acts of the Hallyu Wave's golden era. Known for their powerful choreography, exceptional vocal harmonies, and compelling stage presence, the group accumulated a devoted global following including a particularly passionate fan base in Malaysia. Their previous Kuala Lumpur performance drew a sell-out crowd and is still fondly remembered by Malaysian fans.

The group's return comes as the K-pop industry experiences renewed global momentum in 2026, with several major acts completing their mandatory military service schedules and returning to the spotlight. EXO's comeback is seen as one of the most eagerly awaited re-entries into the live performance circuit, with concerts across Asia confirmed as part of their return world tour.

Concert promoters in Malaysia reported that ticket demand was exceptionally high, with waiting queues in the thousands forming online within minutes of ticket release. Several ticket categories sold out rapidly, prompting organisers to explore options for additional standing sections to accommodate the overwhelming demand from fans who missed out during the initial sale window.

Beyond EXO, Malaysia's concert calendar for 2026 is shaping up to be one of the most vibrant in recent years, with My Chemical Romance and LANY also confirmed for local performances. The resurgence of international acts visiting Malaysia is seen as a positive indicator of the country's appeal as a major live entertainment destination in Southeast Asia, aided by improved venue infrastructure and streamlined event permit processes.
