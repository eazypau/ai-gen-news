Singer 2026 Malaysia Reveals Six Debut Artists Including Jaclyn Victor

Entertainment World of Buzz / Astro 19 June 2026

Astro has officially unveiled the six Malaysian artists set to debut on Singer 2026 – Malaysia, the highly anticipated local edition of the popular Chinese music competition franchise. The lineup features veteran powerhouse Jaclyn Victor, widely regarded as one of Malaysia's greatest vocal talents and a beloved icon since her Akademi Fantasia days, alongside a diverse mix of established and rising performers.

Joining Jaclyn Victor are Priscilla Abby, Vanessa Reynauld, Geraldine Gan, Uriah See, and Nicole Lai — a carefully assembled group of six Malaysian artistes who represent a broad range of musical genres, vocal styles, and performance strengths. Priscilla Abby brings soulful pop sensibility, while Vanessa Reynauld is known for her theatrical presence and commanding stage delivery. Geraldine Gan, Uriah See, and Nicole Lai add diversity and freshness to the cohort.

Singer 2026 – Malaysia is an adaptation of the massively popular Chinese television singing competition Singer (originally known as I Am a Singer), produced under licence by Astro. The format pits established professional singers against one another in a series of live performance rounds judged by a public voting system, emphasising vocal artistry over popularity alone. The Malaysian edition has been adapted to celebrate local musical talent in both Bahasa Malaysia, English, Mandarin, and other languages.

The announcement has generated significant excitement on social media, with fans expressing enthusiasm about seeing Jaclyn Victor in particular compete in the demanding live format. Jaclyn Victor is best remembered for her breathtaking performance at the 2004 Akademi Fantasia and has since established herself as a sought-after concert and theatre performer with exceptional vocal range.

Astro confirmed that Singer 2026 – Malaysia will premiere in the coming weeks, with broadcast details and the full competition schedule to be announced shortly. The show is expected to draw substantial viewership given the strong recognition of its participants and the growing domestic appetite for high-quality locally produced music content.
