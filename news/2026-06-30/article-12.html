Indonesian Patients Drive 23% Growth in Malaysia's Medical Tourism Revenue

Indonesia has once again proven itself as the primary engine of Malaysia's medical tourism sector, with over 970,000 Indonesian healthcare travellers visiting the country in 2025 and generating RM2.2 billion in revenue — representing a 23% increase in both patient arrivals and revenue compared to 2024. The figures were highlighted at the Malaysia Fair 2026, held in South Jakarta, which showcased Malaysia's healthcare, tourism, and education offerings to Indonesian audiences.

Popular treatment areas among Indonesian patients include health screening, gastroenterology, cancer treatment, endocrinology, cardiology, and orthopaedic surgery. Many patients choose Malaysia over alternatives in Singapore and Thailand due to a combination of geographic proximity, cultural and linguistic familiarity, significantly lower costs, and the availability of world-class specialists at major hospitals in Kuala Lumpur, Penang, and Johor Bahru.

Malaysia Healthcare Travel Council (MHTC) brand ambassador Datuk Seri Siti Nurhaliza, speaking at the Malaysia Fair 2026 launch, highlighted the psychological dimension of healthcare travel — noting that Indonesian patients feel at ease in Malaysian hospitals because of shared cultural values and the presence of Malay-speaking medical staff who understand their concerns and needs.

Selangor has emerged as a growing hub for medical tourists, particularly for patients from Jakarta. AirAsia and Malaysia Airlines both reported booking increases for health-travel routes between Indonesian cities and Kuala Lumpur, Penang, and Johor. Hospitals affiliated with the Malaysia Year of Medical Tourism 2026 initiative are offering bundled health screening packages to attract first-time visitors.

Beyond Indonesia, Malaysia is also attracting patients from China, Bangladesh, and India, with combined international patient arrivals exceeding one million annually. The industry targets RM12 billion in medical tourism revenue by 2030 as Malaysia positions itself as the premier global destination for affordable, high-quality healthcare travel.
