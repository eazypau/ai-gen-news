PM Anwar Launches New Malaysian Passport With 94 Security Features

Prime Minister Datuk Seri Anwar Ibrahim officially launched Malaysia's new generation international passport at the Parliament lobby on June 30, 2026, marking a major upgrade to one of the country's most important identity documents. The new passport carries 94 security features — nearly double the 49 features of its predecessor.

Among the upgraded security elements are advanced holographic elements, ultraviolet (UV) printing, hidden visual features, sophisticated watermarks, and several specialised forensic security components that are designed to make the document significantly harder to forge. The Home Minister noted that Malaysia's passport is already ranked among the world's most powerful travel documents and this update further cements its prestige.

The new passport rollout had faced a brief delay after the initial June 1, 2026 target was postponed by the Malaysian Immigration Department, which cited the need for additional quality assurance checks. The June 30 launch also coincides with Malaysia's preparations ahead of upcoming major international events and the country's increased focus on digitalising government services.

Citizens renewing or applying for new passports from July 2026 onwards will automatically receive the upgraded version. Existing passport holders do not need to renew immediately — their current passports remain valid until the stated expiry date. The government assured that all international counterpart agencies have been notified of the design and feature changes.

The launch is part of the broader Madani Government's push to modernise identity documents, including a new upgraded MyKad with enhanced security features also announced earlier this year. Together, the two initiatives aim to reduce identity fraud and strengthen Malaysia's national security infrastructure.
