FIFA World Cup 2026: Round of 32 Results as Malaysia Watches Through the Night

The 2026 FIFA World Cup — the first edition to feature 48 teams — has entered its knockout stage, delivering drama and upsets that have kept Malaysian fans glued to their screens through the early hours of the morning. The Round of 32 produced two standout results: Paraguay stunned four-time champions Germany on penalties, while Brazil edged Japan in a heart-stopping stoppage-time winner, setting up a blockbuster Round of 16 fixture against Paraguay.

For Malaysian football fans, watching the expanded World Cup presents both a joy and a logistical challenge. With all 104 matches taking place across 16 host cities in the United States, Canada, and Mexico, most games kick off between midnight and noon Malaysian time (MYT). The early morning slot has become a social ritual, with mamak restaurants and sports bars in Kuala Lumpur, Penang, and Johor Bahru reporting record patronage since the group stage began.

Malaysians can watch all matches live through RTM's channels and its RTMKlik streaming platform, as well as Unifi TV, which secured comprehensive broadcast rights for the tournament. Several telecom operators have offered bundled World Cup viewing packages, with streaming data consumption reportedly surging to record highs during peak match windows.

The Round of 16 bracket is now set, with eight matches scheduled over four days from late June to early July. Anticipated fixtures include defending champions Argentina against Colombia, and an all-European clash between Spain and France. Malaysian fans are particularly watching the fortunes of South Korea and Japan — both traditional fan favourites in the region — as Asia's two remaining representatives in the knockout stages.

The Football Association of Malaysia (FAM) has used the World Cup's popularity to promote the 2027 ASEAN Cup, which Malaysia is set to co-host. FAM president Datuk Hamidin Mohd Amin expressed hope that the excitement generated by the World Cup would translate into grassroots football development and stronger attendance at local league matches in the second half of 2026.
