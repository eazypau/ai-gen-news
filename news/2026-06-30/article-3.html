BUDI Diesel Subsidy Goes Nationwide from July 1 at RM2.10 Per Litre

Malaysia's BUDI Madani Diesel (BUDI Diesel) scheme will launch nationwide on July 1, 2026, enabling eligible private diesel vehicle owners to purchase subsidised B10 or B15 diesel blends at RM2.10 per litre using their MyKad. The scheme, which had a soft-launch early access period in Peninsular Malaysia from June 27 at RM2.15 per litre, will standardise to the lower rate for all states including Sabah and Sarawak from the first day of July.

To qualify, a Malaysian citizen must possess a valid MyKad, a valid driving licence, and own a privately-registered diesel vehicle with current road tax. The 200-litre monthly quota is shared between BUDI Diesel and the existing BUDI Madani RON95 scheme, meaning owners of both petrol and diesel vehicles must manage their combined consumption within this cap.

Owners of pick-up trucks and jeeps — both privately-owned and company-owned — may apply for an additional 100 litres per month, on top of the standard 200-litre shared allocation. The Malaysian Automotive Association (MAA) welcomed the expanded eligibility, though it expressed hope that diesel-engine MPV owners would also receive an additional quota in a future revision.

The rollout replaces the previous blanket diesel subsidy mechanism, which was criticised for benefiting commercial users and wealthier vehicle owners disproportionately. The new targeted approach mirrors the success of the earlier RON95 subsidy rationalisation and represents a key component of the Madani Government's fuel subsidy reform strategy.

Motorists can access subsidised diesel at participating petrol stations by swiping their MyKad at the pump terminal. The government advised users to register their vehicles on the Budi Madani portal ahead of July 1 to avoid disruption during the initial rollout period.
