Fuel Prices Drop: RON97 Falls to RM4.10, Diesel at RM4.07 This Week

The Ministry of Finance announced reduced retail fuel prices for the week of June 25 to June 30, 2026, with RON97 petrol dropping by 25 sen to RM4.10 per litre and unsubsidised RON95 falling to RM3.47 per litre. Diesel in Peninsular Malaysia decreased by 30 sen to RM4.07 per litre, reflecting easing global crude oil markets.

The weekly adjustment follows a period of gradual softening in crude oil prices globally, driven by subdued demand from major economies and increased supply from non-OPEC producers. Analysts expect prices to remain at or near current levels for at least the next two to three weeks, barring any major geopolitical escalations.

Under Malaysia's floating price mechanism, RON97 and unsubsidised diesel prices are reviewed and adjusted weekly based on the managed float system, which tracks the movement of global benchmark crude oil prices. The subsidised RON95 and subsidised diesel prices remain fixed under the BUDI Madani scheme.

The price reduction provides relief to motorists, particularly those in the middle-income segment who rely on RON97 fuel. Commercial vehicle operators welcomed the drop in unsubsidised diesel as a modest offset to rising logistics costs, though industry associations noted that the impact on freight rates would take several weeks to filter through the supply chain.

Finance Minister Tengku Datuk Seri Zafrul Abdul Aziz emphasised that the government's fuel subsidy rationalisation programme has saved billions of ringgit since its introduction, funds that have been redirected to targeted cash assistance programmes for lower-income Malaysians.
