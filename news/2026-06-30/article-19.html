Eight Sunway College A-Level Students Gain Entry to Oxford and Cambridge

Eight students from Sunway College's Cambridge A-Level programme have secured places at the University of Oxford and the University of Cambridge for the 2026 intake, marking another strong year for one of Malaysia's most prestigious pre-university programmes. The students will pursue courses in medicine, engineering, economics, law, and natural sciences — among the most competitive fields at both universities.

Sunway College Principal Dr. Elizabeth Lee said the results reflect the institution's holistic approach to education, which emphasises not only academic rigour but also co-curricular excellence, research exposure, and personal development through community service and leadership programmes. "Our students are not just academically prepared — they are globally ready," she said.

The eight students join a growing community of Malaysian Oxbridge alumni, many of whom return to contribute to Malaysia's professional sectors. Several of this year's successful applicants cited their involvement in STEM competitions, debate societies, and social entrepreneurship projects as key differentiators in their applications. One student, who gained entry to Oxford's medical school, noted that her work with a rural health outreach programme in Sabah formed a central part of her personal statement.

Malaysia continues to send a strong cohort of students to the world's top universities annually. UK universities remain the most popular overseas destination for Malaysian students, followed by Australia, the United States, and Singapore. Government scholarships including JPA, Petronas, and Bank Negara Malaysia continue to fund a significant portion of overseas education, though private funding and self-sponsorship have grown as household incomes have risen.

Sunway Group founder and Chairman Tan Sri Jeffrey Cheah congratulated the students, noting that education investment remains the highest-return commitment a family or nation can make. He also highlighted Sunway University's growing research output and its ambition to break into the top 200 world university rankings within the next five years, which he said would further strengthen Malaysia's higher education ecosystem.
