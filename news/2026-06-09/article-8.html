ENERtec Asia 2026 Opens at KLCC, Drawing 12,000 Energy Industry Professionals

Technology The Star 9 June 2026

ASEAN's premier energy technology exhibition, ENERtec Asia 2026, officially opened at the Kuala Lumpur Convention Centre (KLCC), welcoming more than 12,000 energy industry professionals from 60 participating countries and regions. The event marks a pivotal moment for Malaysia as the country accelerates its dual transformation into a regional hub for both renewable energy and advanced digital infrastructure.

The exhibition showcases the latest technologies in solar, wind, hydrogen, and energy storage, alongside innovations in smart grid management and AI-powered energy optimisation systems. Malaysia's abundant solar irradiance and geographic position make it an attractive location for renewable energy development, and the government has set ambitious targets to achieve 70% renewable capacity by 2050 under the National Energy Transition Roadmap.

Energy and Natural Resources Minister Datuk Seri Fadillah Yusof, who opened the exhibition, said ENERtec Asia 2026 comes at the right time as Malaysia navigates the twin imperatives of meeting growing electricity demand from data centres and decarbonising its energy mix. He highlighted plans for new large-scale solar tenders and offshore wind pilot projects as concrete steps toward the government's clean energy goals.

International energy companies from Germany, South Korea, Japan, China, and the United States have set up exhibits, with several expected to announce partnerships with Malaysian state utilities and private developers during the three-day event. Petronas, TNB, and Sarawak Energy are also prominent exhibitors, showcasing their own transition strategies and investment pipelines.

The conference segment of ENERtec Asia 2026 will feature panel discussions on the challenges of powering Malaysia's data centre boom with renewable energy, energy security during the Middle East supply disruption, and the country's progress toward its net-zero commitments under the Paris Agreement.
