Johor on Track to Host 60% of Malaysia's Data Centre Capacity by 2030

Technology Asia Society / The Vibes 9 June 2026

Johor is rapidly cementing its position as Southeast Asia's most dynamic data centre hub, with forecasts indicating it will account for 60% of Malaysia's total data centre capacity by 2030. Malaysia as a whole already has 119 listed data centre facilities across 15 markets, and over two-thirds of all data centre capacity currently under construction in the five main Southeast Asian economies is committed to Malaysian soil.

The concentration in Johor is driven by a unique combination of factors: proximity to Singapore (which has strict data centre capacity caps due to land and power constraints), availability of large greenfield land parcels, reliable power grid connectivity, supportive state government policies, and access to the Trans-Pacific submarine cable network. Tech giants including Google, Microsoft, ByteDance, and several other hyperscalers have announced or broken ground on Johor facilities in the past two years.

The data centre boom has been a major catalyst for Johor's economy, creating thousands of construction and engineering jobs and drawing in supporting industries including cooling systems manufacturers, power equipment suppliers, and fibre network operators. Real estate prices in key data centre corridors around Nusajaya and Kulai have risen sharply, prompting planning authorities to designate new industrial zones to accommodate demand.

However, the rapid expansion is also generating scrutiny over its long-term sustainability. Environmental groups have raised concerns about the energy consumption of large-scale data centres and their impact on Malaysia's carbon reduction commitments. Several tech companies have responded by committing to power their Malaysian facilities entirely with renewable energy through power purchase agreements with solar and hydroelectric generators.

The Johor state government, which views the data centre industry as a cornerstone of its economic transformation agenda, has established a dedicated task force to streamline approvals, resolve utility connectivity issues, and ensure that the state's infrastructure keeps pace with the breakneck speed of investment. Officials say Johor's data centre cluster is expected to contribute significantly to national GDP by 2028.
