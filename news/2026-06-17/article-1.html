PM Anwar Leads Malaysian Delegation to ASEAN-Russia Summit in Kazan

Politics Source: The Star / Bernama Date: 17 June 2026

Prime Minister Datuk Seri Anwar Ibrahim departed Malaysia on June 17 to lead the nation's official delegation to the ASEAN-Russia Commemorative Summit in Kazan, Russia. The summit marks a significant milestone — 35 years of dialogue relations between ASEAN and Russia — and is expected to produce a landmark Kazan Declaration alongside a new Comprehensive Plan of Action for the ASEAN-Russia Strategic Partnership covering 2026 to 2030.

On the first day of the two-day summit, Anwar is scheduled to deliver a keynote address at the ASEAN-Russia Business Forum, which will bring together more than 400 corporate and business leaders from Russia and all ten ASEAN member states. The forum aims to identify new areas of cooperation in trade, investment, energy, and technology.

On the sidelines of the summit, Anwar is expected to hold bilateral meetings with Russian President Vladimir Putin, the Rais (Head) of the Republic of Tatarstan Rustam Minnikhanov, as well as fellow ASEAN leaders. Discussions are anticipated to cover regional security, the expansion of halal trade, and Malaysia's role as a neutral economic bridge in a fragmented geopolitical landscape.

Accompanying the Prime Minister is the Minister of Investment, Trade and Industry, reflecting Malaysia's strategic intent to use the summit to attract Russian investments into the Malaysian manufacturing and digital economy sectors. Malaysia's Ambassador to Russia underscored that the visit is expected to significantly strengthen people-to-people and institution-to-institution ties between both nations.

The Kazan visit follows Malaysia's active chairmanship year at ASEAN, during which Anwar championed reform-oriented multilateralism and greater South-South cooperation. Observers note that Malaysia's diplomatic posture — maintaining cordial relations with both Western nations and Russia — positions it as an increasingly important interlocutor in global diplomacy.
