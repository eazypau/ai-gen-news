EXO Returns to Malaysia for Concert After Six-Year Absence

Entertainment Source: Malay Mail / Hype.my Date: 17 June 2026

K-pop supergroup EXO is set to return to Malaysia for a highly anticipated concert in 2026, six years after their last Malaysian appearance — a landmark event that sold out quickly and remains one of the most talked-about K-pop concerts in the country's recent entertainment history. The announcement has sent Malaysian EXO-L (the fandom's name) communities into a frenzy of excitement on social media, with fans eagerly awaiting ticket sale details.

EXO, formed by SM Entertainment in 2011, is one of K-pop's most celebrated and commercially successful groups, known for their polished choreography, powerful vocals, and diverse musical catalogue spanning pop, R&B, hip-hop, and dance-electronic genres. The group — whose members include international stars Kai, Baekhyun, Chanyeol, and others — has maintained an enormous global fanbase through solo activities, subunit releases, and periodic full group comebacks.

Malaysia's K-pop concert scene has been experiencing a remarkable boom in 2026, with international acts including EXO, My Chemical Romance, Jason Mraz, and other global names announcing Malaysian tour dates. The resurgence reflects growing promoter confidence in the Malaysian live entertainment market following the full recovery of concert and events infrastructure after the disruptions of the early 2020s.

Promoter details, venue confirmation, and ticket pricing are expected to be announced in the coming weeks. Industry insiders anticipate the concert will be held at one of Kuala Lumpur's major indoor arenas — likely Stadium Axiata or Axiata Arena — which have the capacity to accommodate the large crowds an EXO concert is expected to draw. Premium and early-bird ticket packages are likely to sell out rapidly given the pent-up demand from the six-year absence.

The Malaysia Concert Organisers Association noted that the announcement of multiple high-profile international acts visiting Malaysia in 2026 is a significant boost for the local creative economy, generating revenue for local promoters, production crews, hospitality businesses, and tourism. Officials from Tourism Malaysia also acknowledged the increasing role of "concert tourism" — fans travelling from neighbouring countries to attend Malaysian concert dates — as a growing contributor to visitor arrivals.
