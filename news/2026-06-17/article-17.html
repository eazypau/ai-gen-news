Johor Bahru–Singapore RTS Link on Track for December 2026 Completion

Infrastructure Source: IQI Global / The Star Date: 17 June 2026

The highly anticipated Johor Bahru–Singapore Rapid Transit System (RTS) Link remains on schedule for its planned December 2026 opening, with construction crews pushing through final systems integration, station fit-out, and comprehensive trial runs. The cross-border rail link will connect Bukit Chagar station in Johor Bahru to Woodlands North station in Singapore, completing the journey in under five minutes and transforming daily commutes for tens of thousands of cross-border workers.

The RTS Link project, a joint initiative between Malaysia and Singapore, has navigated significant challenges since its inception — including a suspension and renegotiation in 2020 — but has maintained strong bilateral support throughout. Prasarana Malaysia Berhad and Singapore's SMRT are collaborating on operations, with ticketing systems designed to integrate seamlessly with both countries' existing public transport networks.

Daily ridership estimates project between 10,000 and 12,000 passengers per day at opening, rising significantly as commuters shift from the severely congested Causeway road crossing. Currently, the Johor–Singapore Causeway handles over 300,000 vehicle crossings daily, and the RTS Link is expected to alleviate peak-hour gridlock for a substantial segment of cross-border commuters.

The economic impact of the RTS Link extends well beyond reduced commute times. Property developers and analysts have pointed to a surge in property investment activity along the Johor Bahru corridor, with buyers and investors banking on improved connectivity to Singapore elevating the appeal of residential and commercial properties in Iskandar Malaysia. Transit-oriented developments around Bukit Chagar and the wider Johor Bahru city centre are attracting significant capital.

Both governments have also identified the RTS Link as a foundational piece of infrastructure for the proposed Johor-Singapore Special Economic Zone (SEZ), which aims to leverage Malaysia's land and cost advantages alongside Singapore's financial and logistics infrastructure to create a joint economic powerhouse in the southern corridor of the peninsula. Stakeholders anticipate that the RTS Link's opening will accelerate SEZ development momentum into 2027 and beyond.
