Red Bull Malaysia Launches Next Generation Athlete Development Programme

Sports Source: Sin Chew Daily / Media OutReach Date: 17 June 2026

Red Bull Malaysia has officially launched the Red Bull Next Generation programme in June 2026, a regional athlete development platform committed to discovering, nurturing, and supporting the next wave of sporting talent across Southeast Asia. The initiative is open to Malaysian athletes aged 18 to 25 across all sporting disciplines, from motorsports and extreme sports to conventional team and individual sports.

The programme offers more than just financial support. Successful applicants will receive access to Red Bull's global network of elite coaches, sports scientists, nutritionists, and performance analysts. Mentorship from established Red Bull athletes is also a key component, providing young competitors with insights into elite competition, mental resilience, and career management.

Red Bull Malaysia's Country Manager said the programme reflects the company's long-standing global commitment to developing world-class athletes from grassroots level. "Malaysia has incredible sporting talent, but many young athletes lack the resources and visibility to reach their full potential. Red Bull Next Generation is designed to change that," she said at the launch event in Kuala Lumpur.

Applications are open from June 2026, and interested athletes can apply via Red Bull's official Malaysian website. A panel of former national athletes and Red Bull-affiliated coaches will evaluate submissions based on athletic performance, potential, attitude, and personal story. The first cohort of selected athletes is expected to be announced by the end of Q3 2026.

The launch has been welcomed by Malaysia's sports development community, with the National Sports Council expressing hope that private sector initiatives like Red Bull Next Generation will complement government programmes in building a pipeline of elite athletes for major international competitions, including the 2028 Olympic Games in Brisbane.
