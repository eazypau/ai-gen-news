CIMB Securities Projects 7.8% KLCI Returns for 2026, Target at 1,772 Points

Finance Source: The Edge Malaysia Date: 17 June 2026

CIMB Securities has issued a bullish mid-year outlook for Malaysia's equity market, projecting the KLCI to reach 1,772 points by year-end 2026, which represents a 7.8% total return from current levels. The investment bank cites improving earnings visibility, a benign interest rate environment, and Malaysia's growing role in the global semiconductor supply chain as key catalysts.

The brokerage forecasts 6.4% earnings-per-share growth for the KLCI in 2026, driven primarily by three anchor counters: Tenaga Nasional Berhad (TNB), Public Bank, and Gamuda. Tenaga is expected to benefit from continued data centre-linked power demand, while Public Bank's stable loan growth and Gamuda's infrastructure order book provide earnings ballast.

CIMB Securities also highlighted that the potential for an early general election — which must be held by 2028 but may be called as early as 2027 — could catalyse a pre-election spending bump and improved market sentiment. Historical data shows that the KLCI has delivered positive returns in the 12 months preceding each of Malaysia's general elections.

Risks to the bullish call include an escalation of US-China trade tensions that could disrupt Malaysian export flows, a sharper-than-expected global economic slowdown, and any sudden deterioration in commodity prices — particularly crude palm oil and liquefied natural gas, which remain important contributors to Malaysia's export earnings.

Among sectoral recommendations, CIMB Securities favours technology, utilities, construction, and banking stocks, while maintaining a neutral stance on consumer and plantation sectors. The brokerage added that foreign fund inflows — which have been gradually returning to Bursa Malaysia in 2026 — could provide an additional lift to the index if sentiment continues to improve.
