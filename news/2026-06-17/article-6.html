KLCI Gains on Selective Buying in Semiconductor and Technology Stocks

Finance Source: Business Today / Bursa Malaysia Date: 17 June 2026

Malaysia's FTSE Bursa Malaysia KLCI closed the trading session in positive territory on Wednesday as selective buying interest in semiconductor and technology-related counters lifted market sentiment. Gainers outpaced losers across the board, with trading volume remaining healthy as institutional investors returned to accumulate positions in mid-cap tech stocks.

Among the day's top performers were companies in the semiconductor packaging, test-and-measurement equipment, and electronic manufacturing services segments — all benefiting from a rebound in global chip demand following months of inventory corrections. Analysts noted that Malaysian semiconductor companies are well-positioned to capture capacity shifts from higher-cost production bases in Taiwan and South Korea.

The positive session came against a backdrop of improving global risk appetite, with Wall Street's overnight rally and stable commodity prices providing a constructive backdrop for Asian equities. Foreign investors were net buyers on Bursa Malaysia for the third consecutive session, signalling renewed confidence in the Malaysian market's near-term prospects.

Market strategists from several local investment banks attributed the day's gains partly to positioning ahead of the FTSE Bursa Malaysia KLCI's semi-annual index review, which can trigger passive fund flows into index-constituent stocks. Several technology companies are expected to see adjusted weightings in the upcoming review.

The broader market also drew support from continued strength in banking stocks, particularly following positive credit growth data from Bank Negara Malaysia. Analysts maintained their year-end KLCI target range of 1,720–1,780 points, citing a combination of improving earnings momentum, a stable interest rate environment, and manageable global headwinds.
