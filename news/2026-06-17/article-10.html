Toh Ee Wei Refuses to Give Up on World Championship Defence Despite Injury

Sports Source: New Straits Times / FMT Date: 17 June 2026

Malaysia's national number one mixed doubles badminton shuttler Toh Ee Wei has vowed to fight for her place on the court, refusing to completely shut the door on defending her World Championship title in New Delhi despite nursing a significant injury that has kept her off the competitive circuit for several weeks. The 25-year-old champion demonstrated characteristic determination, insisting she will "do everything in my power" to be fit in time.

Toh Ee Wei, who won the BWF World Championships mixed doubles title alongside her partner in a dramatic final last year, sustained the injury during a training camp in May. The exact nature of the injury has not been officially disclosed by the Badminton Association of Malaysia (BAM), but sources close to the national team indicated it involves a lower limb issue requiring careful management to avoid re-aggravation.

Her coach confirmed she is currently undergoing intensive daily rehabilitation at BAM's national training centre in Bukit Kiara, working with the national sports medicine team to accelerate her recovery. "She is progressing well, but we are being cautious," the coach said, emphasising that the priority is a full recovery rather than rushing back prematurely.

Beyond the World Championships in New Delhi, Toh Ee Wei also has her eye on the 2026 Asian Games in Aichi-Nagoya, Japan — an event she described as a "major personal goal." The Asian Games hold special significance for Malaysian shuttlers, and a medal would cap off what has been a transformative period for Malaysia's mixed doubles programme.

BAM officials noted that they are monitoring her progress closely and will make a final decision on her availability for the World Championships once she demonstrates the required fitness benchmarks. They also confirmed that a backup pairing has been placed on standby, ensuring Malaysia remains competitive in the mixed doubles draw regardless of Toh Ee Wei's fitness status.
