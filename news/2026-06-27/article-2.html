Fahmi Urges Healthy Campaigning in Johor Election, Warns Against Slander

New Straits Times | 27 June 2026

Communications Minister Fahmi Fadzil called on all political parties and candidates to campaign on facts and substantive policy platforms ahead of the Johor state election, cautioning that the spread of slander and content touching on sensitive racial, religious, and royal (3R) issues would be met with enforcement action.

Fahmi made the remarks as nomination day opened on June 27, with the 14-day campaign period officially underway until July 10. He stressed that voters deserved an informed and respectful electoral process, and that the government would not tolerate content designed to inflame communal tensions or make false allegations against political opponents.

The minister also confirmed that the Malaysian Communications and Multimedia Commission (MCMC) has been tasked with monitoring internet traffic and social media activity throughout the election to ensure that online platforms are not used to spread disinformation or harmful content. The MCMC has the authority to request the removal of content that violates existing laws.

In addition, the Communications Ministry has established two dedicated media centres in Johor to facilitate comprehensive coverage by journalists throughout the campaign period and on polling day. Fahmi expressed confidence that both local and international media would have adequate access and support to cover the election fairly and accurately.

Political observers noted that the minister's warning comes amid heightened sensitivity following several high-profile cases of election-related disinformation in recent Malaysian by-elections. All major parties have been urged to ensure their campaign materials are factually grounded and that social media teams are briefed on legal obligations under the Communications and Multimedia Act.
