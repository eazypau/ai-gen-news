Limp Bizkit Picks KL as Sole Asia Stop for December 2026 World Tour

New Straits Times | 27 June 2026

American nu-metal legends Limp Bizkit will make their long-awaited Malaysian debut at the National Hockey Stadium in Bukit Jalil, Kuala Lumpur, on December 9, 2026. The concert has been confirmed as the band's exclusive one-stop Asia show — the sole Asian destination on their 2026 world tour — making Kuala Lumpur the only city on the continent where fans from across Asia can see the iconic act perform live this year.

The announcement has electrified Malaysian music fans, particularly millennials who grew up with the band's late-1990s and early-2000s catalogue. Hits such as "Rollin'," "Break Stuff," "Nookie," and "My Way" are anthems for an entire generation, and the prospect of hearing them performed live for the first time in Malaysia has generated substantial social media buzz since the show was announced. Tickets are priced from RM398 to RM786, with sales opening on June 30 at 12pm via the Hitman Live platform.

The concert is being promoted by a Malaysian entertainment agency that has secured an exclusive Asia deal with the band's management, ensuring that Malaysian fans will not need to travel to neighbouring countries to catch the show. Industry insiders said negotiations for the Malaysia date had been ongoing for over a year, with the band's camp reportedly impressed by the strength of their fanbase in the country based on streaming and social media data.

The National Hockey Stadium in Bukit Jalil, which has hosted major international concerts and sporting events, has a capacity of up to 15,000 in concert configuration and is considered one of Malaysia's premier large-scale live music venues. Organisers have indicated that the production will be a full-scale arena show with Limp Bizkit's trademark high-energy performance style, including elaborate stage design and their renowned crowd interaction.

The Limp Bizkit show adds to a strong lineup of international concerts already confirmed for Malaysia in the second half of 2026, reflecting the country's growing reputation as a premier destination for live entertainment in Southeast Asia. Concert promoters said demand from major international acts to play Malaysia had increased significantly as the post-pandemic live music market continued its robust recovery.
