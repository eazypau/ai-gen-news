K-pop Legends EXO to Return to Kuala Lumpur After Six-Year Absence

Malay Mail / Hype Malaysia | 27 June 2026

Legendary K-pop group EXO is set to return to Kuala Lumpur for their first Malaysian concert in six years, delighting a devoted fanbase that has waited through military service hiatuses and the global pandemic to see the group reunite on stage in Malaysia. The announcement triggered an outpouring of excitement from EXO-Ls — the group's official fanbase — across Malaysian social media, with fans sharing throwback concert memories and marking the announcement date in their calendars.

EXO debuted under SM Entertainment in 2012 and rapidly became one of the defining acts of the second generation of the Korean Wave (Hallyu), known for their powerful choreography, intricate vocal harmonies, and superhero-inspired concept. Their Malaysian fanbase is among the most passionate in Southeast Asia, having sold out previous concerts at the Malaysia International Trade and Exhibition Centre (MITEC) and Stadium Merdeka in past tours.

Details of the concert venue, date, and ticketing arrangements for the 2026 Kuala Lumpur show are expected to be announced in the coming weeks. Concert promoters indicated that the show was in advanced stages of planning and that a major KL venue had already been secured, with ticket sales anticipated to go live in July 2026. Given the high anticipated demand, fans were encouraged to register early via the official ticketing platform to maximise their chances of securing seats.

The return of EXO to Malaysia follows a broader resurgence in K-pop live event activity across Southeast Asia in 2026, with multiple high-profile groups touring the region for the first time in several years. Malaysia has positioned itself as a key destination for K-pop concert promoters, supported by the country's strong entertainment infrastructure, well-organised fan communities, and a robust market of Hallyu enthusiasts.

The concert is expected to draw fans not just from within Malaysia but from neighbouring Singapore, Indonesia, Thailand, and beyond, given EXO's iconic status in the global K-pop community. Tourism industry observers noted that major K-pop concerts have become significant drivers of short-stay tourism to Kuala Lumpur, with international fans typically spending several days in the city around the event date.
