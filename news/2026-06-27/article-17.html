Suzuki e VITARA Launches in Malaysia with Prices Starting from RM188,000

Sin Chew Daily | 27 June 2026

Suzuki's all-electric e VITARA SUV has made its official Malaysian market debut with an opening price of RM188,000 for the base variant, positioning the model in the growing mid-premium EV segment. The launch marks Suzuki's first full battery electric vehicle offering in Malaysia and signals the brand's commitment to participating in the country's accelerating EV transition, which has seen consumer interest and government policy support increase significantly over the past two years.

The e VITARA is built on a new dedicated electric vehicle platform co-developed with Toyota under the two companies' strategic alliance, offering a modern design language with clean exterior lines and a spacious cabin adapted for Malaysian driving conditions. The vehicle is equipped with a battery pack delivering a range suited for both urban commuting and occasional highway journeys, addressing a key concern among Malaysian EV first-movers about range adequacy given the country's developing charging network.

Suzuki Malaysia's authorised distributor confirmed that two variants would be available initially, with the higher-specification model featuring advanced driver assistance systems (ADAS), a panoramic sunroof, and an enhanced in-car connectivity suite. Charging compatibility includes both AC home charging and DC fast-charging capability at public stations, with the distributor also offering a home charger installation package bundled with the purchase.

The e VITARA enters a Malaysian EV market that is rapidly expanding, with brands including BYD, Chery, Volvo, BMW, and Proton all actively competing for the attention of buyers making the switch from internal combustion engines. Industry observers noted that Suzuki's brand recognition and existing loyal customer base from its conventional petrol models could give the e VITARA a warm reception among buyers seeking a trusted Japanese marque in their first EV purchase.

Test drives for the e VITARA have already begun at selected Suzuki showrooms across the Klang Valley, Penang, and Johor Bahru, with dealer staff reporting high levels of walk-in interest since the launch announcement. The company has set an initial sales target of several hundred units for 2026 and indicated that allocation volumes would be reviewed upward if demand proves robust in the opening months.
