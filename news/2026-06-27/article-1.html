Johor State Election Nomination Day: 56 Seats to Be Contested on July 11

Malay Mail / The Star | 27 June 2026

June 27, 2026 marks a pivotal moment in Malaysian politics as it is the official nomination day for the 16th Johor State Legislative Assembly election. All 56 seats in the Johor State Legislative Assembly will be contested, with major road closures and traffic diversions implemented near nomination centres across the state to manage the influx of candidates, party supporters, and media personnel.

The Election Commission has set July 11 as polling day, with early voting scheduled for July 7. The campaign period officially began on June 27 and will run for 14 days until 11.59pm on July 10. A total of 2,727,926 Johor voters are registered for the election, comprising 2,703,175 regular voters, 12,041 army personnel and their spouses, and 12,710 police personnel and their spouses.

The election is shaping up to be a closely watched contest between the incumbent Barisan Nasional (BN) government, which won a commanding two-thirds supermajority of 40 out of 56 seats in the 2022 Johor state election under Menteri Besar Onn Hafiz Ghazi, and challenger coalitions including Pakatan Harapan (PH) and Perikatan Nasional (PN).

DAP has named candidates for key seats including Tiram, Johor Jaya, Bukit Permai, and Senai, while other parties within PH and PN are expected to field candidates across the remaining constituencies. Political analysts predict BN will face a sterner test than in 2022, given shifting demographic patterns and evolving voter sentiment on cost-of-living issues.

The Communications Ministry has set up two dedicated media centres in Johor to support press coverage throughout the campaign period, while the Malaysian Communications and Multimedia Commission (MCMC) has confirmed it will monitor internet access to ensure smooth and fair coverage of political rallies and voter information services during the election season.
