Pentamaster Clears Legal Hurdle as Ocado Innovation Drops Lawsuit

The Edge Malaysia | 27 June 2026

Pentamaster Corporation Berhad (PCB), a Malaysian technology company listed on Bursa Malaysia, announced that UK-based Ocado Innovation Limited has formally discontinued its legal action against the group and all its subsidiaries. The case, which had been a significant overhang on Pentamaster's stock, revolved around intellectual property and contractual disputes in the automated retail and logistics technology space.

Pentamaster said it received formal notice of the discontinuation and that no settlement payments or admissions of liability were involved. The company's management expressed relief at the resolution, noting that it allows the group to channel its full focus and resources back into business development and its technology pipeline without the burden of ongoing litigation.

Shares in Pentamaster responded positively to the announcement on Bursa Malaysia, with investors welcoming the removal of legal uncertainty. Analysts at several brokerage firms upgraded their outlook on the stock, citing the cleaner balance sheet position and reduced risk profile as catalysts for a rerating of the company's valuation.

Pentamaster operates in the automated test equipment (ATE), factory automation, and smart manufacturing space, serving global clients in the semiconductor, medical device, and consumer electronics industries. The group has been expanding its presence in automated solutions aligned with the growth of the electric vehicle ecosystem and AI-driven manufacturing processes.

The resolution comes at a time when several Malaysian technology companies are strengthening their intellectual property portfolios and establishing cleaner legal frameworks ahead of anticipated capital market activity. Pentamaster's management indicated that it would provide a more detailed operational update at its next quarterly results briefing.
