World Cup 2026 Viewing Guide: RTMKlik Free Streaming, Mamak Spots Packed Across Malaysia

Sports Malay Mail / RTM 15 June 2026

Malaysia's football fans have been celebrating an unexpected gift this World Cup season: all 104 matches of the FIFA World Cup 2026 are available to watch for free via RTMKlik, RTM's streaming platform, and Unifi TV — following Astro's surprise failure to secure the broadcast rights it held for two decades. The shift has transformed how Malaysians access the world's biggest sporting event.

RTMKlik is accessible through a mobile app and RTM's official website. Viewers can register using their MyDigital ID to unlock free streaming of every group stage, knockout, and final match. The platform has been upgraded to handle peak streaming loads, and RTM has confirmed it is broadcasting in HD quality with both English and Bahasa Malaysia commentary options.

Unifi TV is also showing all 104 matches for both subscribers and non-subscribers. A daily pass is priced at RM20, a full-season pass at RM60, and existing Unifi TV subscribers receive an exclusive discounted season pass at RM50. The flexibility of daily passes has been popular with casual viewers who only want to watch specific high-profile matches.

Bars, mamaks, and sports cafes across Kuala Lumpur, Petaling Jaya, Penang, and Johor Bahru have reported packed crowds for every match session, with many venues staying open through the early hours of the morning to cater to the matches played in North America. Football fans from across age groups have been gathering at their favourite mamak spots, recreating the communal viewing atmosphere that Malaysians love.

Today's schedule (Malaysian Standard Time) features Germany vs Curaçao at 1am, Netherlands vs Japan at 4am, Côte d'Ivoire vs Ecuador at 7am, and Sweden vs Tunisia at 10am. The 48-team format of this year's tournament — the first in history with so many nations — means there are more matches per day than any previous World Cup, keeping fans busy throughout June and into July.
