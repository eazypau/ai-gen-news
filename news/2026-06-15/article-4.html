KLIMS 2026: Zeekr X Opens for Booking in Malaysia with Fridge and Auto Doors

Lifestyle Malay Mail / ZigWheels 15 June 2026

The updated Zeekr X 2026 has officially opened for booking in Malaysia, making its debut at the Kuala Lumpur International Mobility Show (KLIMS 2026) at MITEC, which runs from June 12 to 21. The electric vehicle brings a range of notable new features designed to elevate everyday convenience for Malaysian drivers.

Key highlights of the 2026 model include a built-in fridge, auto-opening doors, faster charging capability, and a redesigned interior that offers a more premium feel. The vehicle has attracted significant attention at the KLIMS showfloor, drawing queues of prospective buyers and EV enthusiasts eager to experience the new features firsthand.

Zeekr is among more than 20 automotive brands participating in KLIMS 2026, which has positioned itself around the theme of a smarter, greener future for Malaysian mobility. The show also features over 70 test drive vehicles across a range of categories, from EV sedans and SUVs to hybrid crossovers and commercial vans.

EV adoption in Malaysia has surged dramatically in 2026, rising 113.7% in Q1 compared to the same period in 2025. Brands like Zeekr, Dongfeng, GWM, MG, Smart, XPeng and Wuling are competing aggressively for a growing middle-income EV buyer segment. Government incentives under Budget 2026, including import duty exemptions on EVs, have been credited with driving the surge.

Interested buyers can register their bookings at the KLIMS showroom or through Zeekr Malaysia's official website. Delivery timelines and final pricing details are expected to be announced before the close of the show on June 21.
