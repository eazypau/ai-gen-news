Bursa Malaysia Closes Higher, Outperforms Regional Peers Amid Global Risk-Off

Business BusinessToday 15 June 2026

Bursa Malaysia closed higher last week, outperforming several regional stock markets as global risk-off sentiment weighed on broader Asian indices. The resilience of Malaysia's market was attributed to strong domestic fundamentals and defensive investor positioning in Malaysian equities amid heightened global uncertainty.

Global risk-off sentiment was driven primarily by renewed weakness in major technology stocks and rising geopolitical tensions in the West Asia region. While bourses in Singapore, Thailand, and Hong Kong recorded declines, Bursa Malaysia's FBM KLCI index bucked the trend, closing with modest gains supported by buying activity in banking, plantation, and infrastructure-linked counters.

Market analysts from several brokerages noted that Malaysia's market resilience reflects the underlying strength of the domestic economy, including robust GDP growth of around 5%, a strengthening ringgit, and continued foreign direct investment inflows. Institutional investors have been gradually increasing their Malaysia weightings in regional portfolios.

The government's Budget 2026 spending commitments — particularly in infrastructure, digital economy, and green energy — are expected to support corporate earnings in sectors directly tied to these themes. Construction, utilities, and technology services companies listed on Bursa are seen as key beneficiaries of government contract awards under the 13th Malaysia Plan.

Retail investor participation in Bursa Malaysia has also grown significantly in 2026, spurred by increased financial literacy campaigns and the popularity of investment platforms among younger Malaysians. Trading volumes in June have remained elevated compared to the same month in 2025, suggesting continued momentum in market activity.
