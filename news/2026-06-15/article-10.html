Ringgit Seen Strengthening Further as Malaysia GDP Growth Hits 5% in 2026

Economy IDN Financials / Bloomberg 15 June 2026

The Malaysian ringgit — now recognised as Asia's top-performing currency over the past year — is forecast to reach new highs in 2026, currently trading at around 3.95 per US dollar. Economists and financial analysts are pointing to Malaysia's strong cyclical fundamentals as the key driver of continued currency appreciation.

Malaysia's GDP growth is estimated at 5.0% for 2026, underpinned by robust private investment, stable employment, and a strong performance in the semiconductor and digital services sectors. The government's ongoing execution of the 13th Malaysia Plan (RMK13) and Budget 2026's infrastructure commitments are providing additional support to economic confidence.

Analysts from Bloomberg and Capital Economics note that the ringgit is likely fairly valued at current levels, having recovered sharply from the historic lows seen in late 2023 when it briefly weakened past 4.80 against the US dollar. The recovery has been attributed to improved current account balances, stronger foreign direct investment, and the Madani government's fiscal reform credibility.

Malaysia's clean energy export potential — particularly in green hydrogen and solar power — and its positioning in the global technology supply chain are providing structural tailwinds for the ringgit that go beyond cyclical factors. Bank Negara Malaysia has stated it is comfortable with the current trajectory of ringgit appreciation and is not seeking to intervene.

Despite the positive outlook, some economists have flagged that a strong ringgit could begin to squeeze Malaysia's export competitiveness, particularly in the manufacturing and palm oil sectors. Finance Ministry officials have said they are monitoring the situation and will adjust policies if required to ensure balanced growth.
