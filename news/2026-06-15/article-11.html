Anwar Administration Declares 2026 a Year of 'Execution' to Lock In Policy Gains

Economy Fortune / ISEAS 15 June 2026

Prime Minister Anwar Ibrahim's administration has officially declared 2026 a year of "execution", shifting the government's focus from policy design to delivery as it enters the critical mid-term phase of its mandate. Economy Minister Akmal Nasrullah Mohd Nasir, appointed in December 2025, has taken the lead in this delivery push, overseeing progress under the 13th Malaysia Plan (RMK13).

A central pillar of the execution drive is MyRMK, a new digital platform designed to track, monitor, and report on the progress of RMK13 projects in real time. Officials say the system will improve government accountability and help identify bottlenecks in delivery before they become political liabilities ahead of the next general election.

Minister Akmal Nasrullah has identified semiconductor investment in Johor — particularly in the Johor-Singapore Special Economic Zone — and clean energy exports as the two most high-priority economic bets for Malaysia in 2026. The government is targeting foreign direct investment from major chipmakers and green energy buyers across Asia and Europe.

A cabinet reshuffle in December 2025 brought in several new ministers with specific mandates tied to delivering measurable outcomes. Political analyst Ong Kian Ming from ISEAS noted that the reshuffle was more consequential than expected, reflecting PM Anwar's desire to reinvigorate the government's momentum after a period of policy announcements without commensurate results.

Visit Malaysia 2026 — which targets 43 million tourist arrivals — is another flagship execution milestone, with the Tourism, Arts and Culture Ministry under pressure to demonstrate tangible results from the campaign. Observers say the government's credibility in 2026 rests heavily on whether its ambitious plan targets translate into real outcomes for ordinary Malaysians.
