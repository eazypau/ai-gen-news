Proton Launches e.MAS 7 Premium Plus at KLIMS 2026 for RM119,000

Lifestyle ZigWheels 15 June 2026

PRO-NET, Proton's electric vehicle arm, has launched the new e.MAS 7 Premium Plus variant at KLIMS 2026, priced at RM119,000. The upgraded model offers a larger battery pack, extended driving range, and a suite of premium features that position it as a flagship EV for the Malaysian market.

The e.MAS 7 Premium Plus builds on the success of the original e.MAS 7, adding improvements to onboard tech, cabin comfort, and charging speed. Specific range figures have not been disclosed ahead of the official handover ceremony at MITEC, but the company has indicated the range increase is significant over the standard variant.

Proton's EV push comes at a time when Malaysia's electric vehicle market is experiencing explosive growth. EV adoption surged 113.7% in the first quarter of 2026, and brands including Zeekr, BYD, MG and Smart are competing fiercely for Malaysian buyers. The e.MAS 7 Premium Plus is Proton's answer to that competitive pressure.

KLIMS 2026, which runs from June 12 to 21 at the Malaysia International Trade and Exhibition Centre, has over 20 automotive brands participating. The show serves as a crucial launchpad for Malaysia's push towards becoming a regional hub for EV manufacturing and adoption under the National Energy Transition Roadmap.

Proton is expected to roll out test drive programmes for the e.MAS 7 Premium Plus in the weeks following KLIMS. Interested buyers can register their interest at the Proton booth at MITEC or through the official PRO-NET website.
