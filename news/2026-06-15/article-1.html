Hamzah Zainudin Reinstated as Opposition Leader After Bersatu Exit

Politics Malay Mail 15 June 2026

Datuk Seri Hamzah Zainudin has been reinstated as Malaysia's Opposition Leader, just two months after relinquishing the role, following an announcement by PAS president Tan Sri Abdul Hadi Awang on June 13, 2026. The reinstatement ends a brief period of uncertainty for Perikatan Nasional (PN) over its parliamentary leadership.

Hamzah had stepped down as Opposition Leader after being expelled from Parti Pribumi Bersatu Malaysia (Bersatu), with Datuk Seri Dr Ahmad Samsuri Mokhtar taking over the role following PN's Supreme Council meeting on May 16. His expulsion from Bersatu had raised questions about the legitimacy of his continued occupancy of the post.

The situation changed when Hamzah established his own new party, Parti Wawasan Negara, and formally aligned it with PAS. PAS said it had always preferred Hamzah in the role, noting that Bersatu's discomfort had been the primary obstacle. With that tie severed, PAS consulted lawmakers from within its own ranks and those aligned with Hamzah before deciding to restore him as Opposition Leader.

Datuk Seri Dr Ahmad Samsuri Mokhtar will continue as PN chairman. PAS added that the decision to reinstate Hamzah reflected the majority will of Perikatan Nasional lawmakers, with the Saifuddin faction noting that the support base remained strong. Political analysts have described the development as a consolidation of PAS's dominance within the PN coalition structure.

The move is expected to sharpen the opposition's focus heading into upcoming state elections in Johor and Negeri Sembilan. Hamzah has dismissed speculation about any further power struggles within PN, saying attention should now return to policy and holding the government to account in Parliament.
