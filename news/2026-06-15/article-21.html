Visit Malaysia 2026 Campaign Targets 35.6 Million Arrivals and RM147 Billion Receipts

Tourism Tourism Malaysia 15 June 2026

Malaysia's flagship Visit Malaysia 2026 (VM2026) campaign is targeting 35.6 million international tourist arrivals and RM147.1 billion (approximately USD 33 billion) in tourism receipts for the full year. Tourism Malaysia describes the campaign as the country's most ambitious tourism drive ever, building on the momentum of strong Q1 results that saw 10.65 million arrivals in the first three months of 2026.

VM2026 is anchored on five strategic pillars designed to broaden Malaysia's appeal beyond traditional beach and shopping tourism: sustainable and eco-tourism, cultural and heritage experiences, adventure and nature-based travel, digital innovation in hospitality, and enhanced global air connectivity. The campaign has been launched in multiple key source markets including the United Kingdom, India, China, the Middle East, South Korea, and Japan.

The government has supported the campaign with visa-free access for travellers from India, China, and several other key markets, alongside a significant increase in bilateral flight capacities. Tourism Malaysia has also invested in digital marketing campaigns across social media platforms, leveraging Malaysia's rich cultural diversity and natural landscapes as key selling points.

Niche tourism products — including culinary trails, heritage walks, wildlife tourism in Sabah and Sarawak, and adventure tourism in Cameron Highlands and Langkawi — are being promoted to attract higher-spending visitors who stay longer and contribute more to local economies beyond just hotel stays and airport shopping.

The PM's office has been personally involved in promoting VM2026 during international engagements, with PM Anwar Ibrahim using bilateral meetings and multilateral forums to personally invite heads of state and business delegations to visit Malaysia. Tourism observers say that top-level political championing of the campaign has been a significant factor in the strong arrivals numbers seen so far in 2026.
