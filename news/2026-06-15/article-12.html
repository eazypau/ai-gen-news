Malaysia Welcomes Record 10.6 Million Tourists in Q1 2026, Surpassing Thailand

Tourism Travel and Tour World 15 June 2026

Malaysia has achieved a landmark tourism milestone in the first quarter of 2026, welcoming 10.65 million international tourists between January and March — a 5.4% rise year-on-year. The figure places Malaysia ahead of Thailand, Singapore, and Indonesia for the same period, cementing its status as Southeast Asia's tourism frontrunner for 2026.

Singapore contributed the largest share of visitors with over 5.14 million arrivals, reflecting the close ties between the two countries and the ongoing popularity of cross-border day trips and weekend getaways. India and China also showed significant growth, driven by visa-free access policies introduced under the Madani government and expanded direct flight connectivity.

The strong results are a timely boost for Malaysia's Visit Malaysia 2026 (VM2026) campaign, which targets 35.6 million international tourists and RM147.1 billion in tourism receipts for the full year. If current quarterly growth rates are maintained, Malaysia is well-positioned to meet or exceed its annual target.

Tourism Malaysia has attributed the Q1 success to five strategic pillars underpinning VM2026: sustainable tourism, cultural and heritage experiences, adventure and nature-based travel, digital innovation in hospitality, and enhanced global air connectivity. New flight routes from key markets in the Middle East, India, and Northeast Asia have been credited with driving increased arrivals.

The tourism boom is also generating broader economic benefits, with hotel occupancy rates in Kuala Lumpur, Penang, Langkawi, and Kota Kinabalu reaching multi-year highs. The hospitality and retail sectors are among the chief beneficiaries, with government data showing tourism-related employment growing by 7.2% compared to Q1 2025.
