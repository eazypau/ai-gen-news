Kia Malaysia Previews EV4, EV5 and PV5 Electric Models at KLIMS 2026

Lifestyle ZigWheels 15 June 2026

Kia Malaysia has made a bold statement at KLIMS 2026 by previewing four exciting new models: the EV4 sedan, the EV5 SUV, the PV5 electric commercial van, and the K4 sedan. The showcase signals Kia's serious intent to expand its EV footprint in Malaysia beyond the EV6, which remains its flagship electric offering in the country.

The EV5 is considered the most market-ready of the new models, having already been launched and proven in Thailand and the Philippines. Its competitive pricing in those markets suggests a comparably attractive price point for Malaysia, potentially making it one of the most affordable premium EVs in the country when it eventually goes on sale.

The EV4 — a sleek C-segment electric sedan — is particularly noteworthy for its design-forward approach, with a low-slung silhouette and a sophisticated interior targeting younger professional buyers. The PV5, meanwhile, addresses the commercial segment, offering businesses an electric van option for last-mile delivery and service applications.

KLIMS 2026, running at MITEC from June 12 to 21, has been dominated by EV news with every major brand unveiling new electric models or concept cars. EV adoption in Malaysia surged 113.7% in Q1 2026, and the show's strong attendance reflects robust consumer interest in transitioning away from combustion engines.

Kia Malaysia has not confirmed official launch dates or pricing for the new models, but said it will make further announcements in the coming months based on market feedback gathered at KLIMS. Test drive slots for the EV5 are already heavily oversubscribed at the show, underlining strong market appetite for the model.
