Mikro MSC and Hong Kong Cospower Partner to Advance Battery Energy Storage in Malaysia

Business BusinessToday 15 June 2026

Mikro MSC, a Malaysian power technology company listed on Bursa Malaysia, has announced a strategic partnership with Hong Kong Cospower Technology to develop battery energy storage system (BESS) projects across Malaysia. The collaboration aims to accelerate the country's clean energy transition and support national grid modernisation goals under the National Energy Transition Roadmap (NETR).

Battery energy storage systems are increasingly critical to Malaysia's energy agenda, as the country expands its renewable energy capacity — particularly solar — and needs reliable storage solutions to manage intermittency. The Sabah Southern MADANI Link project, recently approved under Budget 2026, is one of several infrastructure initiatives that will benefit from enhanced grid storage capabilities.

Hong Kong Cospower Technology brings expertise in large-scale BESS deployment, having completed projects across Asia including China, Singapore, and Vietnam. For Mikro MSC, the partnership represents a significant expansion of its business beyond its traditional power protection and automation systems portfolio into the renewable energy storage segment.

The two companies have signed a memorandum of understanding (MOU) covering joint project development, technology transfer, and market expansion in Malaysia and potentially the broader Southeast Asian region. Financial terms of the deal have not been disclosed, but project development timelines suggest early BESS installations could come online in late 2026 or early 2027.

Malaysia has set a target for renewable energy to make up 70% of its electricity generation capacity by 2050. Meeting that target will require massive investment in storage technology, smart grid infrastructure, and demand management systems — areas where partnerships like this Mikro-Cospower deal are expected to play an increasingly important role.
