Sabah Southern MADANI Link Phase 1 Approved Under Budget 2026

Economy Free Malaysia Today 15 June 2026

The federal government has approved Phase 1 of the 60-kilometre Sabah Southern MADANI Link project under Budget 2026, marking a significant step forward in addressing East Malaysia's long-standing grid infrastructure gap. The project is designed to boost electricity transmission stability and reliability across southern Sabah, benefiting hundreds of thousands of residents and businesses in the region.

The MADANI Link initiative forms part of the Madani government's broader commitment to bridging the urban-rural divide and ensuring equitable access to essential infrastructure, particularly in Sabah and Sarawak, which have historically received less infrastructure investment relative to Peninsular Malaysia. Phase 1 of the project will focus on a new high-voltage transmission corridor connecting key population and industrial centres.

Federal authorities have indicated that the project will be developed in partnership with Sabah Electricity Sdn Bhd (SESB) and is expected to create hundreds of skilled employment opportunities during the construction phase. The completed corridor is also intended to support future renewable energy integration in Sabah, including solar farms and mini-hydroelectric projects.

The Sabah Southern MADANI Link is one of several East Malaysia infrastructure projects under the 13th Malaysia Plan (RMK13). Other approved projects include rural road upgrades in Sarawak, expansion of treated water supply coverage in inland Sabah, and improved broadband connectivity in remote communities.

Political representatives from Sabah have welcomed the approval, describing it as a long-overdue investment in the state's economic future. They noted that improved grid stability would reduce operational costs for manufacturers and attract new industries to Sabah, supporting diversification away from commodity dependence toward higher-value manufacturing and services.
