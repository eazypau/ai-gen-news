FIFA World Cup 2026: Live Matches Today — Germany, Netherlands, Côte d'Ivoire in Action

Sports Says.com / RTM 15 June 2026

Malaysian football fans are waking up early today as the FIFA World Cup 2026 Group Stage continues with a packed schedule. June 15 features four high-profile matches: Germany vs Curaçao at 1am, Netherlands vs Japan at 4am, Côte d'Ivoire vs Ecuador at 7am, and Sweden vs Tunisia at 10am — all in Malaysian Standard Time.

The expanded 48-team tournament, hosted across the United States, Canada, and Mexico, is available to Malaysians for free via RTMKlik — RTM's streaming platform — and Unifi TV. Astro, which held the broadcast rights for two decades, was not among the winning bidders this year, marking a significant change for Malaysian viewers accustomed to watching the World Cup on satellite television.

RTMKlik can be accessed through a mobile app and RTM's official website, while Unifi TV offers both free and subscriber options including a daily pass at RM20 and a full-season pass at RM60, with an exclusive RM50 discounted rate for existing Unifi TV subscribers.

Bars and mamak stalls across Kuala Lumpur, Petaling Jaya, and major cities have been reporting packed crowds for each session. Match-day fever has also boosted local food and beverage outlets, with many staying open through the night to accommodate the time difference with North America.

The tournament is Malaysia's biggest sporting viewing event of the year, with local sports associations reporting record streaming numbers in the first four days since the June 11 kick-off. Fans have been using their MyDigital ID to register for free access on RTMKlik, as per the government's arrangement with FIFA to ensure free-to-air coverage for all Malaysians.
