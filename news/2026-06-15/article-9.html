Toh Ee Wei Keeps World Championship and Asian Games Hopes Alive Despite Injury

Sports The Sun Malaysia / BWF 15 June 2026

National number one mixed doubles shuttler Toh Ee Wei has refused to completely close the door on defending her World Championship title in New Delhi and competing at the 2026 Asian Games in Aichi-Nagoya, Japan, despite ongoing injury concerns. Speaking to reporters ahead of a recovery update, Ee Wei struck a cautiously optimistic tone about her rehabilitation progress.

Ee Wei, who won the World Championship crown with partner Chen Tang Jie, has been sidelined with a recurring injury that has forced her to skip several major international tournaments in the first half of 2026, including the Indonesian Open and the ongoing Australian Open. The nature and severity of the injury have not been disclosed, but BAM has confirmed she remains under close medical supervision.

The World Badminton Championships in New Delhi are scheduled for later in 2026, and Ee Wei's potential participation hinges on the outcome of her recovery over the next several weeks. A decision on whether she will travel to India is expected to be made in consultation with the BAM medical team and coaching staff.

The 2026 Asian Games in Aichi-Nagoya, Japan, represent another major milestone in Ee Wei's calendar, with Malaysia aiming for a strong showing in badminton at the regional multi-sport event. Her inclusion in the Malaysian team would significantly bolster the country's medal prospects in mixed doubles.

BAM has expressed confidence in Ee Wei's resilience and determination, noting she has previously returned from injury setbacks in strong form. Fans and followers of Malaysian badminton are keenly watching her recovery, hopeful that the country's most decorated mixed doubles player will be back on the court before the year is out.
