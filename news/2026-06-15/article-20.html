Attempt to Topple Negeri Sembilan Government Thwarted, PH Chairman Says

Politics Malay Mail 15 June 2026

The Pakatan Harapan (PH) chairman has revealed that there was a recent attempt to topple the Negeri Sembilan state government, despite the state's current political and economic stability. The chairman confirmed that the plot had been detected and neutralised, with the Negeri Sembilan government firmly remaining in control of the state assembly.

Negeri Sembilan, currently governed by Pakatan Harapan under Menteri Besar Aminuddin Harun, has been one of PH's more stable state governments since the 2023 state elections. The attempt to destabilise it is being viewed by political observers as part of broader opposition efforts to weaken PH's hold on key peninsular states ahead of the next general election cycle.

The PH chairman did not disclose specific details of how the attempted government toppling was organised or who was behind it, citing the sensitivity of ongoing investigations. However, the disclosure is likely to put state assemblypersons on alert and prompt tighter party discipline measures within PH's Negeri Sembilan caucus.

Political analysts have noted that such attempts are not unprecedented in Malaysian politics, recalling the Sheraton Move of 2020 and subsequent attempts to destabilise state governments through the defection of elected representatives. The Madani government has previously signalled its intention to pursue anti-hopping legislation reforms to deter such manoeuvres.

The Negeri Sembilan government has continued to function normally, with ongoing infrastructure, economic development, and social welfare programmes running on schedule. The Menteri Besar has urged state assemblypersons to remain focused on their constituents and not be distracted by political noise.
