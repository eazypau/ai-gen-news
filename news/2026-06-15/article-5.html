KLIMS 2026: Perodua Unveils Futuristic C2 Electric Sedan Concept

Lifestyle ZigWheels / DSF.my 15 June 2026

Perodua has turned heads at KLIMS 2026 with the unveiling of its C2 concept car — a B-segment electric sedan that previews the brand's next generation of design language. The showcar signals a bold departure from Perodua's previous combustion-engine aesthetic and the earlier EM-O concept, and marks the automaker's most ambitious electric vehicle vision to date.

The C2 concept incorporates a suite of AI-driven features including real-time navigation, charging station location guidance, live traffic updates, enhanced anti-theft systems, and semi-autonomous parking assistance. The interior adopts a minimalist cockpit layout with a large central display screen and ambient lighting throughout the cabin.

Perodua officials have clarified that the C2 is a concept vehicle and not yet confirmed for production, though the brand has signalled its intention to enter the EV market in the coming years. The concept is seen as a direct response to the growing competition from Chinese EV brands now entering the Malaysian market.

Malaysia's national carmaker has historically dominated the country's automotive sales with its affordable Myvi and Axia models, but faces pressure to transition as EV adoption accelerates. The government's Budget 2026 EV incentives and the Madani economic agenda's clean mobility push are creating urgency for Perodua to show a credible future roadmap.

Industry watchers have praised the C2's sleek design and tech integration, with many noting it represents a mature leap forward for the brand. KLIMS 2026, running through June 21 at MITEC, continues to draw large crowds eager to see what Malaysia's automotive future holds.
