PAS Says Hamzah's Return as Opposition Leader Reflects Majority Will

Politics The Star 15 June 2026

PAS has defended the reinstatement of Datuk Seri Hamzah Zainudin as Opposition Leader, asserting that the decision reflects the majority will of Perikatan Nasional (PN) lawmakers. Senior PAS officials, including Datuk Seri Saifuddin Nasution, confirmed that consultations with allied MPs had produced a clear consensus in Hamzah's favour.

Ahmad Samsuri Mokhtar, who briefly held the Opposition Leader post following Hamzah's departure in May, will continue as PN chairman — a distinction that separates the coalition's administrative leadership from its parliamentary function. Party officials were keen to emphasise that there is no rivalry between the two men and that PN remains cohesive.

The move has been interpreted by political observers as a reaffirmation of PAS's growing authority within PN following Bersatu's internal turmoil. Having steered through two months of uncertainty, PAS emerged from the episode with its grip on the opposition bloc strengthened.

Critics within Bersatu questioned whether Hamzah's new party, Parti Wawasan Negara, has sufficient representation to justify the role. However, PAS countered that the position is awarded based on parliamentary numbers and broad coalition support, not any single party's internal preferences.

With Hamzah back in his role, observers say PN can now refocus on presenting a united front ahead of the next general election cycle and upcoming state polls. Parliament is expected to reconvene soon, giving the reinstatement immediate practical significance for opposition parliamentary strategy.
