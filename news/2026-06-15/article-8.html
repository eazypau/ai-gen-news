Malaysian Badminton Pairs Eye Quarter-Finals at BWF Australian Open 2026

Sports New Straits Times 15 June 2026

Two newly formed Malaysian mixed doubles pairs are advancing strongly at the BWF Australian Open 2026 (Sathio Group Australian Badminton Open), held at the Quaycentre in Sydney. Both pairs are poised for a potential quarter-final clash, setting up what could be a memorable all-Malaysia showdown deep in the tournament.

Among the pairs, Pang Ron and Pei Jing have been in dominant form, dispatching Australian opponents convincingly in the early rounds with scores of 21-17 and 21-14. Their performance has drawn praise from BAM officials, who note the partnership has gelled quickly despite being relatively new.

The Australian Open is part of the BWF World Tour calendar and holds Super 300 status, making it an important points-gathering event for players seeking to qualify for the year-end World Tour Finals. Malaysian shuttlers have historically performed well at the Australian Open, with several national players having claimed titles at the event in recent editions.

National number one mixed doubles player Toh Ee Wei has been recovering from injury and is not competing at this tournament, but her longtime partner Chen Tang Jie is participating with a new pairing that has shown considerable promise. Badminton Association of Malaysia (BAM) selectors are watching closely as they finalise the squad for upcoming World Championship trials.

Malaysia's presence at the Australian Open reflects BAM's strategy to rotate and develop new talent on the international circuit. Results from this tournament will feed into national ranking assessments and selection considerations for the 2026 Asian Games in Aichi-Nagoya, Japan, scheduled for later this year.
