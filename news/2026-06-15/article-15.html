Malaysian AI Startups Gain Global Spotlight at Science x AI Summit 2026

Technology MySinChew 15 June 2026

Malaysian AI entrepreneur Chow Kit Hui attended the prestigious Science x AI Summit 2026 held in Silicon Valley, California, representing Malaysia's growing artificial intelligence enterprise ecosystem on the global stage. The summit, held on May 12, brought together leading AI researchers, founders, and investors from around the world.

Key discussion topics at the summit included AI Agents, autonomous reasoning systems, research-grade AI development, and the future of computing power architecture — themes that align closely with Malaysia's own national AI agenda under the Madani government's digital economy push. Chow Kit Hui engaged in panel discussions and bilateral meetings with global AI counterparts during the event.

Malaysia's AI startup ecosystem has gained significant traction in 2026, with Kuala Lumpur increasingly recognised as a regional technology hub for fintech, health tech, edtech, and mobility solutions. Companies like JurisTech and Fundaztic have demonstrated that Malaysian firms can compete internationally in regulated technology verticals, not merely in consumer applications.

The government's MDEC (Malaysia Digital Economy Corporation) has been actively facilitating international exposure for Malaysian tech founders, including sponsored participation at global summits and accelerator partnerships with Silicon Valley, London, and Tokyo-based innovation centres. Budget 2026 allocated additional funding for AI talent development and startup internationalisation.

Chow Kit Hui described the summit as a major networking milestone for the Malaysian AI community and said the experience would inform new product development directions for her company upon returning to Malaysia. She called on more Malaysian AI founders to seek international exposure, noting that global recognition helps attract both talent and investment to the local ecosystem.
