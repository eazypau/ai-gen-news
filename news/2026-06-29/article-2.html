PM Anwar Urges Malaysia to Accelerate Drone and AI Technology Adoption

Prime Minister Datuk Seri Anwar Ibrahim delivered a strong call to action at the closing ceremony of MyDrone Expo 2026 (MDX2026), urging Malaysia to move swiftly in adopting and developing drone and artificial intelligence technologies to secure a competitive position in the global economy. The three-day expo, held at the Malaysia Agro Exposition Park Serdang (MAEPS), drew delegates from 46 countries and featured over 100 exhibition booths.

Anwar highlighted that the global UAV (unmanned aerial vehicle) market is projected to surpass USD 55 billion by 2030, with the emerging low-altitude economy increasingly shaping worldwide investment trends. He noted that countries that delay adoption risk being left behind as UAVs transform agriculture, logistics, infrastructure inspection, and defence sectors globally.

The Prime Minister pledged that Putrajaya will strengthen regulatory frameworks, increase research and development investment, and foster greater collaboration between government agencies, universities, and the private sector to accelerate Malaysia's drone industry ecosystem. He emphasised the importance of local talent development to sustain long-term growth in the sector.

MDX2026, co-organised by the World UAV Federation Malaysia Chapter and the Malaysia UAV Development Association, showcased six dedicated zones covering raw materials, component manufacturing, enterprise deployments, defence applications, electric vertical take-off, and autonomous flight systems. The expo attracted significant interest from both domestic startups and established multinational companies.

Industry observers say Malaysia is well-positioned to become a regional hub for drone innovation, given its strategic location, skilled workforce, and existing aerospace manufacturing base. The government's clear policy direction and PM Anwar's personal backing are expected to accelerate investment flows into the sector in the coming months.
