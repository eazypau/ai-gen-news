DAP Names Four Candidates for Johor State Election

The Democratic Action Party (DAP) has announced four candidates to contest in the 2026 Johor state election as part of Pakatan Harapan's broader strategy for the July 11 polls. The announcement follows weeks of internal deliberations as PH allocated seats among its component parties — DAP, PKR, Amanah, and UPKO — to mount a coordinated challenge across Johor's 56 constituencies.

DAP is expected to focus its resources on urban and semi-urban seats with significant Chinese and mixed-ethnic voter populations, where the party has historically performed strongly. The four chosen candidates reflect DAP's intention to target winnable seats rather than contest in areas where the party's support base is weak.

The announcement adds to a growing list of confirmed candidacies from all major parties, intensifying competition across multiple constituencies. With BN fielding candidates in all 56 seats and PH running independently rather than in seat-sharing arrangements, the contest is notably more fragmented than the 2022 Johor election.

DAP leaders have expressed confidence in their chosen candidates, citing their local community work and policy track records. The party is calling on supporters to unite around the Pakatan Harapan ticket and reject what it characterises as the regressive politics of the Perikatan Nasional opposition bloc.

Political analysts note that DAP's performance in the Johor election will be a key indicator of whether the party has maintained its urban support base following years of governing alongside UMNO at the federal level. The result could influence intra-coalition dynamics within Pakatan Harapan going forward.
