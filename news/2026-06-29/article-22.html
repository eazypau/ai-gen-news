Johor Election: Interactive Guide to All 56 State Seats and What's at Stake

The Star has published an in-depth interactive guide covering all 56 constituencies in the 2026 Johor state election, providing voters with detailed profiles on each seat's demographics, incumbents, candidate lists, and historical voting patterns. The guide is designed to help Malaysia's 2.72 million registered Johor voters make informed decisions ahead of polling day on July 11.

The interactive resource highlights key battleground seats where BN, PH, and PN are locked in the tightest contests, and provides data-visualised breakdowns of how ethnic composition, urbanisation levels, and youth voter percentages may influence outcomes. Seats in Iskandar Puteri, Johor Bahru city, and the rapidly developing Bandar Nusajaya area are identified as crucial swing zones where no single party has a clear advantage.

Data shows that Johor's registered voter base has grown significantly since the 2022 election, boosted by Undi18 lowering the voting age and automatic voter registration. Young voters aged 18–30 now make up a substantial proportion of the electorate in many urban and semi-urban seats, and their preferences are seen as highly unpredictable by party strategists.

The guide also tracks multi-cornered contests, including the three five-way races and twelve four-way battles, to help voters understand how vote-splitting dynamics might influence their preferred candidate's chances. In several seats, the difference between winning and losing is expected to come down to only a few hundred votes.

Political journalists and academics have praised the resource as a valuable public service, noting that informed voters are better equipped to hold elected representatives accountable. The guide will be updated in real-time on polling night to show live results as they come in from returning officers across Johor's 56 constituencies.
