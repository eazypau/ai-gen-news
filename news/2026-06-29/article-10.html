Malaysian Barista Nas Jaafar Makes History Winning World Brewers Cup 2026

Malaysia's Nas Jaafar has etched his name into coffee history by winning the 2026 World Brewers Cup, becoming the first Malaysian ever to claim the prestigious title. He outperformed elite competitors from Australia, Hong Kong, the Czech Republic, South Korea, and France in what was hailed as one of the most competitive editions of the championship in recent years.

Nas's journey to the world stage began with his victory at the Malaysia Brewers Cup in 2025, earning him the right to represent the country internationally. His winning routine showcased a meticulously crafted brew from a single-origin Malaysian coffee bean, paired with a compelling narrative about Malaysia's growing specialty coffee scene.

The victory has sent shockwaves through the global coffee community, with specialty coffee enthusiasts and café owners from around the world turning their attention to Malaysia. Travel and tourism analysts say the win could give a significant boost to coffee tourism in Malaysia, attracting visitors interested in exploring the cafés and farms behind the country's award-winning brews.

Malaysia's café culture has experienced explosive growth over the past decade, with specialty coffee shops proliferating in Kuala Lumpur, Penang, and Johor Bahru. Nas's win validates the talent and passion of the country's barista community, many of whom have quietly built world-class skills through years of dedicated competition and training.

Reactions from the Malaysian coffee community have been overwhelmingly enthusiastic. The Malaysia Specialty Coffee Association praised Nas's dedication and said his win would inspire a new generation of Malaysian baristas to pursue excellence on the international stage. Prime Minister Anwar Ibrahim also extended congratulations to Nas via social media, calling the achievement a source of national pride.
