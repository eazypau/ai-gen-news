Batik Air Launches First Direct Tashkent–Langkawi Flight for Visit Malaysia Year

Batik Air has launched a direct weekly flight service from Tashkent, Uzbekistan to Langkawi International Airport, making it the first direct air connection between Central Asia and one of Malaysia's premier island destinations. The new route is expected to open Langkawi to a growing market of Central Asian tourists who have demonstrated increasing interest in Southeast Asia's nature, wellness, and halal tourism offerings.

The launch forms part of a broader effort to diversify Malaysia's tourist source markets and reduce reliance on traditional arrivals from Singapore, China, and Australia. Central Asian travellers — particularly from Uzbekistan, Kazakhstan, and neighbouring countries — are emerging as higher-yield visitors with strong spending power and a preference for destinations with strong Islamic credentials.

Langkawi has been actively positioning itself as a halal-friendly and eco-tourism destination, leveraging its UNESCO Global Geopark status, pristine beaches, and accessible duty-free shopping to attract diverse visitor profiles. The new Tashkent route provides Langkawi with a direct pipeline to a market that was previously only reachable via multi-stop itineraries.

Tourism Malaysia signed a Memorandum of Collaboration with Batik Air as part of Visit Malaysia Year 2026, committing the airline to rolling out expanded flights from key Asian markets. The Tashkent–Langkawi service is the latest in a series of new routes announced under this partnership, which also includes connections from Thailand and other new markets.

Langkawi's tourist arrival numbers have been climbing steadily throughout 2026, with Visit Malaysia Year promotional campaigns contributing to record monthly arrivals in several months. Stakeholders on the island are optimistic that the new international routes will sustain momentum and position Langkawi as a globally recognised island destination by the end of the year.
