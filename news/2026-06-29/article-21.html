PERKESO and 7-Eleven Malaysia Sign MOU to Support Worker Reintegration Nationwide

Social Security Organisation PERKESO and 7-Eleven Malaysia have formalised a strategic partnership through a Memorandum of Understanding (MOU), aimed at supporting the rehabilitation, employability, and workforce reintegration of PERKESO-insured persons under the Return to Work (RTW) Programme. The signing marks a significant step in public-private collaboration to address long-term employment challenges for Malaysians recovering from workplace injuries or health conditions.

Under the partnership, eligible RTW participants will be placed in suitable positions within 7-Eleven's nationwide store network, giving them structured re-employment pathways with appropriate support and monitoring. 7-Eleven Malaysia, which operates thousands of outlets across the country, provides a uniquely wide geographic reach that can accommodate participants from diverse locations and backgrounds.

Both organisations committed to welcoming the first cohort of RTW programme participants before the end of 2026, with plans to progressively scale the programme to more locations and participants over time. The initiative will be supported by PERKESO's rehabilitation team, who will provide job coaching and ongoing support to placed employees.

The partnership reflects a broader national strategy to improve labour market inclusivity and reduce the economic burden of long-term disability by facilitating re-entry into employment. Human Resources Minister Sivakumar Varatharajah praised the collaboration as a model for how private sector partners can contribute meaningfully to social safety net objectives.

PERKESO Director General Datuk Seri Mohd Sahar Darusman expressed confidence that the programme would improve the quality of life for beneficiaries, many of whom struggle with the psychological and financial challenges of long-term absence from the workforce. The RTW programme has helped over 18,000 Malaysians return to employment since its inception.
