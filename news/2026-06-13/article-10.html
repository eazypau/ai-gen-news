Injured Toh Ee Wei Refuses to Shut Door on World Championship Title Defence

National mixed doubles ace Toh Ee Wei has defiantly refused to rule out defending her BWF World Championship title in New Delhi, insisting she is fighting to recover from her current injury in time to compete. The 26-year-old world champion, who has been sidelined from competition for several weeks following a knee complaint, told the Malay Mail she remains determined to give herself the best possible chance of being fit for the prestigious event.

Toh, who partnered mixed doubles specialist Chen Tang Jie to claim the world title, admitted the injury has been frustrating and that she has had to exercise considerable patience during her rehabilitation. She is currently working with the Badminton Association of Malaysia (BAM) sports medicine team and has been undergoing intensive physiotherapy at the BAM training centre in Bukit Kiara.

Despite the setback, Toh said she has not given up on her other major target for the year: representing Malaysia at the 2026 Asian Games in Aichi-Nagoya, Japan. The Asian Games are scheduled for September 2026, giving her a potential window of several weeks to recover from the World Championships in August and compete at the Games if selected.

BAM secretary-general Kenny Goh said the association is closely monitoring Toh's recovery and will make selection decisions based on medical clearance and fitness assessments closer to the World Championship nomination deadline. Goh acknowledged that BAM would not risk aggravating her injury by rushing her back too early, but said the door remained open if she can demonstrate full fitness.

Malaysian badminton fans have rallied around Toh on social media, with the hashtag #RecoverEeWei trending across Twitter and Instagram. Her coach, national mixed doubles coach Tan Chun Seang, expressed confidence that her mental resilience and competitive drive give her the best chance of returning stronger from the injury. The badminton fraternity will be watching her recovery progress with considerable interest in the weeks ahead.
