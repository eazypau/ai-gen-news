FIFA World Cup 2026: Malaysians Can Watch Every Match Free on RTM

The 2026 FIFA World Cup — the largest in the tournament's history with 48 participating nations — kicked off on 11 June across stadiums in Canada, Mexico and the United States, and Malaysian football fans have the opportunity to watch all 104 matches live and free via RTM's terrestrial television channels. The arrangement ensures that the World Cup remains accessible to all Malaysians regardless of pay-TV subscriptions.

The expanded tournament format, introduced for the first time at this edition, features four groups of 12 teams in a revamped group stage before moving to a 32-team knockout round. The new format was designed by FIFA to maximise commercial revenues and include more teams from Asia, Africa, and North and Central America's CONCACAF region. Asian representation at the tournament is at an all-time high.

Malaysia did not qualify for the World Cup but the national team's performance in the AFC third-round qualifiers earlier in 2025 was considered a creditable step forward under coach Kim Pan-gon's successor. Malaysian fans are largely backing their preferred Southeast Asian nation Singapore, which qualified for the first time in the country's history, creating significant regional excitement.

For the Malaysian time zone, most group-stage matches are scheduled at convenient early morning and late evening slots, with flagship games on weekend mornings. Sports bars and mamak stalls across Kuala Lumpur, Penang, and Johor Bahru have already registered record pre-orders for World Cup viewing packages and are preparing large outdoor screens to accommodate crowds.

BERNAMA reported that RTM has signed a sublicensing agreement with FIFA's regional broadcaster to ensure comprehensive coverage including commentary in Bahasa Malaysia. FIFA streaming rights on the RTM Sports digital platform will also be available, allowing Malaysians to watch matches on mobile devices and computers. The final is scheduled for 19 July 2026 at the Los Angeles Memorial Coliseum.
