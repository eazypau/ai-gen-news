HAWANA 2026 Fun Walk Draws 1,500 Participants, Closes Registration Early

The inaugural HAWANA 2026 Fun Walk, held in conjunction with National Journalists' Day (Hari Wartawan Nasional or HAWANA), attracted an impressive 1,500 participants when it was held on 12 June 2026 in Kuala Lumpur. The event was so popular that organisers were compelled to close registration ahead of schedule due to the overwhelming response from media professionals, their families and members of the public.

The fun walk, which wound through a scenic route in the heart of Kuala Lumpur, was organised to celebrate the important role that journalists and media workers play in Malaysian society. Participants donned matching event T-shirts and were treated to refreshments, entertainment and a post-walk prize-giving ceremony. The atmosphere was described by attendees as festive and inclusive.

Malaysian Communications and Multimedia Commission (MCMC) chairman Datuk Mohamad Salim Fateh Din, who flagged off the event, praised the media fraternity for its resilience and dedication in an era of rapid digital transformation and rising disinformation. He called on journalists to uphold the highest ethical standards and to harness new technologies, including AI-powered tools, responsibly in service of the public interest.

National Journalists' Day is observed annually on 12 June to coincide with the founding date of the National Union of Journalists (NUJ) Malaysia. This year's celebrations had a special flavour, with the fun walk being a new addition to the traditional programme of forums, award presentations and networking dinners that mark the occasion.

The success of the fun walk has prompted organisers to announce that it will become a permanent fixture in future HAWANA celebrations, with plans already afoot to expand the route and raise the participant cap for the 2027 edition. Senior media executives and government ministers expressed hope that the event would continue to strengthen bonds within the journalism community and raise public appreciation for the work of news professionals.
