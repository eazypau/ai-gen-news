Malaysia & Japan Strengthen Cooperation in Hi-Tech Investments: AI, Quantum & Semiconductors

Malaysia and Japan have agreed to significantly deepen their strategic cooperation in high-technology investments, spanning quantum computing, artificial intelligence, and semiconductor manufacturing. The commitment was made during Prime Minister Datuk Seri Anwar Ibrahim's business roundtable in Tokyo on 9 June 2026, where he met with chief executives from some of Japan's most prominent technology conglomerates.

PM Anwar underscored that Malaysia's industrial trajectory is shifting decisively toward advanced manufacturing and digital infrastructure, positioning the country as a regional hub for high-technology production and computing. He highlighted Malaysia's complementary strengths in semiconductor assembly, testing and packaging, which dovetail well with Japan's global leadership in semiconductor materials, precision equipment and advanced fabrication processes.

Both governments have agreed to explore the establishment of joint research laboratories, talent exchange programmes, and co-investment structures to facilitate the transfer of knowledge and capital. Japanese companies including major electronics manufacturers and materials suppliers expressed strong interest in expanding their Malaysian operations within technology parks in Penang, Selangor and Johor.

Malaysia currently has 33 active semiconductor-related capital projects worth approximately US$20 billion. The government sees Japan as a crucial partner in helping Malaysia move up the semiconductor value chain, from back-end packaging toward more advanced front-end chip design and fabrication activities over the medium term.

The New Straits Times reported that follow-up technical working groups between ministries of the two countries are expected to convene within the next 60 days to operationalise the cooperation framework. The Malaysian Investment Development Authority (MIDA) is understood to be preparing a tailored investment facilitation package for Japanese technology firms looking to establish or expand Malaysian operations.
