Alibaba Cloud Opens New Public Cloud Region in Johor

Digital News Asia 14 June 2026

Alibaba Cloud has officially launched its latest public cloud region in Johor, marking a significant expansion of Malaysia's digital infrastructure and the Chinese tech giant's footprint in Southeast Asia. The new region, featuring multiple availability zones, will provide businesses and government agencies across Malaysia with lower latency cloud services and improved data sovereignty compliance.

The Johor launch is part of Alibaba Cloud's broader strategy to capitalise on the region's booming demand for cloud computing, particularly as Malaysia positions itself as a digital hub for Southeast Asia. The facility, located within the Iskandar Malaysia economic zone, was built in partnership with the Johor state government and is expected to create over 1,000 direct and indirect technology jobs in the state.

For Malaysian enterprises, the new cloud region means access to Alibaba Cloud's full portfolio of services — including elastic computing, AI-powered analytics, database management and cybersecurity solutions — from a locally hosted infrastructure. This addresses longstanding data residency concerns among Malaysian businesses in regulated sectors such as banking, healthcare and government services.

The Johor region also strengthens Malaysia's position as a bridge between Greater China's tech ecosystem and the broader ASEAN market. Several Malaysian fintech and logistics startups have already signed agreements to migrate workloads to the new region, attracted by preferential pricing for early adopters and access to Alibaba Cloud's AI development platforms.

Malaysia's Minister of Digital welcomed the launch as "a vote of confidence in Malaysia's digital readiness and regulatory maturity." The minister noted that with multiple global cloud providers now operating local regions, Malaysia is among the top three cloud adoption markets in Southeast Asia. Further cloud region announcements from other major providers are expected before the end of 2026.
