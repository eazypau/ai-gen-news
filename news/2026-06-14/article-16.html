K-pop Legends EXO Set to Rock Kuala Lumpur This June

Buro 247 Malaysia 14 June 2026

Global K-pop phenomenon EXO is making a long-awaited return to Malaysia this June, marking their first performance in Kuala Lumpur in six years. The group's comeback tour, which follows their much-publicised reunion in early 2026 after a hiatus, has become one of the most hotly anticipated concert events in Malaysia this year, with all available tickets selling out within hours of going on sale last month.

The concert, scheduled at Stadium Axiata Arena in Bukit Jalil, is expected to draw an audience of 12,000 fans per night across two consecutive performances. Event organisers have reported unprecedented demand from fans not just within Malaysia but from across Southeast Asia, with ticket resellers listing sold-out tickets at multiples of the face value — a testament to EXO's enduring popularity with their dedicated "EXO-L" fanbase.

EXO's return comes at a buoyant time for Malaysia's live entertainment industry, which has rebounded strongly after the pandemic-era venue closures. The KL concert market in 2026 has already seen major performances from artists including Coldplay, Taylor Swift's additional Asia dates and BLACKPINK's solo members, with EXO's arrival capping what industry insiders are calling the best concert season Malaysia has seen in over a decade.

The concert production promises to be a spectacle, featuring a full arena stage set with state-of-the-art LED panels, pyrotechnics, and what organisers describe as "the most elaborate runway stage EXO has ever used in Southeast Asia." Setlists from the tour's earlier stops in Seoul and Tokyo have included a mix of classic hits and tracks from the group's recently released comeback album, giving fans both nostalgia and new material to celebrate.

Beyond the concerts themselves, EXO's KL visit is expected to generate a boost for the local tourism and hospitality sector, with hotels in the Bukit Jalil vicinity reporting near-full occupancy for the concert weekend. Korean cultural exchange groups and Malaysian K-pop community organisations have also planned a series of fan events, pop-up merchandise markets and meetups in the days surrounding the concert, turning the EXO visit into a broader cultural celebration.
