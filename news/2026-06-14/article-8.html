Collins Aerospace Quadruples MRO Footprint in Malaysia with US$63M Expansion

RTX / New Straits Times 14 June 2026

RTX's Collins Aerospace has unveiled a US$63 million expansion of its maintenance, repair and overhaul (MRO) facility at Subang Aerotech Park in Shah Alam, Selangor, quadrupling its operational capacity in Malaysia. The investment represents the single largest MRO expansion announcement in the country this decade and cements Malaysia's growing reputation as a regional aerospace services hub.

The expanded facility will significantly increase Collins Aerospace's ability to service a wide range of aircraft systems, including avionics, actuation systems and nacelle components for major commercial airline operators across Asia. With air travel continuing its strong post-pandemic recovery across Southeast Asia, demand for MRO services in the region is projected to grow at 6-8% annually through 2030.

The expansion is expected to create approximately 400 new highly skilled positions in areas including aerospace engineering, quality assurance and precision manufacturing. Collins Aerospace's Malaysian workforce will receive specialised training at the company's facilities in the United States, further developing the local talent pool for Malaysia's aerospace industry.

Malaysian Investment Development Authority (MIDA) CEO Datuk Wira Arham Abdul Rahman said the Collins Aerospace investment validates Malaysia's strategy of positioning itself as a preferred destination for high-value aerospace investment. He noted that Malaysia's aerospace sector now employs more than 20,000 workers and contributes over RM4 billion annually to the national economy, with MRO services accounting for the largest share.

The Subang Aerotech Park, where the expansion is based, is home to several other international aerospace companies including CTRM, Asia Aviation Technic and a number of Tier 1 suppliers. Government officials have announced plans to further develop the park's infrastructure and connectivity to attract additional aerospace investment before the end of the 12th Malaysia Plan period.
