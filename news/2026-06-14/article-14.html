Malaysian Pair Pang Ron-Pei Jing Advance at Australian Badminton Open

New Straits Times 14 June 2026

Newly formed Malaysian mixed doubles partnership Pang Ron and Pei Jing made an impressive start at the Australian Badminton Open in Sydney, defeating the home crowd's Australian pair Michael Owen and Gronya Somerville 21-17, 21-14 in the first round. The result was an encouraging sign for a pairing that has only been playing together since the start of the 2026 season following a series of partner realignments within the national squad.

The win at the Quaycentre took approximately 42 minutes and showed the pair's growing chemistry in terms of court coverage, attack transitions and net play. National coach Rosman Razak praised the performance after the match, noting that Pang Ron's sharp cross-court smashes and Pei Jing's solid defensive work at the net complemented each other well, particularly in the second game when the pair raised their intensity after a brief mid-game wobble.

The Australian Open is a BWF Super 300 event and provides valuable ranking points as the international badminton calendar heads into its second half of the season. Malaysian badminton fans are watching the new mixed doubles partnerships closely ahead of the World Championships in New Delhi and the Aichi-Nagoya Asian Games — two tournaments that BAM has identified as priorities for the national squad in 2026.

The pair will face a stiffer test in the quarter-finals, where they are scheduled to meet the second-seeded Chinese pair, who have been in dominant form throughout 2026. BAM officials have said they see the Australian Open as an important developmental tournament for the new partnerships, with the emphasis on gaining match experience rather than purely chasing results at this stage of the season.

Meanwhile, two other newly formed Malaysian mixed doubles pairs also progressed past the first round in Sydney, setting up a potential all-Malaysian quarter-final clash — a scenario that would be a significant boost for BAM's mixed doubles programme after a period of transition. Fans on social media have already begun debating which pairings should receive the most support ahead of the Asian Games selection trials in August.
