Malaysia Mega Sale 2026 Kicks Off June 15 — Deals Nationwide

BizToday News / Tourism Malaysia 14 June 2026

The Malaysia Mega Sale Carnival 2026 officially opens its doors tomorrow, June 15, ushering in seven weeks of nationwide discounts running through to July 31. The annual shopping extravaganza, organised by the Ministry of Tourism, Arts and Culture in collaboration with retail associations and e-commerce platforms, is positioned to be the largest edition yet, with over 15,000 participating retailers across brick-and-mortar and digital channels.

Shoppers can expect discounts of up to 80% across fashion, electronics, home furnishings, food and beverage, travel packages and beauty products. Major shopping malls in Kuala Lumpur, Penang, Johor Bahru and Kota Kinabalu will be anchor venues for the campaign, each featuring flagship sale events, live entertainment and food festivals designed to extend the shopping experience beyond the transactional.

The 2026 edition places particular emphasis on the integration of the Malaysia Mega Sale with the digital retail ecosystem, following the explosive growth of e-commerce in Malaysia over the past five years. Platforms including Shopee, Lazada, TikTok Shop and Zalora are participating as official online partners, hosting dedicated Mega Sale storefronts with exclusive flash sales, live-stream shopping events and buy-now-pay-later promotions timed to drive customer acquisition and loyalty.

The Malaysia Mega Sale coincides with the Malaysia Festival Kuala Lumpur, which runs throughout June and July, making KL particularly attractive to international visitors. Tourism Malaysia has been promoting the combined shopping-and-culture package to travellers from the Gulf Cooperation Council (GCC) countries, China, Indonesia and India, countries that collectively represent Malaysia's highest-spending inbound tourist demographics.

Minister of Tourism, Arts and Culture Datuk Seri Tiong King Sing set a target of RM4.5 billion in total consumer spending during the Mega Sale period, a significant increase over last year's RM3.8 billion. He urged Malaysians to "shop Malaysian first" during the sale, highlighting that the campaign also features a dedicated "Buy Malaysian" section showcasing homegrown brands across food, fashion and crafts — an initiative designed to support local SMEs and showcase Malaysia's creative economy.
