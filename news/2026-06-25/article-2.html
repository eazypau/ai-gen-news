BN Johor Candidate List Stirs Internal UMNO Controversy

The Vibes | 25 June 2026 | Politics

An UMNO leader has publicly hinted that the Barisan Nasional candidate list for the 2026 Johor state election is not without fault, describing the selections as having "something not right." The remarks, made ahead of nomination day on June 27, have added a layer of internal tension to BN's campaign preparations as the coalition contests all 56 seats solo without a formal coalition partner.

The unnamed UMNO leader suggested that certain seat allocations and candidate choices do not fully reflect grassroots sentiment, with some long-serving divisional leaders reportedly passed over in favour of loyalists closer to the party leadership hierarchy. Sources within the state UMNO machinery say the selections have caused frustration among several wings of the party in key constituencies.

Johor Menteri Besar Onn Hafiz Ghazi, who is helming BN's campaign, has sought to downplay the controversy, urging party members to rally behind the official list and focus on securing a mandate for continued development in the state. He maintained that all candidate selections went through a thorough vetting process and were made in the best interest of BN's electoral prospects.

The internal friction within UMNO echoes similar tensions seen in previous state elections, where candidate selection disputes occasionally resulted in rogue candidates contesting under independent banners. Party insiders say leaders are working quickly to resolve any grievances before nomination day to prevent vote-splitting in closely contested seats.

With Pakatan Harapan and Perikatan Nasional both fielding full slates, BN cannot afford to have disgruntled members sabotage its campaign from within. The outcome of the Johor election is widely expected to influence the national political landscape ahead of the next general election, making party unity an essential condition for BN's hopes of retaining the state government.
