Theta Edge Berhad Chairman Tan Sri Abd Rahman Mamat Resigns

The Edge Malaysia | 25 June 2026 | Business

Theta Edge Berhad, a Malaysian IT services and solutions provider listed on Bursa Malaysia, announced on Wednesday that its chairman, Tan Sri Abd Rahman Mamat, has resigned from his position effective June 22, 2026. The company disclosed the development via a filing with the stock exchange, noting that the resignation was a personal decision by Tan Sri Abd Rahman.

Tan Sri Abd Rahman had been a key figure in the company's governance and strategic direction, and his departure marks one of the most significant leadership changes at Theta Edge in recent years. The company has not yet announced a replacement chairman, stating that the board of directors will initiate a formal process to identify and appoint a suitable successor in due course.

Theta Edge is a wholly-owned subsidiary of Telekom Malaysia Berhad and serves as the group's IT arm, providing services including cloud computing, cybersecurity, data management, and enterprise solutions to both public sector clients and private corporations across Malaysia. The company has been involved in several major government digitalisation initiatives under Malaysia's digital economy agenda.

Market observers said the sudden departure raised questions about potential changes in strategic direction at the company, particularly in the context of TM Group's broader push into AI-driven data centre infrastructure and digital services. Theta Edge's stock moved modestly on the news before stabilising, as investors assessed the near-term impact of the leadership change on existing contracts and project pipelines.

The company assured shareholders and stakeholders that all ongoing projects and operations will continue without disruption, with the existing management team maintaining full oversight of day-to-day activities. Theta Edge said it remains committed to delivering on its strategic roadmap and supporting Malaysia's ambitions as a regional digital hub.
