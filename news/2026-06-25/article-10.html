Luno Lists 29 New Digital Assets in Malaysia's Largest Ever Crypto Expansion

Fintech News Malaysia | 25 June 2026 | Technology

Cryptocurrency exchange Luno has announced the listing of 29 new digital assets for trading in Malaysia, representing the largest single batch of new listings on the platform in the country's history. The expansion significantly broadens the range of cryptocurrencies available to Malaysian investors on the Securities Commission-registered exchange.

The newly listed assets include a mix of established layer-1 blockchain tokens, decentralised finance (DeFi) protocols, and infrastructure tokens that have demonstrated strong liquidity and community adoption on global markets. Luno said the selection process was rigorous and included due diligence reviews covering the asset's legal standing, security track record, and compliance with SC Malaysia's digital asset framework.

The timing of the expansion coincides with a surge in crypto market activity globally and in Malaysia specifically. Trading volumes on regulated Malaysian digital asset exchanges jumped 23% in 2025 to over USD 4.3 billion, and the trend has continued into 2026 as mainstream institutional and retail interest in digital assets grows. An estimated four million Malaysians are projected to actively use cryptocurrencies in 2026.

Investors under the age of 35 continue to dominate Malaysia's digital asset market, making up approximately 62% of all digital asset account holders. Luno's expanded offering is seen as a strategic move to cater to this demographic's appetite for diversified crypto exposure, as many younger investors seek alternatives to traditional investment products like unit trusts and fixed deposits.

Luno Malaysia's country manager noted that the 29 new listings represent the company's commitment to providing Malaysian investors with access to globally competitive investment options within a fully regulated and secure environment. The exchange said it will continue to evaluate and add digital assets that meet its listing criteria as Malaysia's crypto ecosystem matures.
