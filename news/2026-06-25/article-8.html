TM Nxera's AI-Ready Data Centre Campus in Johor to Launch Phase 1 in 2026

Telekom Malaysia | 25 June 2026 | Technology

TM Nxera, the data centre and digital infrastructure arm of Telekom Malaysia Berhad, has confirmed that the first phase of commercial operations at its state-of-the-art, hyperconnected, AI-ready data centre campus in Iskandar Puteri, Johor, is on track to launch in 2026. The campus represents one of the most significant investments in digital infrastructure in Southeast Asia this decade.

The facility is designed to meet the exponentially growing demand for high-performance computing, artificial intelligence workloads, and cloud services across Malaysia and the broader ASEAN region. Connectivity for the campus will be underpinned by the subsea cable networks of both Telekom Malaysia and Singtel Group's Digital InfraCo, providing ultra-low latency links to Singapore, Hong Kong, and global internet exchange points.

The Iskandar Puteri campus is part of Malaysia's broader strategy to position the Johor-Singapore Special Economic Zone (JS-SEZ) as a leading digital and technology hub in Southeast Asia. The proximity to Singapore offers significant advantages for hyperscaler clients seeking sovereign Malaysian data residency while maintaining seamless connectivity to one of the world's most connected financial and technology centres.

TM Nxera said the campus will be built to Tier IV standards — the highest level of reliability and uptime for data centre infrastructure — and will incorporate sustainability features including renewable energy integration, advanced cooling technologies, and water-efficient systems. The company aims to achieve carbon neutrality across its data centre operations by 2030.

Several major technology companies and hyperscalers have already expressed interest in collocating operations at the Iskandar Puteri campus, according to industry sources. With Malaysia's data centre market growing rapidly on the back of increased AI adoption, cloud migration by enterprises, and government digitalisation initiatives, TM Nxera's Johor campus is positioned to capture a significant share of regional demand.
