Johor Election July 11: Pakatan Harapan Unveils 56 Candidates

The Star | 25 June 2026 | Politics

Prime Minister Datuk Seri Anwar Ibrahim personally unveiled Pakatan Harapan's full slate of 56 candidates for the 2026 Johor state election on Sunday, signalling the coalition's serious intent to challenge the incumbent Barisan Nasional government in its traditional stronghold. The announcement was made at a major gathering in Johor Bahru, attended by senior leaders from PKR, DAP, and Amanah.

Among the notable candidates confirmed for the election is Maszlee Malik, who will contest the Puteri Wangsa seat under the PKR banner. His inclusion is seen as a strategic move by the coalition to attract progressive and youth voters in the constituency. Several new faces were also introduced alongside experienced incumbents, reflecting the coalition's efforts to balance continuity with fresh appeal.

The Election Commission has set nomination day for June 27, with early voting on July 7 and polling day on July 11. All 56 seats of the Johor State Legislative Assembly are being contested following the dissolution announced by Menteri Besar Onn Hafiz Ghazi on June 1. The dissolution was signed by the Regent of Johor with the consent of the King and Sultan Ibrahim Iskandar.

Pakatan Harapan faces a tough battle against BN's entrenched network in Johor, a state that has historically been an UMNO stronghold. However, the coalition believes that national sentiment favourable to the unity government, combined with cost-of-living concerns and infrastructure development promises, gives them a genuine shot at wresting control of the state assembly.

Political analysts note that the Johor election serves as a key barometer for national politics, with both BN and PH needing strong showings ahead of the next general election. Perikatan Nasional is also fielding candidates across all 56 seats, setting up a three-cornered fight that could split opposition votes and benefit the incumbent administration.
