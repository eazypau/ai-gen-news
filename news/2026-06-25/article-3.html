Onn Hafiz Rules Out Coalition Government for Johor After BN Arrogance Claims

Malay Mail | 25 June 2026 | Politics

Johor Menteri Besar Onn Hafiz Ghazi has categorically ruled out forming a coalition state government following the July 11 election, reaffirming Barisan Nasional's decision to contest all 56 seats independently. He acknowledged that BN's solo stance had drawn accusations from rival parties of arrogance and racial insensitivity but dismissed these characterisations as politically motivated.

Onn Hafiz argued that BN's track record in Johor — highlighted by infrastructure growth, economic zones like the Johor-Singapore Special Economic Zone (JS-SEZ), and improvements to public services — justifies contesting alone. He said forming a coalition government would complicate decision-making and create instability for the state's long-term development agenda.

The Menteri Besar's statement comes in the wake of criticism from Pakatan Harapan lawmakers who accused BN of displaying arrogance by refusing to enter a formal alliance, particularly given the fractured political landscape in the state. Opposition leaders have urged voters to consider whether a solo BN administration would adequately represent the diverse ethnic and regional interests across Johor's 56 constituencies.

BN's position is notably different from the federal level, where it forms part of the unity government under PM Anwar Ibrahim. Onn Hafiz has been careful to frame the state's approach as a pragmatic decision based on local conditions, rather than a rejection of the unity government model at the national level.

Political observers note that BN's performance in the Johor election will serve as a significant test for the party's relevance and drawing power in its own backyard. A poor performance could reinvigorate calls for broader coalition-building, while a strong majority would vindicate the party's confidence in going it alone in Malaysia's most economically vibrant southern state.
