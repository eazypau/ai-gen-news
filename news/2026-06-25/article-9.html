Malaysia Advances Ringgit Stablecoin Testing Framework for 2026

Fintech News Malaysia | 25 June 2026 | Technology

Bank Negara Malaysia (BNM) is advancing its plans to test ringgit-backed stablecoins and tokenised deposits within a controlled regulatory sandbox framework in 2026. The central bank confirmed that greater regulatory clarity on ringgit-denominated digital money is expected to be issued before the end of the year, following the conclusion of the testing phase.

The stablecoin testing initiative is focused primarily on wholesale financial applications and cross-border payment use cases, rather than retail consumer transactions. BNM views ringgit stablecoins as a potential mechanism for reducing friction and settlement times in international trade payments, particularly for Malaysia's extensive trade relationships within the ASEAN bloc and with GCC countries.

Malaysia's approach follows similar regulatory sandbox initiatives in Singapore, the United Arab Emirates, and the European Union, where central banks and financial regulators have been exploring the use of tokenised money to modernise payment infrastructure. The ringgit stablecoin framework is part of a broader national digital financial infrastructure strategy aimed at keeping Malaysia at the forefront of financial technology innovation in Southeast Asia.

The Securities Commission Malaysia (SC) has also been active in building complementary regulatory frameworks for digital assets, having registered six Recognised Market Operators (RMOs) to operate digital asset exchanges in Malaysia. The combined efforts of BNM and the SC are designed to create a coherent regulatory environment that supports responsible innovation while managing the risks associated with digital financial instruments.

Industry participants welcomed the announcement, with fintech companies and banks alike expressing interest in participating in the stablecoin sandbox. The Malaysian Fintech Association said that clarity on the regulatory treatment of tokenised money would significantly accelerate the adoption of blockchain-based payment solutions by Malaysian financial institutions and corporate treasuries.
