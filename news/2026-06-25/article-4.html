Fuel Prices Reduced for Week of June 25–30, 2026

Bernama | 25 June 2026 | Economy

The Ministry of Finance announced on Wednesday that retail prices for RON95 petrol and unsubsidised diesel have been reduced for the week of June 25 to June 30, 2026. The adjustment follows a notable decline in average international oil market prices, which the government's weekly fuel pricing mechanism is designed to reflect.

Under Malaysia's managed float pricing system, fuel prices are reviewed and adjusted weekly based on movements in global crude oil and refined fuel product prices. The latest reduction provides modest but welcome relief to Malaysian motorists who have faced elevated fuel costs over much of the first half of the year due to geopolitical tensions affecting global supply chains.

The Finance Ministry's announcement did not specify the exact per-litre reduction figures but confirmed the cuts apply to both RON95 — the most widely used petrol grade among Malaysian consumers — and the unsubsidised diesel variant sold at market prices. The subsidised diesel price for eligible Malaysians enrolled in the targeted subsidy scheme remains unchanged.

Transportation and logistics industry players welcomed the reduction, noting that even marginal decreases in fuel costs help to manage operating expenses, particularly for last-mile delivery operators and public transport providers. The Malaysian Motor Traders Association said the adjustment would ease some pressure on independent vehicle dealers and workshop operators who rely heavily on road transportation.

Economists caution that global oil prices remain subject to volatility, given ongoing tensions in the Middle East and continued supply uncertainty from major producers. The Ministry of Finance said it will continue to monitor international market conditions and adjust retail prices accordingly, in line with its commitment to balancing consumer affordability with fiscal sustainability.
