Five Men Arrested Over Disappearance of Two Teenage Girls in Machang

The Sun | Crime | 22 June 2026

Police in Kelantan have arrested five local men in connection with the disappearance and subsequent rescue of two teenage girls in an operation conducted in Machang. The girls, both minors, were reported missing by their families several days prior to the rescue, prompting a coordinated police response involving multiple units from the state contingent.

The rescued teenagers were found in a location linked to the detained suspects and are currently receiving medical attention and counselling support from the Social Welfare Department. Police have confirmed they are in stable condition. Investigators are now building a case against the five suspects, who face potential charges under multiple provisions of the Penal Code and the Child Act 2001.

Kelantan Police Chief Datuk Muhamad Zaki Harun said the swift resolution of the case was a result of close cooperation between the public, who provided vital tip-offs, and specialised police units including the Sexual Crimes Investigation Unit and the Women and Child Investigative Division. He praised the community for coming forward with information.

The case has sparked renewed public debate about child safety measures and the adequacy of existing frameworks for protecting vulnerable young people. Child rights advocates have called on the government to review and strengthen laws governing child exploitation and to increase funding for awareness programmes targeting at-risk youth communities.

Social media attention on the case has been intense, with Malaysians expressing outrage and demands for the full force of the law to be applied against those found guilty. Police have urged the public to refrain from sharing unverified information about the suspects or the victims online, to protect the integrity of the ongoing investigation and the privacy of the rescued girls.
