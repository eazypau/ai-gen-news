New MyKad Launches With 53 Security Elements Including Holograms and QR Codes

Malay Mail | Technology | 22 June 2026

The National Registration Department (NRD) has launched an extensively upgraded MyKad featuring 53 distinct security elements — more than double the 23 elements on the previous generation card. The enhanced national identity card incorporates laser engraving, ultraviolet (UV) reactive elements, sophisticated holographic patterns, guilloche microprint, QR codes for law enforcement use, and a chip with advanced encryption technology.

Home Minister Datuk Seri Saifuddin Nasution Ismail said the new MyKad represents one of the most significant upgrades to Malaysia's national identity system in over two decades. He underscored that the revamped card will dramatically reduce the risk of identity fraud, document forgery, and illegal impersonation, which have been growing concerns in recent years.

Notably, the new MyKad will no longer carry the Touch 'n Go payment function, which had been embedded in previous versions. Officials explained that this function is being phased out in favour of dedicated contactless payment cards and digital wallet solutions, streamlining the card's focus to identity verification and citizen services.

The rollout will begin with new applicants and those renewing expired cards, with existing cardholders given a transition period. The NRD has established additional service counters and extended operating hours at major registration offices nationwide to accommodate anticipated demand during the initial rollout phase.

Cybersecurity experts have commended the upgrade, noting that the new card's chip encryption meets international ISO standards for identity documents. However, they also called on the government to ensure that backend systems handling MyKad data are equally fortified against digital threats, as the card becomes increasingly central to government service access and verification.
