Rais Yatim Named Chairman, Saifuddin Abdullah Appointed Secretary-General

Malay Mail | Politics | 22 June 2026

Veteran statesman and former cabinet minister Rais Yatim has been appointed as the new chairman of the party, while Indera Mahkota Member of Parliament Saifuddin Abdullah has been named its secretary-general. The appointments mark a significant shift in the party's leadership structure and are expected to influence its strategic direction in the coming months.

Rais Yatim, a seasoned politician with decades of experience spanning multiple administrations, brings considerable gravitas and institutional knowledge to the chairman's role. His appointment is seen as a move to stabilise the party and leverage his extensive network within Malaysia's political landscape.

Saifuddin Abdullah, known for his diplomatic background and progressive outlook, takes on the administrative leadership as secretary-general at a time when the party is navigating complex coalition dynamics within Malaysia's current political environment. His experience as a former foreign minister is expected to prove valuable in managing intra-party and inter-party relations.

Political observers noted that the combination of Rais Yatim's seniority and Saifuddin's operational acumen presents the party with a strong leadership team capable of articulating a coherent vision ahead of future electoral contests. The duo is expected to focus on grassroots engagement and policy articulation.

The announcement was met with a mix of cautious optimism and scrutiny from various quarters. Critics urged the new leadership to address longstanding concerns within the party's membership, while supporters expressed confidence that the appointments would inject fresh momentum into the organisation's activities.
