Police Seize 57 Motorcycles in Integrated Perak Road Safety Operation

The Sun | Crime | 22 June 2026

Police in Perak have seized 57 motorcycles during a targeted integrated road safety operation conducted along Jalan Jelapang-Silibin, one of the busiest arterial roads in the Ipoh metropolitan area. The operation, which involved officers from the Traffic Investigation and Enforcement Department (TIED), targeted motorcyclists committing offences under Section 64(1) of the Road Transport Act 1987.

Among the offences recorded during the crackdown were riding without a valid licence, failure to wear a helmet, illegal modifications to exhaust systems, and riding vehicles with expired road tax or insurance. Officers also issued summonses to motorcyclists who failed to adhere to lane discipline and road markings, which have been identified as key contributors to accidents on the stretch.

Perak Police Chief Datuk Seri Mohd Yusri Hassan Basri said the operation reflects the force's commitment to reducing the number of road fatalities in the state, which has recorded a higher-than-average rate of motorcycle-related accidents in recent years. He called on all road users to respect traffic regulations and reminded motorcyclists of the lethal consequences of dangerous riding behaviours.

Community leaders in the Jelapang area have welcomed the crackdown, noting that the road has long been a hotspot for illegal racing and reckless riding, particularly late at night and during weekends. Residents have previously petitioned authorities to increase enforcement presence along the stretch.

The seized motorcycles will be impounded pending payment of fines and compliance with regulatory requirements. Owners who fail to settle their outstanding summonses within the stipulated period risk having their vehicles auctioned. Police said further operations are planned for other high-risk roads in Perak as part of a statewide road safety campaign.
