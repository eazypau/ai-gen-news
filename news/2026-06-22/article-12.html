Pickleball Fever: ODN Malaysia Open 2026 Wraps Up in Shah Alam With RM100,000 Prize

The Sun | Sports | 22 June 2026

The inaugural Oriental Daily News (ODN) Malaysia Open 2026 pickleball tournament concluded successfully at the Grand Pickleball Arena in Shah Alam, capping off three days of competitive action and entertainment. The event featured a total prize pool of RM100,000 and attracted players across Junior, Novice, Intermediate, and Open categories from across Malaysia and the region.

Pickleball, a paddle sport that combines elements of badminton, table tennis, and tennis, has experienced explosive growth in Malaysia over the past two years. Club memberships have surged, with courts springing up in recreation centres, sports complexes, and private clubs in major cities, reflecting a demographic appeal that spans age groups from teenagers to senior citizens.

The three-day tournament saw intense competition in the Open category, with top seeds from Kuala Lumpur, Selangor, and Penang battling it out for the lion's share of the prize money. Organisers reported strong spectator turnout throughout all three days, with many attending their first pickleball event after discovering the sport through social media and community groups.

Tournament director Tan Boon Keat said the ODN Malaysia Open is intended to become an annual fixture on the Malaysian sports calendar, with plans to expand the event and invite international players from ASEAN countries in future editions. He credited Sin Chew Daily's parent group, Oriental Daily News, for their sponsorship commitment in elevating the sport's profile.

The Pickleball Association of Malaysia (PAM) said the tournament's success reflects the sport's rapid professionalisation and the growing interest of corporate sponsors. PAM president Lim Ah Kow announced plans to petition the Olympic Council of Malaysia to include pickleball in the National Sports Council's priority development programme.
