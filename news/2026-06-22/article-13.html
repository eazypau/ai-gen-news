Toh Ee Wei Refuses to Close Door on World Championship and Asian Games

New Straits Times | Sports | 22 June 2026

National badminton star Toh Ee Wei, the reigning world number one in mixed doubles, has refused to rule out competing at the upcoming BWF World Championships in New Delhi and the 2026 Asian Games in Aichi-Nagoya, Japan, despite battling an injury that has kept her off the court in recent weeks. The 26-year-old expressed cautious optimism about her recovery timeline.

Toh sustained the injury during training following her triumphant World Championship title run, and her availability for the upcoming tournaments has been the subject of intense speculation among Malaysian badminton fans. Badminton Association of Malaysia (BAM) officials have maintained a measured stance, prioritising her long-term health over short-term tournament targets.

Speaking through BAM's media office, Toh said she is working intensively with the national team's physiotherapy unit and is making steady progress. She added that she remains fully motivated and that the thought of defending her world title and competing for her first Asian Games medal is a powerful source of inspiration during her rehabilitation.

Her mixed doubles partner, Peck Yen Wei, has continued training with a replacement partner to maintain form and readiness. BAM has said a final decision on Toh's participation will be made closer to the tournament qualification deadlines, based on assessments by the team medical staff.

Malaysian badminton fans have rallied behind Toh on social media, flooding her accounts with messages of support and recovery wishes. Sports analysts note that her presence or absence will significantly impact Malaysia's medal prospects in mixed doubles at both prestigious events, given her world-class status and the chemistry she has built with Peck Yen Wei.
