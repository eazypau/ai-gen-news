Malaysian Army Suspends Hand Grenade Training After Fatal Kedah Blast

New Straits Times | Politics | 22 June 2026

The Malaysian Army has temporarily suspended all hand grenade training activities across its facilities following a fatal explosion that occurred during a live training session at Hobart Camp in Kedah. The incident claimed the lives of two military personnel and left others injured, sending shockwaves through the defence establishment and prompting an immediate halt to similar exercises nationwide.

Army Chief General Tan Sri Muhammad Hafizuddeain Jantan confirmed that a formal investigation has been launched to determine the cause of the tragedy. Preliminary reports suggest a malfunction during a live grenade exercise, though officials cautioned against drawing conclusions before the full investigation is complete. The victims' families have been notified and are receiving full support from the military.

The suspension will remain in place until investigators produce a comprehensive safety review of all grenade training protocols. Senior officers from the Army's Safety and Training Directorate have been deployed to Hobart Camp to assist with the inquiry and to review the adequacy of existing safety procedures.

Defence Minister Datuk Seri Mohamed Khaled Nordin expressed his deepest condolences to the families of the fallen soldiers, pledging that their sacrifice would not be in vain. He called on the Army to use the tragedy as an opportunity to strengthen training safety standards across all branches of the Malaysian Armed Forces.

The incident has reignited debates over military training safety in Malaysia, with opposition lawmakers calling for an independent parliamentary review of the armed forces' training protocols. The Army has assured the public and parliament that full transparency will be maintained throughout the investigation process.
