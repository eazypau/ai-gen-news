Subsidised Diesel Price Cut to RM2.10 per Litre from July 2026 via MyKad Verification

SoyaCincau | Lifestyle | 22 June 2026

Prime Minister Datuk Seri Anwar Ibrahim has announced that the price of subsidised diesel will be reduced to RM2.10 per litre nationwide starting from July 2026, following the government's review of fuel subsidy distribution. The subsidy will be administered through a MyKad-based verification system, similar to the BUDI MADANI RON95 (BUDI95) programme for petrol, to ensure benefits flow only to eligible Malaysians.

The announcement brings significant relief to millions of Malaysians who depend on diesel-powered vehicles and equipment, particularly farmers, fishermen, and transport operators. The RM2.10 price represents a reduction from the previous subsidised rate and is below the current market price, with the difference absorbed by the government through the subsidy mechanism.

Finance Minister Tengku Zafrul Abdul Aziz said the MyKad verification requirement is essential to prevent subsidy leakage to non-eligible parties, including foreign-registered vehicles and commercial operators who do not qualify for the targeted assistance. He noted that leakage in the previous open-market subsidy system had cost the government billions of ringgit annually.

The implementation will require diesel pump operators to install MyKad readers at their stations, a transition that the government has pledged to support with technical and financial assistance. Station operators in rural areas have been given additional lead time to comply, given infrastructure constraints in remote regions.

Consumer groups have cautiously welcomed the move, though some expressed concern about the administrative burden on small businesses that use diesel for generators and agricultural equipment. The government said it will establish a dedicated helpline and digital portal to assist individuals and businesses in navigating the new subsidy registration and verification process.
