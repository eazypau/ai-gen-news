Malaysians Can Watch All 104 FIFA World Cup 2026 Matches on RTM and Unifi TV

Malay Mail | Sports | 22 June 2026

Malaysian football fans are celebrating after it was confirmed that all 104 matches of the 2026 FIFA World Cup — the first edition to feature 48 nations — can be watched via free-to-air and subscription platforms operated by RTM and Unifi TV. The arrangement marks a historic departure from the previous two decades, during which pay-TV broadcaster Astro held an effective monopoly on World Cup coverage in Malaysia.

RTM will broadcast select matches on its terrestrial channels, while Unifi TV subscribers will have access to every fixture across multiple dedicated channels. The platforms have invested significantly in production infrastructure to handle the unprecedented scale of coverage, with matches being played across venues in the United States, Canada, and Mexico.

For fans without Unifi subscriptions, RTM's free-to-air coverage ensures that key matches — including all knockout rounds and the final — will be accessible to all Malaysians regardless of income level. This democratic approach to broadcasting rights has been widely praised by football enthusiasts and consumer advocates.

The 2026 FIFA World Cup kicked off on June 12, and Malaysian supporters have been closely following the progress of Asian teams in the tournament. There is particular interest in the performances of regional sides as Asia's allocation of berths was expanded for this edition, raising hopes for stronger showing from the continent.

Sports bars, mamak restaurants, and community viewing centres across Malaysia are reporting record crowds for World Cup screenings, with the festive atmosphere reminiscent of previous tournaments. The nationwide excitement has provided a boost to food and beverage outlets and hospitality businesses recovering from earlier economic pressures.
