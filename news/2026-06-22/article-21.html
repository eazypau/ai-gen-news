100-Foot Dragon Boat from China Sets Record at 2026 Malacca River Carnival

Sin Chew Daily | Culture | 22 June 2026

A spectacular 100-foot dragon boat from Yiyang, Hunan province in China, has made its maiden voyage on the Malacca River, setting a new record as the longest dragon boat ever showcased in Malaysia and the longest to sail on the Malacca River. The magnificent vessel served as the centrepiece of the opening ceremony for the 2026 Malacca River Carnival, drawing thousands of spectators to the historic riverside.

The Malacca River Carnival, an annual celebration of the state's rich multicultural heritage and maritime history, chose the dragon boat as a symbol of the deep historical and cultural ties between Malaysia and China. Officials from Yiyang city attended the ceremony alongside Malacca Chief Minister Datuk Seri Ab Rauf Yusoh, underscoring the bilateral cultural significance of the event.

Dragon boat racing has a long tradition in Malaysia, particularly among the Chinese community, and the spectacle of the record-setting vessel attracted media coverage from across the region. Organisers said the boat required special logistical arrangements to transport from Hunan and was assembled at a designated staging area near the river mouth before its ceremonial launch.

The 2026 Malacca River Carnival runs over several weekends throughout June and July, featuring cultural performances, traditional crafts exhibitions, culinary showcases, and heritage tours along the UNESCO-recognised waterway. Tourism officials are projecting a significant increase in visitor arrivals to Malacca during the festival period compared to 2025.

Local cultural organisations have praised the inclusion of the dragon boat as a symbol that bridges Malaysian Chinese heritage and contemporary cultural diplomacy between Malaysia and China. Plans are underway to make the dragon boat a permanent cultural exhibit in Malacca following the conclusion of the carnival, pending discussions with the vessel's owners in Yiyang.
