K-pop Legends EXO to Perform in Kuala Lumpur After Six-Year Hiatus

Buro 247 | Entertainment | 22 June 2026

Iconic K-pop group EXO will make their highly anticipated return to Malaysian shores this June, performing in Kuala Lumpur for the first time in six years. The concert is one of the most talked-about entertainment events of 2026 in Malaysia, with fans who have waited through pandemic-related delays and the group's various hiatus periods finally getting their reunion with the legendary ensemble.

EXO, formed by SM Entertainment in 2011, rose to global superstardom with hits including 'Growl', 'Monster', and 'Ko Ko Bop'. The group's Kuala Lumpur concert is part of a broader Asia tour following the group's activities after members completed their mandatory military service obligations, which had temporarily paused group activities.

Tickets for the Kuala Lumpur date sold out within hours of going on sale, prompting organisers to add additional seating. The event is expected to draw fans not only from Malaysia but also from neighbouring countries including Singapore, Thailand, and Indonesia, boosting local tourism and hospitality revenues.

EXO-Ls (the official EXO fan club) in Malaysia have organised elaborate fan projects including light stick coordination events, fan art exhibitions, and meet-and-greet fundraisers in the run-up to the concert. The Malaysian EXO fan community is recognised as one of the most active in Southeast Asia.

Concert promoters noted that the K-pop wave continues to be a major driver of entertainment tourism in Malaysia, with government support through Tourism Malaysia helping to position Kuala Lumpur as a premier live entertainment destination in the region. Officials expect the event to contribute millions of ringgit to the local economy.
