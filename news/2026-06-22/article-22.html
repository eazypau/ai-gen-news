Eight Sunway College A-Level Students Earn Entry to Oxford and Cambridge Universities

The Sun | Education | 22 June 2026

Eight exceptional A-Level students from Sunway College have secured admission offers to the University of Oxford and the University of Cambridge, two of the world's most prestigious academic institutions. The remarkable achievement underscores the growing calibre of Malaysia's private education sector and the effectiveness of well-structured A-Level programmes in preparing students for elite global universities.

The eight students, all Malaysian nationals, will pursue courses spanning medicine, natural sciences, engineering, law, and economics at the two storied British universities. Their acceptance into Oxbridge marks the culmination of years of academic dedication, co-curricular excellence, and preparation under the guidance of Sunway College's experienced academic faculty.

Sunway College principal Professor Graeme Wilkinson expressed immense pride in the students' accomplishments, crediting the holistic education model that blends rigorous academic preparation with critical thinking, leadership development, and community engagement. He noted that the college's Oxbridge success rate has grown steadily over the past five years.

The students' families expressed overwhelming joy at the outcome, with many sharing their journey through social media and inspiring a new generation of Malaysian students to aim for the highest educational goals. Education advocates pointed to the results as evidence that Malaysian students can compete at the very highest levels globally when given the right support and environment.

Education Minister Fadhlina Sidek congratulated the students and their teachers, noting that the achievements reflect the broader ambitions of Malaysia's national education plan 2026–2035, which prioritises academic excellence, global competitiveness, and nurturing world-class talent from within the country's education system.
