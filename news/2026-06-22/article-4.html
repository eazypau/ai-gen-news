Malaysia Sets Historic Trade Record, Surpasses RM1 Trillion in First Four Months of 2026

New Straits Times | Business | 22 June 2026

Malaysia has achieved a landmark milestone in its trade history, with total trade surpassing RM1 trillion within the first four months of 2026 — the fastest pace ever recorded. Total trade increased by 15.3 per cent year-on-year to reach RM1.127 trillion, driven by a remarkable 19 per cent surge in exports to RM609.31 billion, while imports grew 11.1 per cent to RM517.4 billion.

Trade Minister Tengku Zafrul Abdul Aziz described the achievement as a testament to Malaysia's competitive positioning in global supply chains and the government's concerted efforts to diversify export markets. He noted that the figures reflect the continued strength of Malaysia's manufacturing and commodities sectors, alongside growing service exports.

The electrical and electronics (E&E) sector remained the backbone of Malaysia's export performance, with demand for chips, semiconductors, and electronics components surging in tandem with the global artificial intelligence investment boom. Palm oil, petroleum products, and chemical products also registered strong growth during the period.

Economists cautioned that while the record figures are encouraging, Malaysia must continue to invest in higher-value industries and move up the technology value chain to sustain momentum. They highlighted the importance of trade diversification, particularly as geopolitical uncertainties in the Middle East and shifting US-China trade dynamics continue to reshape global commerce.

Bank Negara Malaysia has projected overall economic growth of between 4% and 5% for 2026, with the strong trade performance lending upside potential to those projections. The central bank will publish updated economic forecasts later this month alongside its monetary policy assessment.
