PM Anwar Thanks Putin for Russia's Consistent Stance on Gaza and Iran

Free Malaysia Today | Politics | 22 June 2026

Prime Minister Datuk Seri Anwar Ibrahim has expressed his gratitude to Russian President Vladimir Putin for Russia's consistent and principled stance on the ongoing conflict in Gaza and the crisis involving Iran. The acknowledgment came during a high-level diplomatic exchange, underscoring Malaysia's engagement with global powers on humanitarian issues in the Middle East.

Anwar reiterated Malaysia's longstanding position that a two-state solution remains the only viable path to peace in the Palestinian territories. He called on the international community, including permanent members of the UN Security Council, to exert greater pressure to bring about a ceasefire and facilitate the delivery of humanitarian aid to Gaza's civilian population.

Russia's position on Gaza, which has been broadly aligned with calls for a negotiated settlement rather than military escalation, has drawn praise from several developing nations including Malaysia. Analysts note that Kuala Lumpur's diplomatic outreach to Moscow is part of a broader effort to build multi-polar support for the Palestinian cause.

The Prime Minister also touched on the Iran situation, where international tensions have remained elevated. Anwar emphasised Malaysia's commitment to diplomacy and dialogue as the preferred means of resolving conflicts, warning that military confrontation would have dire consequences for regional and global stability.

Malaysia's foreign policy on the Middle East has remained active under the Madani government, with Putrajaya hosting several diplomatic delegations and engaging multilateral platforms including the Organisation of Islamic Cooperation (OIC) and the United Nations to advocate for peace and justice in the region.
