Android Malware Scam Cases on the Rise, Police Warn of Government Impersonators

Sin Chew Daily / MySinchew | Crime | 22 June 2026

Malaysian police have issued an urgent public warning following a notable rise in scam cases that combine impersonation of government officials with malware deployed on victims' Android devices. At least eight cases have been reported to authorities since April 1, and investigators believe the actual number may be higher due to under-reporting driven by victims' embarrassment.

In a typical attack scenario, victims receive a phone call or message from someone claiming to be from a government agency such as the Immigration Department, Royal Malaysian Police, or the Attorney General's Chambers. The caller persuades the victim to download an app — ostensibly for official purposes — which in reality installs malware that grants the scammer remote access to the device's banking apps and personal data.

The Commercial Crime Investigation Department (CCID) has advised the public to never download apps at the request of individuals claiming to be government officers, and to always verify such claims through official government hotlines. They also stressed that legitimate government agencies will never request sensitive banking information over the phone.

Android users in particular are at risk because the malware typically requires installation through third-party sources outside the Google Play Store, exploiting Malaysia's significant user base of affordable Android smartphones. Cybersecurity experts have urged users to enable Google Play Protect, avoid sideloading apps, and install reputable security software.

Bank Negara Malaysia has also issued guidelines requiring banks to implement additional verification steps for suspicious transactions, and has urged customers to immediately contact their bank's fraud hotline if they suspect their accounts have been compromised. The central bank is coordinating with police on a task force to pursue the criminal networks behind the scam operations.
