Singer 2026 Malaysia Lineup Announced: Jaclyn Victor, Priscilla Abby and More

Astro has officially unveiled the six debut singers for Singer 2026 – Malaysia, the local edition of the popular Chinese music competition format, with acclaimed artistes Jaclyn Victor, Priscilla Abby, and Vanessa Reynauld headlining a diverse and star-studded cast.

Joining them are Geraldine Gan, Uriah See, and Nicole Lai, completing a group of six Malaysian artistes who bring a broad spectrum of musical styles, cultural backgrounds, and performance strengths to the competition stage. The lineup spans genres from pop ballads and R&B to folk and Mandopop, reflecting Malaysia's vibrant and multicultural music scene.

Jaclyn Victor, a household name in Malaysian entertainment following her win on Malaysian Idol in 2004, is considered one of the country's most powerful vocalists and her participation is expected to draw significant viewership from older audiences who grew up watching her career. Priscilla Abby, known for her strong theatrical performances and international competition experience, is expected to challenge viewers with more adventurous song choices.

Vanessa Reynauld brings a contemporary pop sensibility to the competition, while Geraldine Gan, a veteran of the Mandarin music circuit, is expected to deliver technically flawless performances. Uriah See and Nicole Lai represent the newer generation of Malaysian musical talent, having built significant followings through digital platforms and independent music releases before joining the Singer roster.

The show will air on Astro beginning later this month, with judges drawn from Malaysia's music industry. Producer comments indicate that the Malaysian edition will retain the format's signature combination of celebrity mentors, live competition episodes, and public voting, adapted for local sensibilities. Entertainment analysts predict Singer 2026 Malaysia could be one of the most-watched local productions on Astro this year, capitalising on the global resurgence of music competition formats.
