Lee Zii Jia Called Up for Malaysia's BWF Thomas Cup Finals Squad

Veteran shuttler Lee Zii Jia, currently ranked world No. 83 after a difficult 18 months, has been recalled to the Malaysian national team squad for the 2026 BWF Thomas Cup Finals, in a move that has surprised some observers but reflects the team's need for experienced campaigners at the highest level.

Lee, who rose to world No. 1 in 2021–2022 and won multiple Super 1000 tournaments during his peak years, has been struggling with form and injury during the past season. His ranking dropped sharply after a series of early-round exits at marquee events, raising questions about whether the Kedah-born player would regain his best level before reaching his athletic prime years.

The Badminton Association of Malaysia (BAM) confirmed that Lee's selection is based on his overall capability when at his best, his big-match experience, and the assessment that the Thomas Cup team needs his firepower as an attacking singles player who has the ability to beat anyone on his day. Lee expressed gratitude for the call-up and promised to give his all to help Malaysia reclaim the Thomas Cup, which the country last won in 2023.

The 2026 BWF Thomas Cup Finals squad also includes current national hero Ng Tze Yong, who has been in excellent form this season, as well as a younger generation of players from the national youth pipeline. The competition is expected to feature strong challenges from Indonesia, China, and South Korea, all of whom have been investing heavily in their men's singles depth.

Badminton remains one of Malaysia's strongest sporting exports, with a deep cultural connection to the game that spans all communities. A strong Thomas Cup campaign would provide a significant morale boost for the national sports establishment, particularly as Malaysia prepares to send its largest-ever team to the Glasgow 2026 Commonwealth Games in late July.
