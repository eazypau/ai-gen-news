FIFA World Cup 2026 Quarter-Finals: Malaysian Fans Follow Quarter-Final Drama

With the FIFA World Cup 2026 quarter-finals now underway in the United States, Canada, and Mexico, Malaysian football fans are staying up through the night to catch the action, with broadcasters RTM and Unifi TV both providing free live coverage of the tournament's knockout stages.

The 2026 edition of the World Cup expanded to 48 teams for the first time in tournament history, meaning the group stage and round of 32 produced far more matches and surprises than previous editions. For Malaysian viewers, most quarter-final matches are scheduled to kick off between midnight and 6am Malaysian time, leading to a wave of social media commentary about bleary-eyed fans making sacrifices for the beautiful game.

RTMKlik, the national broadcaster's streaming platform, has reported record concurrent viewers during the knockout stage matches, demonstrating significant pent-up demand for live international football among the Malaysian public. Sports bars and mamak restaurants in Kuala Lumpur, Penang, and Johor Bahru have also reported packed crowds for late-night screenings, with many staying open until dawn to accommodate fans.

The tournament has drawn particular interest in Malaysia due to the strong showings of several Asian teams, including Japan, South Korea, and Saudi Arabia, all of which qualified from their groups and have attracted widespread support from Malaysian fans who see Asian football as a collective endeavour. The performance of these nations has sparked fresh discussion about whether Malaysia should accelerate its own football development programme to become competitive at future World Cups.

The World Cup final is scheduled for July 19–20, 2026, and will be broadcast live across all RTM channels and Unifi TV at 3am Malaysian time. The Football Association of Malaysia has confirmed it will host a public viewing event at the National Stadium in Bukit Jalil for the final match, with free admission for the public.
