Frasers Group Sells Sports Direct Malaysia Stake for $150 Million

UK retail giant Frasers Group has announced the sale of its entire 100% interest in Sports Direct Malaysia to its existing partner MAP Active for approximately $150 million, exiting the Malaysian market as part of a broader strategic review of its Asian retail portfolio.

Frasers Group, led by billionaire Mike Ashley, said the sale allows the group to streamline its international operations and focus capital on its core UK and European markets, where it continues to operate a large network of Sports Direct, House of Fraser, and Flannels stores. The Malaysian operation, which operates dozens of stores across the country, will continue to trade under the Sports Direct brand under its new sole owner MAP Active, the sports and lifestyle retail division of the Mitra Adiperkasa Group.

MAP Active is one of Southeast Asia's largest sports retail operators, with extensive presence in Indonesia, Singapore, Thailand, and the Philippines in addition to Malaysia. The acquisition of full control over Sports Direct Malaysia strengthens MAP Active's footprint in one of its strategically important markets, where a growing young, health-conscious consumer base is driving demand for international sportswear and equipment brands.

Sports Direct Malaysia currently holds distribution rights for numerous premium sports brands in the country, including Nike, Adidas, Under Armour, and Puma in certain retail formats. The chain operates stores in major shopping malls across Kuala Lumpur, the Klang Valley, Penang, Johor Bahru, and East Malaysia, catering to both professional athletes and recreational sports participants.

Analysts noted that the deal reflects wider consolidation trends in Southeast Asian sports retail, where scale and exclusive brand partnerships are becoming critical competitive advantages. MAP Active's full ownership of the Malaysia operation simplifies its management structure and gives it greater flexibility to invest in store refurbishments, digital commerce capabilities, and exclusive product ranges for the Malaysian market.
