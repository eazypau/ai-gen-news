AIBP Enterprise AI Conference Opens in Kuala Lumpur July 8–9

Southeast Asia's leading enterprise technology forum, the AIBP Conference and Exhibition Malaysia, opens at the W Hotel Kuala Lumpur on July 8 and 9, with the central theme of scaling artificial intelligence responsibly and demonstrating measurable return on investment for corporations.

The two-day conference will bring together over 600 C-suite executives, technology leaders, and government officials from across Malaysia and the broader ASEAN region to discuss how enterprises can move beyond AI pilot projects to production-scale deployment. Sessions will cover AI governance frameworks, workforce impact and reskilling, cybersecurity risks in AI systems, and sectoral applications in banking, manufacturing, healthcare, and retail.

A highlight of the event will be the announcement of the 2026 AIBP Enterprise Innovation Awards Malaysia, which recognise companies that have achieved the most impactful and measurable AI deployments in the past year. Finalists span industries from financial services, where several banks have deployed AI for fraud detection and credit scoring, to the manufacturing sector, where AI-driven predictive maintenance has delivered significant cost savings.

The conference also addresses growing concerns about AI's impact on employment. A panel titled "AI as Partner, Not Replacement" will feature human resources leaders and economists discussing how companies can manage workforce transitions transparently, with findings from a recent IBM Malaysia study showing that 80% of Malaysian CEOs now view AI sovereignty and local talent development as essential to their long-term strategy.

Minister of Digital is expected to deliver a keynote address on the government's newly launched Digital Action Plan 2030, using the AIBP platform to engage directly with the private sector on implementation priorities and the regulatory environment for AI in Malaysia. The event is expected to generate significant deal-making activity, with technology vendors, startups, and investors expected to engage in bilateral meetings on the conference sidelines.
