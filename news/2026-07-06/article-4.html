PH Promises 250,000 Jobs and Shenzhen-Style Transformation for Johor

Pakatan Harapan has launched its Johor State Election manifesto, anchored by an ambitious pledge to create 250,000 jobs and transform Johor into a high-technology economic powerhouse modelled after China's Shenzhen technology hub.

The manifesto, unveiled at a rally in Johor Bahru, outlines PH's vision of leveraging Johor's strategic location adjacent to Singapore to attract high-value foreign direct investment, particularly in semiconductors, advanced manufacturing, data centres, and green energy. The coalition promises that, if elected, it would work closely with the federal government to fast-track approvals for the Johor-Singapore Special Economic Zone, which is seen as the centrepiece of the state's economic future.

PH also pledged to address the cost-of-living pressures facing Johor residents — particularly in housing, where property prices near the Singapore border have become increasingly unaffordable for local buyers. The manifesto includes commitments to build 50,000 affordable homes within five years and to establish a first-homebuyer assistance scheme for Johoreans below the age of 40.

Other headline promises include free public bus services in Johor Bahru, the expansion of public hospital capacity by 2029, and the creation of a Johor Digital Talent Academy to upskill workers for technology sector jobs. PH also committed to cleaning up state-linked corporations and publishing detailed audits of their finances within the first 100 days of any new state government.

Political analysts noted that the Shenzhen comparison is aspirational rather than immediately achievable, but they acknowledged that the framing is effective in signalling a bold economic vision to voters who feel the state has underperformed its potential despite years of being governed by the same political machinery. Whether voters translate aspiration into ballot support remains the key question for July 11.
