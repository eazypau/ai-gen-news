Johor Election: 172 Candidates Set to Contest 56 State Seats on July 11

The 2026 Johor State Election is five days away, with the Election Commission confirming that 172 candidates will be vying for all 56 seats in the Johor State Legislative Assembly on polling day, 11 July 2026.

The election was called after the dissolution of the Johor State Assembly in early June, with nomination day completed last Saturday. Early voting is scheduled to take place on July 7, allowing eligible early voters — including police, military personnel and their spouses — to cast their ballots ahead of the main polling day.

The contest is being closely watched at the national level as a bellwether for public sentiment ahead of the 16th General Election, which must be held by early 2028. In the previous state election held on 12 March 2022, Barisan Nasional secured a commanding two-thirds supermajority, winning 40 of 56 seats. Political analysts are now observing whether the same coalition can repeat that performance or whether opposition coalitions Pakatan Harapan or Perikatan Nasional can make significant inroads.

Key issues dominating the campaign include affordable housing, employment opportunities particularly for Johor's growing young workforce, infrastructure development in districts bordering Singapore, and the proposed Johor-Singapore Special Economic Zone. Both the ruling coalition and opposition parties have released their respective manifestos pledging economic transformation, job creation, and social welfare improvements tailored to Johor residents.

Political observers note that campaign dynamics this election cycle appear more contested than in 2022, with all three major coalitions fielding credible candidates and mounting aggressive ground campaigns. Voter turnout will be a key variable — higher turnout historically benefits opposition parties, while lower turnout tends to consolidate support for incumbents who have stronger grassroots machinery.
