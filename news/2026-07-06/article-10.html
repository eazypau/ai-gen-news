Omirol Launched as Southeast Asia's First AIGC Creation Platform

The 2026 Malaysia AI Innovation and Development Exchange Forum, held in Kuala Lumpur on July 3, culminated in the official launch of Omirol, which positions itself as Southeast Asia's first native AI-Generated Content (AIGC) creation ecosystem platform, developed in partnership with China's V Valley industrial chain.

AIGC platforms use generative artificial intelligence to enable creators — including individuals, brands, and media companies — to produce video, audio, images, and written content at scale using AI tools. Omirol aims to be the regional hub for this emerging creative economy, targeting Malaysia's growing creator community as well as enterprise clients who want to automate content production for marketing, e-commerce, and social media.

The platform is designed to support multilingual content creation, with particular strength in Bahasa Malaysia, Mandarin, and English — the three dominant commercial languages of the Malaysian market. Omirol's founders described the platform as "creator-first," meaning the AI tools are designed to augment human creativity rather than replace it, with human creators retaining copyright over AI-assisted works produced on the platform.

The China-Malaysia partnership behind Omirol reflects the broader deepening of technology ties between the two countries, following several large Chinese technology investments in Malaysia's data centre and AI infrastructure sectors. The V Valley industrial chain, which provides Omirol with its core AI technology stack, is known for its work in generative video and voice synthesis in the Chinese market.

Industry observers noted that while the AIGC sector is highly competitive globally, with established players from the US, China, and Europe, Malaysia's regulatory environment — which is relatively permissive compared to the EU's AI Act — and its ASEAN gateway position give a locally headquartered platform a potential structural advantage in winning Southeast Asian enterprise customers who want regional data sovereignty and localised AI models.
