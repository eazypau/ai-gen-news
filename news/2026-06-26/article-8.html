AI Nation 2030: MDEC Celebrates 30 Years Accelerating Malaysia's Digital Future

The Edge Malaysia · June 2026 · Technology

The Malaysia Digital Economy Corporation (MDEC) is marking its 30th anniversary in 2026 as Malaysia advances toward the ambitious AI Nation 2030 goals. Established in 1996 to spearhead the development of the Multimedia Super Corridor (MSC), MDEC has evolved over three decades from a promoter of basic ICT adoption into a global-level orchestrator of digital economy transformation.

Under the current Malaysia Digital (MD) initiative, MDEC has been the catalyst for attracting world-class investments in artificial intelligence, cloud computing, cybersecurity and advanced manufacturing. Global leaders across high-value AI technology stacks have established Malaysian operations, helping to shape a local ecosystem where AI is developed responsibly and adopted widely across industries.

A key pillar of MDEC's AI Nation 2030 strategy is enabling local companies to expand globally rather than simply serving as a destination for foreign technology firms. MDEC has been funding and mentoring Malaysian technology companies with the potential to become regional champions, particularly in areas such as fintech, healthtech, agritech and e-government solutions.

The corporation also emphasises inclusion in its vision, with programmes targeting communities in rural Malaysia, Sabah and Sarawak, and underserved populations who risk being left behind by the digital transition. Digital literacy, affordable internet access and micro-entrepreneur e-commerce enablement are central to MDEC's grassroots strategy.

As Malaysia looks toward 2030, the AI Nation roadmap targets a significant increase in the country's digital economy contribution to GDP, more high-value jobs created in technology sectors, and a Malaysian AI governance framework that earns international recognition. MDEC's CEO noted that at 30 years, the corporation is "just getting started" on Malaysia's most ambitious digital chapter yet.
