EC Sets Johor State Election for July 11, Negeri Sembilan on August 1

Malay Mail · 12 June 2026 · Politics

The Election Commission of Malaysia (EC) has officially gazetted the 2026 Johor State Legislative Assembly election for July 11, with polling for the Negeri Sembilan State Legislative Assembly set for August 1. The two elections mark significant political tests for Malaysia's Unity Government coalition ahead of the next federal general election, which must be held by 2027.

The Johor election was triggered by the dissolution of the state assembly on June 1, 2026, signed by the Regent of Johor with the consent of the King of Malaysia and the Sultan of Johor. Nomination day is set for June 27, and early voting will take place on July 7 for eligible voters including military personnel, police officers and their spouses registered at certain constituencies.

All 56 seats in the Johor State Legislative Assembly will be contested. As of the updated electoral roll on May 31, 2026, there are 2,727,926 registered voters eligible to participate in the Johor state polls, making it one of the largest state elections in Malaysia's recent history by voter numbers.

The incumbent Barisan Nasional government, led by Menteri Besar Onn Hafiz Ghazi, is defending its majority against a potentially divided opposition. Pakatan Harapan, Perikatan Nasional and the Bersama coalition are each fielding their own slates, raising the possibility of multi-cornered contests in numerous seats that could split the opposition vote and benefit BN's candidates.

Political analysts see the Johor election as a bellwether for national sentiment, given the state's economic significance as home to the Johor-Singapore Special Economic Zone and a major industrial and commercial hub. Gen Z voters are expected to play an increasingly decisive role, making up approximately one-third of the eligible electorate in both Johor and Negeri Sembilan.
