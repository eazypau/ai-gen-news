Barisan Nasional to Contest All 56 Johor Seats Solo, No Wawasan Leaders Nominated

New Straits Times · 24 June 2026 · Politics

Johor Barisan Nasional has unveiled its full candidate line-up for the upcoming state election on July 11, comprising exclusively members of its three traditional component parties — UMNO, MCA and MIC. No candidates from the Wawasan coalition have been nominated, confirming that BN will contest the election on its own without extending seats to non-BN coalition partners.

The decision to go solo reflects both BN's confidence in its electoral machinery in Johor — historically one of the party's strongholds — and a strategic calculation that a clean BN ticket avoids the complications of multi-party seat negotiations. Incumbent Menteri Besar Onn Hafiz Ghazi announced the move, saying that BN has the organisation and experience to defend all 56 seats without external support.

Onn Hafiz himself will be defending the Machap state seat, which he has held since 2018. He has ruled out forming a coalition state government even if BN wins a majority, underscoring his preference for a purely BN-governed Johor. The Menteri Besar has faced criticism in some quarters for the decision not to go solo, with opponents accusing BN of displaying "arrogance" in its approach to the election.

The opposition landscape in Johor is fragmented, with Pakatan Harapan, Perikatan Nasional and the Bersama coalition all planning to field independent slates. Multi-cornered fights in many constituencies could work in BN's favour by splitting the anti-BN vote, although opposition strategists are exploring seat-sharing arrangements to minimise vote fragmentation.

With nomination day set for June 27, all candidate information will be finalised within days. Early polls suggest BN holds a comfortable lead in Johor, but political watchers caution that voter sentiment can shift rapidly in the final campaign weeks, particularly with a large cohort of first-time Gen Z voters whose party allegiances are harder to predict.
