Anwar Ibrahim Tops Approval Ratings at 52%, Merdeka Center Survey Shows

Free Malaysia Today · 25 June 2026 · Politics

Prime Minister Datuk Seri Anwar Ibrahim continues to hold the highest approval rating among Malaysia's major political leaders, with a new Merdeka Center survey released on June 25 placing his public approval at 52 per cent. The figure maintains Anwar's position at the top of the political satisfaction rankings, ahead of other federal and state leaders.

The survey, conducted across urban and rural constituencies, reflects broadly sustained confidence in the Unity Government's economic stewardship and reform agenda. Respondents cited cost of living management and anti-corruption initiatives as key factors influencing their positive assessment of the Prime Minister.

However, analysts note that while 52 per cent represents a comfortable lead in comparative terms, there remains a substantial segment of the population expressing reservations — particularly in states where opposition parties such as Perikatan Nasional hold strong grassroots support. The approaching Johor and Negeri Sembilan state elections are expected to serve as a more direct referendum on public sentiment.

Deputy Prime Ministers and senior cabinet members recorded their own approval metrics in the survey, with results broadly tracking the government coalition's performance across policy portfolios. Political observers say the timing of the survey, released ahead of nomination day for the Johor election, is likely to bolster morale within the ruling coalition.

Merdeka Center has been conducting political approval surveys in Malaysia for over two decades and is widely regarded as one of the country's most credible independent polling organisations. The survey sampled over 1,200 respondents across all 13 states and three federal territories.
