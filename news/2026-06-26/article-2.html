Petrol and Diesel Prices Reduced for June 25–30 Amid Global Market Decline

Free Malaysia Today · 25 June 2026 · Economy

The Ministry of Finance has announced a reduction in the retail prices of petrol and unsubsidised diesel for the week of June 25 to June 30, 2026, following a decline in average international crude oil and fuel market prices. The weekly adjustment is part of Malaysia's Automatic Pricing Mechanism (APM), which links domestic fuel retail prices to international benchmarks.

RON95 petrol — the most widely used grade — remains under the government's targeted subsidy scheme. The price adjustment this week primarily affects RON97 petrol and unsubsidised diesel, which are priced in line with global market movements. Drivers of vehicles running on these grades will notice a small reduction at the pump.

The Ministry attributed the price drop to softening global oil prices, which have been under pressure from easing geopolitical tensions and improved supply conditions in major producing regions. Analysts note that while the current reprieve offers some relief to consumers, global energy markets remain volatile and prices could reverse in subsequent weeks depending on OPEC+ production decisions.

Malaysia has maintained a cautious approach to fuel subsidy reform, expanding targeted subsidies to lower-income households and eligible vehicle categories while allowing market-rate pricing for higher consumption segments. The government continues to monitor inflation closely, having set a full-year inflation target of between 1.5% and 2.5% for 2026.

Motorists and logistics operators have welcomed the reduction, though industry groups have called for greater price stability and more transparency in the weekly pricing announcements. The government is expected to review the APM framework later this year as part of broader fiscal reform discussions.
