World Tapas Event WATCEFS 2026 Puts Miri, Sarawak on Global Culinary Map

Visit Sarawak / Meritz Hotel · 21 June 2026 · Lifestyle

The World National & World Asia Tapas Competition, Exhibition, Festival and Seminar (WATCEFS) 2026 was held from June 18 to 21 in Miri, Sarawak, transforming the northern Bornean city into a vibrant hub for global culinary excellence. The event drew over 3,000 visitors and delegates, more than 100 exhibitors, and 55 elite chefs representing 13 countries across Asia, Europe and the Middle East.

WATCEFS is a prestigious international culinary event that brings together world-class chefs to compete in tapas — bite-sized dishes that showcase creativity, technique and the use of local and regional ingredients. This year's edition placed a special emphasis on incorporating Sarawakian indigenous ingredients such as wild fern tips (midin), ambuyat, sago and local freshwater fish into contemporary tapas presentations, drawing international attention to Sarawak's unique culinary heritage.

The decision to host WATCEFS 2026 in Miri was a deliberate strategy by Sarawak's tourism authorities to elevate the city's profile as a culinary and cultural destination. Miri, known primarily as an oil town and gateway to Mulu National Park, has been diversifying its tourism appeal, and events such as WATCEFS contribute to positioning the city within the global culinary tourism circuit.

Participating chefs from countries including Japan, Spain, Italy, India, Singapore and Thailand competed across several categories, with Malaysian chefs performing strongly and winning recognition in the World Asia Tapas category. The competition results were enthusiastically received by the local Sarawak culinary community, which saw the event as validation of the state's growing gastronomic ambitions.

Organisers from Meritz Hotel and the Sarawak Tourism Board expressed satisfaction with the turnout and announced that discussions are already underway to host WATCEFS in Sarawak again in a future edition. The event's success aligns with Visit Malaysia 2026 campaign goals, demonstrating Malaysia's capacity to host world-class international events that attract global media attention and culinary tourism spending.
