Chery Launches TIGGO 9 SUV in Malaysia, Priced from RM166,800

Sin Chew Daily · June 2026 · Automotive

Chery Malaysia has officially launched the TIGGO 9, the brand's flagship premium SUV, in two package variants priced at RM166,800 and RM179,800 respectively. The launch marks another significant milestone in the rapid expansion of Chinese automotive brands in the Malaysian market, which has seen strong consumer appetite for feature-rich, value-for-money SUVs over the past three years.

The TIGGO 9 is positioned as a full-size seven-seater premium SUV, competing directly with established models from Korean, Japanese and European manufacturers in the RM150,000–RM200,000 segment. Chery has equipped the vehicle with a turbocharged petrol engine, a panoramic sunroof, Level 2 driver assistance systems, an expansive touchscreen infotainment system and premium leather interior as standard across both variants.

The higher-specification package adds enhancements including a 360-degree camera system, additional driver comfort features and upgraded audio. Both variants come with a comprehensive warranty covering the powertrain, body and electrical systems, addressing a historical concern among Malaysian buyers considering Chinese brands — the question of after-sales reliability and parts availability.

Chery's expansion in Malaysia has been rapid, with the brand establishing a growing dealer network across the country since its re-entry into the market. The company has invested in local service infrastructure, including certified technicians and a spare parts distribution network, to support the growing fleet of Chery vehicles on Malaysian roads.

The launch of the TIGGO 9 reflects broader market dynamics in Malaysia, where Chinese automotive brands — including Chery, BYD, GWM/Haval and others — are capturing an increasing share of the SUV and passenger car market. Analysts note that competitive pricing, advanced features and improving quality perceptions have combined to shift consumer consideration patterns, posing a genuine challenge to incumbent Korean and Japanese marques that previously dominated the mid-to-premium segment.
