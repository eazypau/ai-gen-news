Bursa Malaysia KLCI Sheds 11 Points as Blue Chips Face Selling Pressure

The Edge Malaysia · 26 June 2026 · Business

Bursa Malaysia's benchmark FBM KLCI index closed lower on Thursday, dropping 11.19 points or 0.65% to finish at 1,700.84. Selling pressure was concentrated in blue-chip counters, with profit-taking activity dragging the broader market alongside the index's key components.

Despite the overall market weakness, technology stocks managed to stand out, with several tech-linked counters appearing on the day's top gainers list. Investors continued to favour companies with exposure to artificial intelligence, data centre infrastructure and semiconductor supply chains — sectors where Malaysia has been actively positioning itself as a regional hub.

Market analysts attributed the day's decline to a combination of regional risk aversion and investors locking in gains ahead of the upcoming Johor state election on July 11. Political uncertainty, even at the state level, tends to temper appetite for domestic equities in the short term, though analysts noted the election is unlikely to materially affect the national economic outlook.

Trading volume on Bursa was slightly below the 30-day average, suggesting the sell-off was not driven by panic but rather cautious repositioning. The ringgit remained relatively stable against major currencies during the session, trading in a narrow band against the US dollar.

Looking ahead, market observers expect the KLCI to find support around the 1,690 level, with catalysts including continued foreign direct investment announcements and any positive surprises in upcoming corporate earnings. The index has traded in a broad range for most of the year, reflecting a balance between solid economic fundamentals and external headwinds from global trade uncertainty.
