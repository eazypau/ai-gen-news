MDEC Appoints Ganesh Kumar Bangah as New Chairman

Digital News Asia · June 2026 · Technology

The Malaysia Digital Economy Corporation (MDEC) has appointed seasoned technology entrepreneur and industry leader Ganesh Kumar Bangah as its new chairman. The appointment, announced this month, is seen as a significant move to strengthen Malaysia's digital economy governance at a time when the country is aggressively pursuing its AI Nation 2030 agenda.

Ganesh Kumar Bangah brings decades of experience in the Malaysian technology ecosystem, having co-founded and led multiple technology companies and industry bodies. He is widely recognised for his advocacy of homegrown technology talent and his work bridging the private sector with government digital transformation initiatives.

MDEC, which celebrates its 30th anniversary in 2026, has been the central agency driving Malaysia's transition to a knowledge-based and digital economy. Under the Malaysia Digital (MD) initiative, MDEC has been attracting hyperscaler investments, supporting local startup growth, and building the country's data centre and cloud infrastructure base.

The new chairman is expected to provide strategic direction as Malaysia competes with Singapore, Thailand and Vietnam for regional technology investment and talent. Key priorities will include accelerating AI adoption among local SMEs, expanding the pool of qualified tech professionals through upskilling programmes, and positioning Malaysia as a trusted hub for cross-border data flows within ASEAN.

The Ministry of Digital, which oversees MDEC, expressed confidence that the appointment would inject fresh momentum into Malaysia's digital ambitions. Industry observers noted that having a private-sector champion at the helm of MDEC could help translate government vision into commercially viable outcomes at scale.
