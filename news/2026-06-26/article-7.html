Malaysia Positions as ASEAN's Digital Infrastructure Powerhouse at DCCI Expo 2026

EIN Presswire · June 2026 · Technology

Malaysia has reinforced its standing as the leading digital infrastructure hub in Southeast Asia at the DCCI (Data Centre, Cloud and Connectivity Infrastructure) Expo 2026. The event, which brought together global technology investors, platform operators and government officials, featured Malaysia as a centrepiece of regional data centre and cloud computing development.

MDEC's participation in the Expo highlighted Malaysia's growing portfolio of hyperscale data centre projects, with major investments from global technology companies including Amazon Web Services, Microsoft, Google and several Chinese technology firms. Malaysia's competitive advantages — political stability, competitive land and power costs, and a growing STEM talent pipeline — have made it a preferred destination for cloud infrastructure build-out in the region.

A landmark development announced at the Expo was ASEAN's endorsement of a Regional Framework on Cross-Border Cloud Computing, conceived and led by Malaysia's Ministry of Digital through MDEC. The framework aims to enable trusted and seamless data flows across ASEAN member states, reducing friction for businesses operating across borders while establishing clear data sovereignty guidelines.

MDEC's CEO emphasised that Malaysia's rise as a regional digital infrastructure hub requires strong ecosystem coordination, with the government, private sector, technology providers and local industry enablers all playing critical roles. The DCCI Expo 2026 comes at a "pivotal moment," according to MDEC, as demand for data centre capacity across the region continues to surge with the proliferation of AI workloads.

Malaysia currently hosts over 60 operational data centres with significant pipeline capacity under construction. The government has set targets to make Malaysia a net exporter of digital services by 2030, with the data centre sector expected to contribute meaningfully to the country's services export revenue.
