Malaysia's Badminton Campaign at Macau Open Ends at Semi-Final Stage

Bernama · 20 June 2026 · Sports

Malaysia's campaign at the 2026 Macau Open badminton tournament concluded at the semi-final stage after all five national representatives were eliminated from their respective categories. The result, while disappointing for fans hoping for a final appearance, highlighted the intense competition on the international circuit as Malaysia continues to rebuild its badminton programme.

The Malaysian contingent showed promising performances in the earlier rounds, defeating higher-ranked opponents and generating optimism heading into the last four. However, the semi-finals proved a bridge too far, with national shuttlers unable to sustain the level required against top-class opposition from China, Indonesia and South Korea.

The Badminton Association of Malaysia (BAM) acknowledged the results and reiterated its commitment to developing depth across all categories. The national coaching team is expected to review the tournament performance as part of ongoing preparations for the 2026 Asian Games in Aichi-Nagoya, Japan, where Malaysian shuttlers will be targeting at least a bronze medal in the team event.

Attention will now turn to the upcoming BWF World Championships in New Delhi, where Malaysia's number one mixed doubles shuttler Toh Ee Wei — currently recovering from injury — is considering a title defence. BAM has indicated it will make a decision on Toh Ee Wei's participation once medical clearance is obtained.

Malaysia's badminton programme has faced a period of transition following the retirements of several established players in recent years. The national federation has invested heavily in youth development, with several Under-21 players showing promise in feeder circuit events. BAM officials remain optimistic that the talent pipeline will translate into more consistent results on the senior circuit from 2027 onwards.
