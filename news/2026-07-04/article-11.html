Lee Zii Jia Eyes Title at Canada Open After Five-Month Win Drought

Sports New Straits Times 4 July 2026

Malaysian badminton ace Lee Zii Jia has a golden opportunity to end a five-month title drought at the Canada Open, currently underway in Vancouver. The world-class shuttler, ranked among the top men's singles players in the world, has been in fine form during the opening rounds of the tournament, dispatching opponents with the fearless attacking play that has made him one of the most exciting players on the BWF circuit.

Zii Jia must first overcome a tough match against Jhuo Fu before a potential quarter-final showdown against either Po Wei or Denmark's rising talent Ditlev Jaeger Holm. Analysts believe a favourable draw has opened the door for the Malaysian to go deep in the tournament, and his camp has spoken of the importance of converting this opportunity into silverware.

The five-month drought since his last title win has drawn scrutiny, with some commentators questioning whether Zii Jia has been managing a fitness issue. However, his coaches have dismissed such speculation, pointing to his consistent performance in recent tournaments even when not lifting the trophy. They argue that Zii Jia has been close to the top of his game and that results will follow with continued tournament time.

For the Badminton Association of Malaysia (BAM), Zii Jia's fortunes at the Canada Open are closely watched as the season builds towards the BWF World Championships. A title at a Super 300 or above event would provide vital ranking points and confidence heading into what promises to be a packed second half of the badminton calendar.

Malaysian badminton fans are eagerly following the action, with live streaming available on Sooka and RTM channels. Zii Jia remains one of the country's most popular sportsmen and any deep run in the Canada Open is expected to generate significant public interest and media coverage back home.
