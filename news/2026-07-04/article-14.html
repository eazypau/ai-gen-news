Malaysia Gears Up for Glasgow Commonwealth Games with Strong Athletic Squad

Sports BERNAMA 4 July 2026

Malaysia is preparing to send a strong contingent to the Glasgow Commonwealth Games, scheduled to run from July 23 to August 2, 2026, in Scotland. Athletes across a wide range of disciplines are finalising their preparations in intensive training camps, with national sports associations submitting final squad lists ahead of the official opening ceremony.

Badminton, lawn bowls, squash, cycling and shooting are among the sports in which Malaysia has historically performed strongly at the Commonwealth Games, and national coaches are optimistic about medal prospects in those disciplines. The swimming and track and field programmes are also expected to send competitive athletes, reflecting the depth of investment in Malaysia's multi-sport athlete development pipeline.

Youth Sports Minister Datuk Hannah Yeoh has expressed confidence in the squad's readiness, noting that the 2026 Commonwealth Games provides an invaluable platform for Malaysian athletes to compete on an international stage and build experience ahead of the 2028 Olympics. She highlighted BERNAMA's "Sports Calendar 2026" report which positions the Games as one of the key events for elite athletes to shine and for juniors to build their reputations.

The Malaysian Olympic Council (MOC) and the National Sports Institute (NSI) have coordinated a comprehensive support programme for the Glasgow-bound athletes, including sports science support, physiotherapy, psychological coaching and performance analysis. Financial support from both the government and national federations has been mobilised to ensure the contingent is well-resourced.

Glasgow will welcome Commonwealth athletes for the second time, having previously hosted the Games in 2014. Malaysian sports fans are encouraged to follow the action via live broadcasts and streaming platforms, with RTM and national sports social media channels expected to provide comprehensive coverage throughout the duration of the Games.
