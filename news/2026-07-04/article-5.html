Foreign Funds Outflow Hits RM5.12 Billion from Malaysia's Equity Market

Business The Edge Malaysia 4 July 2026

Malaysia's equity market recorded a net outflow of RM5.12 billion in foreign funds during the second quarter of 2026, continuing a trend of cautious investor sentiment driven by global geopolitical uncertainty and risk-off behaviour among international portfolio managers. The Bursa Malaysia benchmark index remained relatively resilient in absolute terms but lagged regional peers during the period.

Market analysts attribute the outflows to a combination of factors including the strengthening US dollar, rising global risk aversion triggered by the West Asia conflict, and portfolio rebalancing by institutional investors ahead of the second half of the year. The technology-heavy sectors, which had attracted significant foreign inflows earlier in the year on the back of semiconductor optimism, saw the steepest reversals.

Despite the headline outflow figure, domestic institutional investors — including pension funds and unit trust companies — provided meaningful support, absorbing much of the selling pressure and preventing a sharper market correction. Retail participation on Bursa Malaysia also remained healthy, with individual investors continuing to show interest in high-dividend yield stocks and REITs.

Bank Negara Malaysia and the Securities Commission have jointly noted that Malaysia's capital markets remain well-functioning and that the outflows, while notable, are consistent with broader emerging market trends seen across Southeast Asia. Both regulators expressed confidence in the underlying fundamentals of the Malaysian economy and its attractiveness as an investment destination over the medium term.

Looking ahead, analysts expect foreign fund flows to stabilise in the third quarter as global uncertainty eases and Malaysia-specific growth catalysts — including semiconductor exports, Visit Malaysia 2026 receipts, and ongoing Johor SEZ investments — come into sharper focus for international investors.
