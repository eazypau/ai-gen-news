Malaysia Economy Projected to Grow 4-5% in 2026 Despite Global Headwinds

Business The Star / OECD 4 July 2026

Bank Negara Malaysia has reiterated its full-year economic growth projection of 4% to 5% for 2026, even as the global economic environment becomes more challenging. The central bank's Monetary Policy Committee cited Malaysia's resilient domestic demand, strong export performance in the electrical and electronics sector, and the tailwind from Visit Malaysia 2026 as key pillars of growth.

The OECD's latest Economic Outlook projects Malaysia's GDP to grow by 4.2% in 2026, placing it among the more robust performers within the Southeast Asian region. The projection reflects the OECD's assessment that Malaysia's economic fundamentals — including its diversified export base, relatively low inflation, manageable public debt levels and stable financial system — provide a solid buffer against external shocks.

Economists warn, however, that the more painful effects of global disruptions — including elevated energy costs linked to the West Asia conflict and weakening demand from key export markets — are only beginning to filter through the supply chain. If these headwinds intensify in the second half of 2026, growth could come in at the lower end of the projected range, necessitating a policy response from Bank Negara or the Ministry of Finance.

The construction and services sectors are expected to be the primary domestic growth drivers for the remainder of the year. Major infrastructure projects including the expanded Penang transportation network, the Johor-Singapore Rapid Transit System Link and several large-scale data centre developments are providing sustained demand for construction materials, engineering services and skilled labour.

Investment promotion authorities report that foreign direct investment interest in Malaysia remains healthy, particularly from advanced economy companies seeking to diversify their supply chains away from single-country dependence. Malaysia's location, connectivity and established manufacturing ecosystem continue to make it an attractive destination for investment in electronics, medical devices, renewable energy and digital infrastructure.
