Sabah Eyes Lahad Datu as Future Rocket and Spacecraft Launch Site

Technology The Cekodok 4 July 2026

The Sabah state government, in collaboration with the Ministry of Science, Technology and Innovation (MOSTI), has announced it is conducting a feasibility study to determine whether Lahad Datu could serve as a site for rocket and spacecraft launches in the future. The study aims to assess geographical, logistical and technical suitability, with early findings expected to be presented later this year.

Lahad Datu, located on the eastern coast of Sabah, offers several potential advantages as a launch site including its proximity to the equator, which reduces the fuel requirements needed to achieve low-earth orbit. The region also offers relatively sparse population density in adjacent areas, which is a key safety consideration for rocket launch facilities. However, challenges related to infrastructure, connectivity and security will need to be addressed.

The announcement comes alongside news that Sarawak is also planning to establish its own space agency, focused on coordination in aerospace planning, developing local capabilities, and positioning East Malaysia as a hub for the emerging space economy. The parallel initiatives from both East Malaysian states reflect growing regional ambitions to participate in the global commercial space industry.

Malaysia's space ambitions have been building momentum. The government's broader National Space Policy outlines goals to develop indigenous space technology capabilities and reduce dependence on foreign satellite and launch services. MOSTI has been engaging with international space agencies and commercial launch providers to identify partnership opportunities that could accelerate domestic capacity building.

Civil society and academic institutions in Sabah have welcomed the initiative, with universities expressing interest in contributing research support for the feasibility study. If the Lahad Datu site is deemed viable, the project could attract significant federal and private investment, creating high-skilled employment opportunities and positioning Sabah as a gateway for Malaysia's emerging space economy.
