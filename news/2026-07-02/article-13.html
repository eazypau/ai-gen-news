RTM's TV6 Officially Ceases Broadcasting from 1 July 2026

Lowyat.NET 2 July 2026 Technology

Television Tujuh (TV6), Radio Television Malaysia's retro-themed free-to-air channel, officially ceased broadcasting at midnight on 1 July 2026, ending a nostalgic run that brought classic Malaysian and international content back to air. The channel's final broadcast featured a curated selection of iconic shows and a farewell message from its presenters.

RTM announced the closure as part of a broader strategic review of its channel portfolio, with resources to be redirected towards strengthening its flagship channels TV1 and TV2, as well as its digital streaming platform RTM Klik. The national broadcaster said the decision reflects the shift in viewer habits toward on-demand content and the need to rationalise operating costs in a challenging advertising market.

TV6 had developed a loyal following since its relaunch as a retro channel, particularly among older Malaysians who enjoyed reruns of iconic dramas, game shows and variety programmes from the 1980s and 1990s. Social media was filled with nostalgic tributes from viewers expressing disappointment at the closure and calling for the archival content to be made available online.

RTM responded by pledging to digitise and upload a substantial portion of TV6's classic library to RTM Klik, making it accessible on-demand at no charge. The broadcaster also indicated it is exploring partnerships with streaming platforms to extend the reach of Malaysian archival content to diaspora audiences worldwide.

Media analysts noted that TV6's closure is symptomatic of the wider structural challenges facing free-to-air broadcasters in Malaysia, where advertising revenue has migrated significantly to digital platforms. RTM's management is expected to present a comprehensive five-year digital transformation roadmap to Parliament later this month.
