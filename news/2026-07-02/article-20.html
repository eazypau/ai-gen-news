Singer 2026 – Malaysia Unveils Six Debut Singers, Including Jaclyn Victor

World of Buzz 2 July 2026 Entertainment

Astro officially unveiled the six debut contestants for Singer 2026 – Malaysia, the local edition of the internationally acclaimed singing competition format. The revealed performers are veteran pop icon Jaclyn Victor, powerhouse vocalist Priscilla Abby, the soulful Vanessa Reynauld, R&B singer Geraldine Gan, baritone talent Uriah See and indie sensation Nicole Lai — a line-up that has generated considerable excitement across Malaysia's music community.

Singer 2026 – Malaysia marks a landmark moment in Malaysian entertainment, representing the country's first full-scale participation in the Singer competition format, which originated in China and has become one of the most watched music programmes in Asia. Astro's partnership with Hunan Satellite Television brings the production values and global reach of the original format to a Malaysian context, with the competition expected to draw record viewership on Astro's channels and streaming platform.

The first episode will feature a high-stakes elimination round involving nearly 30 singers competing for the two remaining spots in the Top 8, promising an electrifying opening night. Judges drawn from Malaysia's music industry elite will evaluate performances across a range of musical genres, with live audience voting adding an interactive element to the proceedings.

Jaclyn Victor, a beloved figure in Malaysian pop music best known for winning the Malaysian Idol competition in 2004 and her powerful vocal performances, said she was honoured to represent the country in such a prestigious format. "This is something I have always dreamed of — bringing Malaysian musical heritage to a wider audience," she told reporters at the launch event.

Industry observers expect Singer 2026 – Malaysia to spark a broader conversation about the health and diversity of Malaysia's music industry, particularly its capacity to produce artists with the range and stage presence to compete internationally. The programme is scheduled to air weekly on Astro from mid-July 2026, with simulcast arrangements for Chinese-speaking audiences across the region.
