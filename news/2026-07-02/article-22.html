New 10-Year Malaysian Passport Now Available for Application from 1 July

BERNAMA 2 July 2026 Lifestyle

The Malaysian government officially launched the new 10-year Malaysian Passport (Pasport Malaysia Antarabangsa, or PMA) for public applications from 1 July 2026, fulfilling a long-standing request from frequent travellers and the Malaysian diaspora. The new passport, available at all Immigration Department offices nationwide and through the MyImmigration online portal, offers a decade of validity compared to the standard five-year version, at a cost of RM200.

The 10-year PMA incorporates advanced biometric security features including a next-generation chip with encrypted fingerprint and facial recognition data, a polycarbonate data page resistant to tampering, and ultraviolet-reactive security printing patterns that are among the most sophisticated in the Asia Pacific region. The document meets the International Civil Aviation Organisation's (ICAO) latest document security standards, ensuring seamless acceptance at international border crossings.

Home Minister Datuk Seri Saifuddin Nasution Ismail said the launch was the result of extensive consultation with frequent travellers, business communities and Malaysian citizens living and working abroad. He noted that the longer validity period would reduce the administrative burden on Malaysians who travel extensively for work and would align Malaysia's passport validity standards with those of leading nations including the United States, United Kingdom and Japan.

Applicants can choose between the standard 5-year PMA at RM100 and the new 10-year PMA at RM200 — both prices remain unchanged from previous rates for the respective validity periods. Children under 12 years remain limited to a 5-year passport in line with international norms governing minors' travel documents. Applications can be submitted online with an appointment booking system designed to reduce waiting times at Immigration counters.

Immigration Department Director-General Datuk Ruslin Jusoh urged Malaysians planning to travel to apply early and check that their existing passports have sufficient validity for planned trips, as many countries require at least six months' remaining validity at the time of entry. He noted that the department has increased staffing at high-volume offices to manage the anticipated surge in PMA applications following the launch.
