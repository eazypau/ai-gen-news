SAYS Malaysia 2 July 2026 Sports

The expanded tournament format means more matches, more upsets and more drama. New rule changes introduced by FIFA for this edition include a five-second time limit for throw-ins, a mandatory shot-clock mechanism for goal kicks, and expanded VAR authority to review corner kick decisions.

Malaysian fans following the action are advised to check the full schedule converted to Malaysian Standard Time (UTC+8) as most matches in the Americas kick off between 3:00 AM and 9:00 AM Malaysian time. The tournament has triggered a surge in sports tourism interest, with a number of Malaysian fans having travelled to the United States and Canada to attend matches. Travel agencies reported that World Cup packages to North America were among their best-selling products of the first half of 2026, with the momentum expected to carry over into greater interest in future global sporting events hosted in the region.

With the round of 32 wrapping up and the round of 16 approaching, football debate is at fever pitch in Malaysia. Punters are closely watching the performances of Asia-Pacific representatives, with fans hoping for a strong showing from the ASEAN region's representatives as the knockout rounds intensify.
