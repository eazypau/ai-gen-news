Fake WhatsApp Account Impersonating PH Johor Candidate Condemned

Malay Mail 2 July 2026 Politics

Communications Minister Datuk Fahmi Fadzil strongly condemned the creation of a fake WhatsApp account impersonating Pakatan Harapan candidate Saiful Nizam Samat, who is contesting the Endau state seat in the upcoming Johor election. The fraudulent account was used to spread disinformation to voters, and the minister described the act as irresponsible and a threat to the integrity of the democratic process.

The fake account reportedly circulated misleading messages attributed to Saiful Nizam, some of which made false policy promises and misrepresented the candidate's positions on key issues. Supporters and constituents were urged to verify all communications through official party channels and to report suspicious accounts to the Malaysian Communications and Multimedia Commission (MCMC).

MCMC has since confirmed it is investigating the account and coordinating with Meta — WhatsApp's parent company — to identify and remove the fraudulent profile. Authorities warned that those found responsible could face charges under the Communications and Multimedia Act 1998 as well as the newly passed Cybercrimes Bill 2026.

This incident is not isolated: election observers have flagged a broader pattern of digital disinformation in the lead-up to the Johor polls, including deepfake videos and fabricated screenshots attributed to prominent politicians. The MCMC has deployed a dedicated election misinformation task force to monitor social media platforms during the campaign period.

Saiful Nizam himself urged supporters to remain calm and not be misled by the fake account, affirming that all his official communications will only be made through verified platforms. The incident has reignited calls for stronger pre-election cybersecurity protocols and social media literacy campaigns targeting first-time voters.
