Lee Zii Jia Called Up for Malaysia's BWF Thomas Cup Finals Team

Olympics.com 2 July 2026 Sports

Veteran Malaysian shuttler Lee Zii Jia has been recalled to the national squad for the BWF Thomas Cup Finals despite his current world ranking of No. 83 — a far cry from the top-ten position he held during his peak years. Badminton Association of Malaysia (BAM) selectors cited his vast big-match experience and proven ability to raise his game in high-pressure team championships as the key factors behind the selection decision.

The recall has ignited lively debate among Malaysian badminton supporters, with some hailing it as a pragmatic move to inject experience into the squad, while others argued that lower-ranked but in-form younger players deserved the opportunity. BAM Chief Executive Officer Norman Abdul Halim defended the selection, stating that team dynamics and mental fortitude under pressure are qualities that rankings alone do not capture.

Lee Zii Jia himself expressed gratitude for the recall and acknowledged that his recent form on the individual circuit has not been at the level he expects of himself. He pledged to contribute fully to the team effort and said competing for Malaysia in a team championship motivates him differently from individual tournaments. "The Thomas Cup is special — it brings out the best in all of us," he said at a press conference in Bukit Jalil.

Malaysia is targeting a strong performance at the Thomas Cup Finals as part of a broader resurgence of the national badminton programme following a strategic review by BAM. The federation has invested in new coaching appointments, expanded training facilities and a revamped high-performance unit to develop the next generation of world-class shuttlers.

The Thomas Cup Finals will pit Malaysia against perennial contenders China, Indonesia and Japan, with the host nation hoping to recapture the title it last won in 2016. The tournament is widely regarded as the ultimate test of team badminton, and Malaysia's inclusion of Lee Zii Jia signals a win-now mentality from the selectors ahead of what promises to be an intensely competitive event.
