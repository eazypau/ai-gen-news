Malaysia Football Team to Face Myanmar and Thailand in ASEAN Cup Late July

New Straits Times 2 July 2026 Sports

The Malaysian national football team has a packed fixture schedule ahead as the ASEAN Mitsubishi Electric Cup group stage approaches. The Harimau Malaya are scheduled to face Myanmar on 25 July, Laos on 28 July, and rivals Thailand on 1 August, with the results set to determine their path to the knockout rounds of the biennial ASEAN championship.

Head coach Datuk Pau Marti has been putting the squad through intensive pre-tournament training at the National Football Training Centre in Kajang, focusing on set-piece organisation, high press defending and transition play. The coach, who joined the national setup in late 2025, has introduced a more structured 4-3-3 formation that the squad has been adapting to in recent friendly matches.

Malaysia's campaign will be closely watched given the team's underperformance at the last edition of the tournament, where they exited in the group stage amid controversy over player availability and selection disputes. The Football Association of Malaysia (FAM) has pledged this cycle will be different, with improved coordination between club and national team schedules and clearer player release obligations.

The match against Thailand on 1 August is expected to be the most pivotal fixture in the group stage — the two sides have a fierce football rivalry, and a victory over the War Elephants would dramatically boost Malaysia's chances of progressing. Tickets for the home legs of the tournament have been selling briskly, with stadiums expected to be at or near capacity.

Supporters and pundits are optimistic that the new coaching setup and a crop of talented young players emerging from the Liga Super can deliver a deeper tournament run. Several players who impressed in the Malaysia Super League season have been given trial opportunities in the national squad, raising hopes that a new generation of Harimau Malaya stars is ready to emerge.
