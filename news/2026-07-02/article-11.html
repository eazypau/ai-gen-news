Fuel Prices Drop: RON97 at RM4.00, RON95 Unsubsidised at RM3.37

Sin Chew Daily 2 July 2026 Economy

The Ministry of Finance announced the latest weekly fuel price adjustments effective 2 July 2026, with all key fuel grades seeing a reduction. RON97 petrol fell from RM4.10 to RM4.00 per litre, while the unsubsidised RON95 price dropped from RM3.47 to RM3.37 per litre. Unsubsidised diesel prices also decreased from RM4.07 to RM3.97 per litre.

The reductions reflect a softening in global crude oil prices, with Brent crude dipping below USD 78 per barrel mid-week amid concerns about weaker-than-expected demand from China and expectations of increased OPEC+ output. Malaysia's automatic fuel pricing mechanism, which pegs domestic retail prices to a rolling global crude benchmark, transmitted the international decline to local pump prices.

Subsidised RON95, which remains available to Malaysian citizens meeting income-based eligibility criteria under the targeted subsidy framework, is maintained at its fixed controlled price. The government's ongoing fuel subsidy rationalisation programme — designed to redirect savings towards social protection and infrastructure — continues to be phased in, with the next eligibility review scheduled for the third quarter of 2026.

Consumer advocacy groups welcomed the price drop as timely relief ahead of the school holidays and Johor election season, which typically sees increased road travel. Transport operators, who saw freight costs ease slightly, called on the government to maintain the transparency of the weekly pricing mechanism so that businesses can plan logistics costs with greater predictability.

Petrol station operators noted that the RM4.00 RON97 price point is psychologically significant, as it is the first time the premium grade has fallen below this threshold in several months. Energy analysts expect prices to remain range-bound in the near term, barring a fresh escalation of geopolitical tensions in West Asia.
