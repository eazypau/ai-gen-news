Johor Election Set for 11 July: 172 Candidates, 56 Seats

Malay Mail 2 July 2026 Politics

The Election Commission (EC) has officially confirmed that 172 candidates will contest all 56 seats in the 16th Johor State Legislative Assembly election, scheduled for 11 July 2026. Early voting is set for 7 July, with state residents urged to check their polling stations and ensure their electoral rolls are up to date ahead of nomination day.

Barisan Nasional (BN) and Pakatan Harapan (PH) are each fielding 56 candidates to cover every constituency, followed by Perikatan Nasional (PN) with 33 seats, Parti Bersama Malaysia with 15, the Malaysian United Democratic Alliance (MUDA) with four, and both Parti Orang Asli Malaysia and Parti Sosialis Malaysia (PSM) with one candidate each. Six independent candidates round out the field.

The contest configuration sees 14 straight fights between two parties, 27 three-cornered contests, 12 four-way battles and three constituencies drawing five candidates each. To secure a simple majority and form the next Johor state government, any party or coalition must win at least 29 of the 56 seats.

The incumbent Barisan Nasional government, led by Menteri Besar Onn Hafiz Ghazi, is defending its position after sweeping the 2022 Johor polls. The ruling coalition enters the campaign as the favourite, but faces a reinvigorated PH challenge bolstered by the federal unity government's track record — even as the two parties compete against each other at the state level.

Observers noted that first-time candidates make up a smaller share of aspirants in this cycle compared to previous elections, suggesting both major coalitions are fielding more experienced hands in a high-stakes contest seen as a bellwether for the 16th general election.
