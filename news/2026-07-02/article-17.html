Malaysia Gears Up for Glasgow Commonwealth Games Starting 23 July

BERNAMA 2 July 2026 Sports

Malaysia is finalising preparations to send a multi-sport contingent to the 2026 Commonwealth Games in Glasgow, Scotland, scheduled from 23 July to 2 August. The Olympic Council of Malaysia (OCM) has confirmed that athletes will compete across 20 sports disciplines, making it one of Malaysia's most comprehensive Commonwealth Games campaigns in recent memory.

OCM President Tan Sri Norza Zakaria said the Glasgow Games represent a significant test of Malaysia's true sporting calibre on the global stage, coming just two years ahead of the 2028 Los Angeles Olympics. He expressed confidence that the national contingent, bolstered by new high-performance coaching appointments and training investment, would deliver a medal haul exceeding Malaysia's Birmingham 2022 Commonwealth Games tally.

Badminton, lawn bowls, squash, cycling and athletics are expected to be Malaysia's strongest medal-contending sports in Glasgow. The national badminton team — preparing simultaneously for the Thomas Cup Finals — faces a demanding schedule, while squash's Nicol David Foundation has produced a new generation of racket talent targeting podium finishes.

Team Malaysia's preparation camp has been based at the National Sports Institute (NSI) in Bukit Jalil, with athletes undergoing intensive physiological testing and altitude acclimatisation protocols. Sports Minister Hannah Yeoh said the government had allocated RM35 million for Games preparation, including athlete welfare, specialised coaching and travel logistics.

The OCM also confirmed that Malaysia would observe the Commonwealth Charter's human rights commitments at the Games and welcomed Glasgow's commitment to hosting an inclusive and accessible event. With the Games now less than three weeks away, public excitement is building and corporate sponsors have begun activation campaigns around key Malaysian medal contenders.
