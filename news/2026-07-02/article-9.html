MADANI Government Disburses RM15 Billion in MSME Financing Support

Free Malaysia Today 2 July 2026 Economy

The MADANI Government announced that it has channelled RM15 billion in financing support for micro, small and medium enterprises (MSMEs) as of May 2026, comprising a mix of microfinancing, business loans and government-backed guarantees. The figure represents a significant scaling-up of SME support compared to the previous administration and reflects the government's commitment to inclusive economic growth.

The financing was distributed through a network of development financial institutions including Bank Simpanan Nasional, Agrobank, SME Bank, and Credit Guarantee Corporation Malaysia, supplemented by a dedicated MADANI Entrepreneur programme that fast-tracked applications from micro-enterprises with fewer than five employees.

Minister of Entrepreneur Development and Cooperatives Ewon Benedick said that the RM15 billion figure includes RM3.2 billion disbursed to bumiputera-owned businesses under targeted equity participation programmes, and that women entrepreneurs received RM1.8 billion in preferential financing. He noted that approval rates for first-time MSME borrowers had improved significantly following the streamlining of credit assessment procedures.

Small business owners and industry associations broadly welcomed the announcement, though some noted that access to financing remains challenging for sole proprietors in the informal economy who lack formal financial records or collateral. The government indicated it is working on alternative credit scoring models that incorporate digital transaction data to address this gap.

Economists said the MSME financing push is a key pillar of Malaysia's strategy to broaden the economic base beyond large corporations and foreign direct investment. With MSMEs accounting for 38.4% of GDP and 48.4% of total employment, sustained access to credit is seen as critical to maintaining the momentum of Malaysia's economic expansion.
