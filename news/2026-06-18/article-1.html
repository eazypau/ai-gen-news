Diesel Price Drops 30 Sen to RM4.37 This Week

paultan.org 18 June 2026 Economy

Peninsular Malaysia's diesel retail price has fallen by 30 sen to RM4.37 per litre for the week of June 18 to 24, 2026, according to the weekly fuel price announcement. The more premium Euro 5 B7 blend is priced at RM4.57 per litre. Meanwhile, diesel prices in Sabah, Sarawak and Labuan remain unchanged at the subsidised rate of RM2.15 per litre.

The RON95 unsubsidised petrol price holds steady at RM3.72 per litre, while the subsidised BUDI95 rate for eligible recipients remains at RM1.99 per litre. RON97 petrol also stays unchanged at RM4.35 per litre. The fuel price adjustments follow the government's weekly floating pricing mechanism, which tracks global crude oil benchmarks.

The decrease in diesel prices is expected to provide some relief to the logistics, agriculture and transportation sectors, which are among the heaviest consumers of diesel in Malaysia. Industry groups have welcomed the reduction, noting that lower fuel costs could ease supply chain pressures that have persisted since early 2026.

The government's targeted fuel subsidy programme, known as BUDI95, continues to support lower-income vehicle owners by maintaining the subsidised petrol rate far below market prices. The programme uses MyKad data and vehicle ownership records to identify eligible Malaysians, ensuring the subsidy reaches those who need it most.

Analysts expect global crude oil prices to remain volatile in the near term due to ongoing geopolitical tensions in the Middle East, which could lead to further fluctuations in Malaysia's weekly fuel pricing in the coming weeks.
