Malaysia Attracts RM92.8 Billion in Approved Investments in Q1 2026

MIDA — Malaysian Investment Development Authority 18 June 2026 Business

Malaysia attracted RM92.8 billion in approved investments during the first quarter of 2026, according to data released by the Malaysian Investment Development Authority (MIDA). The figure is expected to generate over 50,000 new jobs across various sectors, reflecting the country's growing attractiveness as a destination for high-quality investment.

Domestic investments rose by 13% year-on-year, signalling growing confidence among local businesses in Malaysia's economic outlook. Foreign direct investment also remained strong, with Japanese investments particularly notable — 93.6% of Japanese approved investments in Q1 2026 were channelled into digital transformation activities, highlighting the deepening technology collaboration between the two nations.

The manufacturing sector continued to attract the lion's share of investment, with the electrical and electronics (E&E), chemical and chemical products, and basic metal products industries leading the way. Services and primary sectors also recorded healthy investment inflows, underscoring the breadth of Malaysia's economic appeal.

MIDA chief executive officer Datuk Wira Arham Abdul Rahman noted that the strong investment performance reflects Malaysia's competitive advantages, including its skilled workforce, strategic location, well-developed infrastructure and stable policy environment. He added that the government's National Investment Aspirations framework has played a key role in attracting high-quality, high-value investments.

The strong Q1 performance puts Malaysia on track to meet or exceed its full-year investment targets. The government has set an ambitious goal of attracting RM330 billion in total investments for 2026, buoyed by ongoing major projects in the semiconductor, data centre and renewable energy sectors.
