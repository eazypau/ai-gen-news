Visit Malaysia 2026: Malaysia Mega Sale Runs Until July 31

Tourism Malaysia / Malaysia.travel 18 June 2026 Tourism

The Malaysia Mega Sale, one of the country's biggest annual retail and tourism events, is now well underway as part of the Visit Malaysia 2026 campaign, running from June 15 to July 31 with extensive promotions, offers and activities across malls, retail districts, hotels and entertainment venues nationwide. The campaign aims to drive both domestic and international visitor spending during the mid-year period, leveraging Malaysia's reputation as a premier shopping and lifestyle destination in Southeast Asia.

Visit Malaysia 2026 has been a landmark campaign for the country's tourism industry. Tourism authorities report that Malaysia is on track to welcome over two million Indian tourists this year, driven by improved air connectivity, simplified visa processes and Malaysia's strong appeal as a culturally rich, affordable and food-diverse destination for South Asian travellers. China also remained a key source market, with 4.7 million Chinese visitor arrivals recorded in 2025.

The campaign promotes a diverse range of Malaysian experiences, from luxury eco-resorts and rainforest retreats to heritage stays, culinary trails and urban cultural journeys. Tourism Malaysia has worked with state tourism boards and private sector partners to develop themed itineraries across all 13 states and three federal territories, ensuring that tourism spending flows beyond the major urban centres.

Domestic tourism has also performed strongly, with total domestic visitor expenditure rising 13.6% to RM121.3 billion in 2025. The Malaysia Mega Sale serves as an additional catalyst for domestic travel, encouraging Malaysians to explore their own country's diverse destinations and experiences during the school holiday period.

Malaysia's tourism recovery has been broad-based, covering beach destinations in Langkawi, Tioman and the East Coast, cultural heritage sites in Penang and Malacca, ecotourism attractions in Sabah and Sarawak, and the vibrant food and lifestyle scenes in Kuala Lumpur and other major cities. Tourism Malaysia is also targeting Gulf Cooperation Council (GCC) travellers, who have shown growing interest in halal-friendly travel experiences in Malaysia.
