Malaysia T20 Cricket League to Debut in September 2026

Flash Sports & Media / Stock Titan 18 June 2026 Sports

Flash Sports & Media (Nasdaq: FLZH) has announced the launch of the Malaysia T20 League (MT20), a new professional franchise cricket league set to make its debut in September and October 2026. The league is being developed through the company's subsidiary, Innovative Production Group FZ LLC, in direct partnership with the Malaysian Cricket Association (MCA), marking a significant milestone for the development of professional cricket in Malaysia.

The MT20 is designed to establish Malaysia as a premier destination for international T20 cricket, sports entertainment and cricket-related investment in Southeast Asia. The league format will feature multiple city-based franchise teams competing in a round-robin and knockout format, with a mix of Malaysian talent and international marquee players expected to attract broad fan interest both domestically and across the region.

Cricket has a long history in Malaysia, introduced during the British colonial era, and the sport retains a passionate following — particularly among the Indian and expatriate communities — as well as growing interest among Malay and Chinese Malaysians. The MT20 organisers are betting that the high-octane T20 format, combined with a strong entertainment package, can tap into Malaysia's young and digitally engaged sports fan base.

Flash Sports & Media has also announced a digital strategy for the league, with plans for live streaming, fantasy cricket platforms and social media-first content production to reach Malaysian fans and the wider South Asian diaspora in Southeast Asia. Talks are reportedly ongoing with broadcasters for television rights in Malaysia and internationally.

The league is part of a broader wave of cricket commercialisation across Asia, following the runaway success of the Indian Premier League (IPL) model and the growth of T20 leagues in the UAE, South Africa, Australia and the Caribbean. If successful, the MT20 could elevate Malaysia's profile on the international cricket circuit and provide a much-needed boost to grassroots cricket development in the country.
