Malaysia Expands Digital Health System to 16 Hospitals and 2,489 Clinics

Healthcare IT News 18 June 2026 Health

Malaysia's Ministry of Health is accelerating its digital transformation agenda, expanding the Total Hospital Information System (THIS) to 16 public hospitals as part of a sweeping modernisation programme. Health Minister Dr Dzulkefly Ahmad has declared 2026 as the landmark year for implementing health system reforms, with digitalisation at the centre of efforts to improve patient outcomes, reduce administrative burdens and strengthen healthcare delivery across the country.

Beyond hospitals, the ministry is also deploying a cloud-based Clinic Management System to approximately 2,489 primary care settings nationwide, including government health clinics and community health centres. A Dental Information System is simultaneously being rolled out to 157 dental clinics, digitising patient records and improving dental care coordination across the public health network.

The digital health push is part of Malaysia's broader Healthcare Digital Transformation Roadmap, which aims to create an integrated national health information ecosystem by 2030. Key objectives include enabling seamless patient record sharing between hospitals and clinics, reducing duplicate testing and investigations, and supporting population health management through data analytics and artificial intelligence.

Malaysia and the World Organisation for Animal Health (WOAH) have also pledged closer cooperation on a One Health approach, recognising the interconnection between human, animal and environmental health. The collaboration includes stronger coordination on zoonotic disease surveillance, rabies eradication, food safety and biosecurity — areas where digital data sharing and early warning systems can play a critical role.

Healthcare professionals and patient advocacy groups have broadly welcomed the digitalisation push but emphasised the need for robust data privacy protections, staff training and system reliability to ensure the transition delivers real benefits at the point of care. The ministry has committed to comprehensive training programmes for healthcare workers and has engaged independent cybersecurity experts to audit the new systems before full deployment.
