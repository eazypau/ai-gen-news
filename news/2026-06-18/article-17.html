OCBC Cycle Kuala Lumpur 2026 Set for June 28

EverythingBoleh / Event Organisers 18 June 2026 Sports

OCBC Cycle Kuala Lumpur 2026, Kuala Lumpur's flagship annual cycling event, is set to take place on June 28, promising a day of competitive racing and mass participation cycling through the heart of the Malaysian capital. The event, which has grown significantly since its inaugural edition, is expected to draw thousands of cyclists of all skill levels, from competitive racers to family groups participating in leisure rides.

The route will take participants through some of Kuala Lumpur's most iconic landmarks and urban landscapes, offering both a challenging experience for competitive cyclists and a memorable and accessible ride for recreational participants. Multiple categories will be offered, including elite races, sportive distances and a family-friendly route, ensuring the event caters to a wide audience across ages and abilities.

OCBC Cycle KL is part of a broader push to promote cycling culture, active lifestyles and sports tourism in Malaysia's capital. Organisers have worked closely with Kuala Lumpur City Hall (DBKL) and traffic authorities to ensure safe route closures and smooth logistics on the day of the event. Community engagement initiatives ahead of the race include group training rides, cycling safety workshops and school outreach programmes.

The event also carries an environmental message, with organisers implementing a zero-waste policy and partnering with local sustainability organisations to offset the event's carbon footprint. Participants are encouraged to arrive by public transport, and cycling-to-the-start initiatives are being promoted to reduce vehicle congestion in the city centre on event day.

With the FIFA World Cup 2026 currently under way in North America, sports fever is running high across Malaysia, and organisers of OCBC Cycle KL are hoping to capitalise on the elevated sports enthusiasm to attract record participation numbers. Registration for the event remains open until June 25, and cyclists are encouraged to sign up early to secure their preferred category and route.
