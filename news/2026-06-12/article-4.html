Unifi TV to Broadcast All 104 FIFA World Cup 2026 Matches Live in Malaysia

Unifi TV has secured the exclusive pay-TV broadcasting rights to all 104 matches of the 2026 FIFA World Cup in Malaysia, the company announced this week. Subscribers can access the full tournament through a flexible daily pass priced at RM20, available from June 12 onwards. The platform will provide multi-channel coverage with simultaneous streams and on-demand replay options for fans who miss live broadcasts due to the time difference.

Alongside Unifi TV's paid offering, the Malaysian government has ensured free-to-air access for all Malaysians through RTM (Radio Television Malaysia). RTM's channels — TV1, TV2, TV Okey, RTM Sports, and Berita RTM — will carry live matches, while the RTMKlik app provides free streaming on smartphones, tablets, and Android TV devices. Users need only register with their MyDigital ID to unlock full access to the RTM World Cup streams at no cost.

The 2026 World Cup's expanded format featuring 48 teams creates a significantly larger schedule than previous editions, with group stage matches running concurrently across venues in the United States, Canada, and Mexico. Unifi TV is broadcasting matches in 4K Ultra HD for subscribers with compatible equipment, while RTMKlik offers standard and high-definition streaming depending on connection speed.

The announcement was welcomed by Malaysian football fans, many of whom had been concerned about access following rights negotiations that ran until earlier this year. The Communications Ministry confirmed that its policy priority was ensuring universal public access to the tournament, which it views as a major national sporting and cultural moment. The ministry also reminded fans that unauthorised streaming or rebroadcasting of World Cup matches is subject to copyright enforcement action.

For diaspora Malaysians abroad, Unifi TV's streaming platform is available internationally subject to geo-licensing restrictions, while RTMKlik may also be accessible via VPN. The government urged fans to use only authorised platforms to ensure they enjoy the best viewing experience and support the broadcast rights framework that makes future major sports events accessible in Malaysia.
