PM Anwar Reaffirms Unity Government Stability and Coalition Commitment

Prime Minister Datuk Seri Anwar Ibrahim on Thursday reaffirmed the Unity Government's commitment to its coalition partners, categorically stating that the administration would not take any action that could be perceived as a betrayal of the existing political consensus underpinning the government. Speaking at a press conference, Anwar described the coalition as a "mature alliance" capable of navigating differing perspectives while remaining united on national priorities.

The reassurance came amid renewed political noise from opposition quarters, who have been attempting to sow discord within the Unity Government's coalition of Pakatan Harapan, Barisan Nasional, Gabungan Parti Sarawak, and Gabungan Rakyat Sabah. Anwar dismissed claims of internal tension as opposition tactics designed to destabilise a government that he said had delivered three years of stable, reform-oriented governance.

On the economic front, Anwar pledged that the government would continue refining its targeted assistance programmes to help Malaysians cope with rising living costs. He highlighted the Budi Madani programme and the BUDI95 targeted fuel subsidy as key mechanisms through which the government is directing support to those who need it most, while at the same time pursuing fiscal prudence. The Prime Minister said the government remained committed to keeping the fiscal deficit on a downward trajectory toward the medium-term target.

The Prime Minister also addressed the cost-of-living issue, which continues to rank as the top concern for Malaysian households according to recent surveys. He acknowledged that inflationary pressures, particularly for food and utilities, remain a challenge despite the government's interventions, and said further measures were under review including enhanced cash transfer programmes under the Sumbangan Tunai Rahmah (STR) scheme.

Political analysts say Anwar's public show of coalition unity ahead of potential state elections in several states — including speculated polls in Negri Sembilan — is strategically significant. Maintaining a united front across the diverse coalition is seen as essential to the government's ability to contest and retain state governments, which in turn strengthens the federal administration's mandate.
